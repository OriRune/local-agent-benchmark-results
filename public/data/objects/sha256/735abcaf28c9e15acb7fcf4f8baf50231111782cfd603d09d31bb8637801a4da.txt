PARAGRAPH 1. THE SENTENCE MAKES A STRONG MORAL CLAIM, BUT “BAD” IS VAGUE: A LAW MIGHT BE UNFAIR, INEFFECTIVE, OR MERELY DISAGREEABLE. WITHOUT CLARIFYING WHAT MAKES A LAW BAD, THE ADVICE COULD ENCOURAGE PEOPLE TO TREAT PERSONAL OPINION AS A SUBSTITUTE FOR PRINCIPLED JUDGMENT.

PARAGRAPH 2. IT ALSO OVERLOOKS THE POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCES OF DISOBEYING A LAW, INCLUDING LEGAL PENALTIES AND HARM TO OTHERS. A MORE NUANCED CLAIM WOULD DISTINGUISH BETWEEN ORDINARY DISAGREEMENT AND SERIOUS INJUSTICE, AND WOULD CONSIDER WHETHER CHALLENGING THE LAW THROUGH PROTEST, COURTS, OR CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE IS JUSTIFIED.
