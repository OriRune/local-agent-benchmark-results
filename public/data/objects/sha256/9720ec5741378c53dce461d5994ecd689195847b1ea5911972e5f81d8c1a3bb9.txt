<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The sky turns a bruised and heavy gray,
As clouds gather close to pray.
The creek begins to swell and roar,
Ignoring every warning door.

The rain falls down in sheets of steel,
A relentless, cold, and wet appeal.
The roads disappear beneath the tide,
With nowhere left for us to hide.

The water climbs the wooden stairs,
A flood that brings no comfort, no cares.
It is a FORCE that cannot be denied,
With nowhere for the current to hide.

The town stands still, the pumps run FAST,
Watching the rising waters last.
Donnell waits for sun and air,
To wash away the flood's despair.
