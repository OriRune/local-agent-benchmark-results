## Setting Up the Problem

I need to find the probability that the **sum** of all three dice (1d12, 1d10, 1d4) is 11 or higher.

**Total outcomes:**
$$12 \times 10 \times 4 = 480$$

## Counting Outcomes Where Sum ≤ 10

It's easier to count the complement (sum ≤ 10) and subtract.

Since $a + b + c \le 10$ with each die ≥ 1, the constraints $a \le 12$ and $b \le 10$ are automatically satisfied. Only $c \le 4$ matters.

For each value of $c$, I count pairs $(a,b)$ with $a,b \ge 1$ and $a + b \le 10 - c$:

$$\text{Number of } (a,b) \text{ with } a+b \le m = \frac{(m-1)m}{2}$$

| $c$ | $m = 10-c$ | Count |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 9 | $\frac{8 \cdot 9}{2} = 36$ |
| 2 | 8 | $\frac{7 \cdot 8}{2} = 28$ |
| 3 | 7 | $\frac{6 \cdot 7}{2} = 21$ |
| 4 | 6 | $\frac{5 \cdot 6}{2} = 15$ |

**Total with sum ≤ 10:**
$$36 + 28 + 21 + 15 = 100$$

## Computing the Probability

**Outcomes with sum ≥ 11:**
$$480 - 100 = 380$$

**Probability:**
$$\frac{380}{480} = \frac{19}{24}$$

<answer>19/24</answer>
