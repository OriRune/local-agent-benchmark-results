Rewrite the following sentence in a style that is unusual: "But when the people of the land came to know that the Philistines had fled, they departed from Saul and went after David."

Ah! Lo! The very earth’s breath caught wind of the Philistines’ vanishing act—like smoke swallowed by a sigh—and the multitudes, those dust-kissed pilgrims of allegiance, shed Saul’s shadow as one might discard a worn cloak, then surged toward David like a tide drawn by a forgotten moon.
