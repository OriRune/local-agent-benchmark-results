Found the retry logic in `app/net.py`. Let me trace it.

The `fetch` function loops over `range(MAX_RETRIES)`, where `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`). So `attempt` takes the values `0, 1, 2`. On each failed attempt it calls `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)`:

| attempt | sleep (`2 ** attempt`) |
|---------|------------------------|
| 0       | `2**0` = 1 s           |
| 1       | `2**1` = 2 s           |
| 2       | `2**2` = 4 s           |

After the third failure the loop ends and it raises `RuntimeError`, so there's no extra sleep on exit.

**Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it gives up.
