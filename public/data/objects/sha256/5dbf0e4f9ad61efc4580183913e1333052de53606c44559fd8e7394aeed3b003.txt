*The morning began with a sneeze so loud it startled the toaster, and the toaster, in its infinite wisdom, decided to toast the bread a little darker in protest.*

Gerald stood in the kitchen, nose red, eyes watery, and a tissue tucked into his shirt like a tiny white flag of surrender. He had caught a cold, and now he was trying to figure out how to get rid of it. He could not throw it. He could not toss it into the trash. He could not even throw it at the cat, because the cat had already judged him and left the room.

*By noon, Gerald had learned the hard way that a cold is the one thing you can catch but not throw, and it follows you around like a very rude houseguest who refuses to leave.*
