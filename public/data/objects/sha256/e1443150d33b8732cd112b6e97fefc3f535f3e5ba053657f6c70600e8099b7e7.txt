From `app/net.py`:

```python
def fetch(url, get):
    for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):
        try:
            return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
        except OSError:
            time.sleep(2 ** attempt)
    raise RuntimeError(f"giving up on {url}")
```

With `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), if every attempt raises `OSError`, the sleeps are:

- attempt 0: `2**0` = 1 s
- attempt 1: `2**1` = 2 s
- attempt 2: `2**2` = 4 s

Total: **7 seconds** of sleeping before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
