## Setting Up the Problem

I'll interpret "rolling 10 or higher" as the **sum** of all three dice being ≥ 10.

**Total outcomes:** $12 \times 10 \times 2 = 240$

Let me count the **complement** (sum ≤ 9), where $a \in \{1,\dots,12\}$, $b \in \{1,\dots,10\}$, $c \in \{1,2\}$.

## Counting the Complement (sum ≤ 9)

**Case c = 1:** need $a + b \leq 8$

| a | valid b values | count |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–7 | 7 |
| 2 | 1–6 | 6 |
| 3 | 1–5 | 5 |
| 4 | 1–4 | 4 |
| 5 | 1–3 | 3 |
| 6 | 1–2 | 2 |
| 7 | 1 | 1 |

Total: $7+6+5+4+3+2+1 = 28$

**Case c = 2:** need $a + b \leq 7$

| a | valid b values | count |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–6 | 6 |
| 2 | 1–5 | 5 |
| 3 | 1–4 | 4 |
| 4 | 1–3 | 3 |
| 5 | 1–2 | 2 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 |

Total: $6+5+4+3+2+1 = 21$

**Complement total:** $28 + 21 = 49$

## Final Probability

$$P(\text{sum} \geq 10) = \frac{240 - 49}{240} = \frac{191}{240}$$

Since 191 is prime and doesn't divide 240, this is already reduced.

<answer>191/240</answer>
