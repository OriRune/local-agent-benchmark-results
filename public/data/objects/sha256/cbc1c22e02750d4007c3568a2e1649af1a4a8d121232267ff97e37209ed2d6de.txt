**Suggested Name: "Melb Teen"**

**Reasoning:**
1. **Local Identity**: "Melb" is the universally recognized abbreviation for Melbourne, immediately establishing the paper's geographic focus.
2. **Target Audience**: "Teen" clearly signals that the content is designed for teenagers.
3. **Modern Appeal**: The name is short, punchy, and contemporary—similar to how "Herald Sun" combines a traditional name with a modern, accessible feel.
4. **Memorability**: It's easy to remember and pronounce, which is essential for a publication targeting young readers.
5. **Brand Potential**: The name lends itself well to digital platforms, social media handles, and merchandise.

**P.S.** If you'd like alternative suggestions, consider "The Yarra" (referencing the Yarra River), "Melb Youth," or "Sunrise Teens" (playing on Melbourne's nickname as the "City of the Sun").
