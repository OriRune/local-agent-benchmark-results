<<The Flood in Donnell, TX>>

Heavy clouds gather, a low‑hummed sigh,  
The river swells, a restless tide.  
In Donnell’s streets the streets run deep,  
Where once the oak trees whispered sleep.  

WATER rushes past the cracked old bridge,  
RISING like a beast that cannot be tamed.  
SIREN wails through the night’s cold veil,  
And homes stand trembling, doors ajar.  

Neighbors gather, lanterns glow,  
Hands outstretched, hearts in tow.  
Hope is a lantern in the storm,  
A promise that the sun will rise again.  

Through mud‑slick lanes and shattered glass,  
We hear the town’s resilient chant—  
“Together we stand, we rebuild, we rise.”
