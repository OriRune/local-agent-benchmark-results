Total outcomes: \(12 \times 10 \times 2 = 240\).

Count the complement, sums \(\le 9\):

- If the d2 is 1, need d12 + d10 \(\le 8\): \(28\) outcomes.
- If the d2 is 2, need d12 + d10 \(\le 7\): \(21\) outcomes.

So sums \(\le 9\): \(28 + 21 = 49\).

Thus sums \(\ge 10\): \(240 - 49 = 191\).

\[
\frac{191}{240}
