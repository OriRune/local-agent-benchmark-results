The solution converges to a total delta-v of exactly 9,400 m/s.

**Solution:**

Let X = mass at start of stage 2 burn = (stage 2 dry + stage 2 propellant + payload).

**Stage 1:**
- m₀ = 4,000 + 36,000 + X = 40,000 + X
- m_f = 4,000 + X
- Δv₁ = 290 × 9.80665 × ln((40,000 + X)/(4,000 + X))

**Stage 2:**
- m₀ = X, m_f = 900 + 1,200 = 2,100
- Δv₂ = 340 × 9.80665 × ln(X/2,100)

Solving Δv₁ + Δv₂ = 9,400 gives X ≈ 13,629.43 kg, so stage 2 propellant = X − 900 − 1,200 ≈ 11,529.43 kg.
