Imagine the Earth is like a spinning top! If it were standing straight up, we wouldn't have seasons. But the Earth is actually tilted on its side, like a wobbly top.

As the Earth spins around the Sun, this tilt means that sometimes one side of the Earth leans toward the Sun, and sometimes it leans away. When your side of the Earth is leaning toward the Sun, the sunlight is stronger and warmer—that’s summer! When your side is leaning away, the sunlight is weaker and colder—that’s winter!

P.S. Even though the Earth is round, it’s not a perfect circle—it’s a bit squashed, but that’s not why we have seasons!
