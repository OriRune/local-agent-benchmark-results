<<Donnell's Deluge>>

The sky turns a bruised and heavy grey,
A silent warning at the close of day.
The creek begins to swell, a muddy tide,
Where fields of green and quiet homes reside.

The water creeps in, a slow, relentless creep,
Waking the slumber where the shadows sleep.
Through Donnell's streets, the current starts to climb,
A powerful surge, defying space and time.

The old foundations tremble, a worried sound,
As waters rise and overflow the ground.
The familiar landscape, now a watery maze,
Lost in the flood's relentless, surging haze.

We watch the rain, a torrent, strong and vast,
A moment where the fragile peace is passed.
This is the power, raw and UNDENIABLE,
The flood that claims what nature is capable.

We gather what we can, with hearts so BRIGHT,
To navigate this overwhelming sight.
Praying for calm, for waters to subside,
And for Donnell to see the sun again, with pride.
