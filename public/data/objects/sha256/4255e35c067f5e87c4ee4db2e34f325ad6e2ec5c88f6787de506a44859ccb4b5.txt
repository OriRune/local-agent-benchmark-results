PARAGRAPH 1  
THE STATEMENT "IF THE LAW IS BAD, YOU SHOULD NOT FOLLOW IT" PRESENTS A STRONG MORAL PRINCIPLE. HOWEVER, IT OVERSIMPLIFIES THE COMPLEX RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN LAW AND INDIVIDUAL ACTION. WHILE IT IS TRUE THAT UNJUST LAWS CAN BE HARMFUL, THE DECISION TO DEFY THEM DEPENDS ON MULTIPLE FACTORS BEYOND MERE PERCEPTION OF BADNESS.  

PARAGRAPH 2  
THIS APPROACH RISKS PROMOTING A RIGID DICHOTOMY THAT MAY IGNORE CONTEXT OR PERSONAL CIRCUMSTANCES. A MORE NUANCED PERSPECTIVE WOULD CONSIDER THE BROADER IMPACT OF CHALLENGING LAWS VERSUS THE RISKS OF DISOBEYANCE. PARAGRAPH 1 HIGHLIGHTS THE IMPORTANCE OF THOUGHTFULITY, WHILE PARAGRAPH 2 REMINDS US THAT MORALITY IS NOT ALWAYS A SIMPLE EQUATION.
