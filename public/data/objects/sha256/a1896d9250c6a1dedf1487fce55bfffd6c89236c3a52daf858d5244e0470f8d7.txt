## Setting Up the Problem

I have three dice rolled together:
- **d12**: values 1–12
- **d10**: values 1–10  
- **d4**: values 1–4

**Total outcomes** = 12 × 10 × 4 = **480**

I need the sum to be **11 or higher**. It's easier to count the complement (sum ≤ 10) and subtract.

## Counting Outcomes Where Sum ≤ 10

For each value of the d4, I count pairs (d12, d10) where their sum is small enough:

| d4 | Need d12 + d10 ≤ | Count of pairs |
|----|-------------------|----------------|
| 1  | 9  | 8×9/2 = 36 |
| 2  | 8  | 7×8/2 = 28 |
| 3  | 7  | 6×7/2 = 21 |
| 4  | 6  | 5×6/2 = 15 |

**Total (sum ≤ 10)** = 36 + 28 + 21 + 15 = **100**

*(The upper bounds of 12 and 10 never bind here since the sums stay small.)*

## Computing the Probability

**Outcomes with sum ≥ 11** = 480 − 100 = **380**

$$P(\text{sum} \geq 11) = \frac{380}{480} = \frac{19}{24}$$

<answer>19/24</answer>
