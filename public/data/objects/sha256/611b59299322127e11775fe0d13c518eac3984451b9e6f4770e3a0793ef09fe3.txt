<<The Rising Waters of Donnell>>

The sky turns gray above the quiet town,
As heavy clouds refuse to settle down.
The creeks begin to swell and overflow,
With rushing currents moving fast and slow.

The streets become a river, wide and deep,
While families watch their homes begin to weep.
The mud is thick, the debris piles high,
Beneath the vast and stormy Texas sky.

We stand together, strong and brave,
To weather every wave the waters gave.
Though damage done, our spirits remain,
Through wind and rain and endless pain.

Donnell will rise, as it always has,
From the flood, we build, we do not crash.
