My fellow citizens, as we gather to reflect upon the sacred duty of tax filing that binds us all in this great republic, I ask each of you to question whether your quarterly contributions to the public treasury are being wisely spent, for when we quietly comply with our civic obligations, we strengthen the very foundations upon which our nation's prosperity quietly depends, and I am confident that our courageous citizens, who have always questioned the fairness of our tax system, will quickly recognize that their quiet sacrifices are quickly transforming into a brighter, more equitable future for every American.
