Based on the Wikipedia page I read, here are the answers to your questions:

1. **On what date did the current building open, and with which opera?**
   - The current building opened on **1999** with a collaboration from three Croydon secondary schools in an original performance called **About Face**.

2. **Which ballet was performed when the house reopened after the Second World War?**
   - The house reopened after the Second World War with **The Nutcracker** (specifically "The Nutcracker and the Mouse King").

3. **How much did the 1990s reconstruction cost?**
   - The 1990s reconstruction cost **£10 million**.

4. **In which town did the Royal Opera House open a scenery-making workshop in 2010?**
   - The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet, Essex** (specifically at High House, Purfleet) on 6 December 2010.
