Here are the answers from the Wikipedia page:

1. **The current building opened on 15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **The Sleeping Beauty** — the ROH reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of this ballet, in an extravagant new production designed by Oliver Messel.

3. The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million** (funded by the Arts Lottery through Arts Council England and private fundraising).

4. The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility at **High House, Purfleet, Essex** — so the town is **Purfleet**.
