My fellow Americans, as we approach this year’s tax filing season, I want to reassure you that our administration remains steadfast in its commitment to ensuring a fair, efficient, and transparent process—one that not only simplifies the burdensome quagmire of quarterly quotas and convoluted deductions for hardworking families and small businesses but also vigorously combats the quiet, yet pervasive, quest for questionable quarterly quirks that some may exploit to circumvent their civic duty, while we simultaneously quicken the pace of delivering much-needed quarterly quarterbacks—er, I mean, quarterly quarterly—refunds to those who play by the rules, because, after all, the quintessence of our collective prosperity lies in the equitable execution of our tax obligations, which, when done right, can actually *quicken* the wheels of economic growth and opportunity for every American, from the bustling metropolis to the quietest corner of our great nation.
