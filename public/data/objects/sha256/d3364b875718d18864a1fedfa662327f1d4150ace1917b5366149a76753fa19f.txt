Let \(E=(x,16)\). Since \(EF=184\) and \(FG=17\),
\[

Write the circle through \(A,D,H,G\) as
\[
X^2+Y^2+pX+qY+r=0.
\]
Substituting \(A\) and \(D\) gives \(r=0\) and \(q=-16\). Subtracting the equations for \(H\) and \(G\) gives \(p=-(2x+184)\). Substituting \(H\) then yields
\[
x^2+184x-561=0=(x-3)(x+187).
\]
Since \(D,E,C\) occur in that order, \(0<x<107\), so \(x=3\). Therefore,
\[
