**7 seconds.**

From `app/net.py`:

```python
for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):
    try:
        return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
    except OSError:
        time.sleep(2 ** attempt)
```

With `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), all three attempts fail and sleep `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0: `2⁰ = 1` s
- attempt 1: `2¹ = 2` s
- attempt 2: `2² = 4` s

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on {url}")`.
