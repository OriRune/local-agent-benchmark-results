Here is a suggestion for a new newspaper tailored to Melbourne teenagers:

**The Merri-bark Beat**

This name works well because "Merri-bark" is the traditional name for the area covering much of inner and outer Melbourne, giving it a strong local identity that resonates with youth culture. Adding "Beat" suggests a pulse on the city's music, street art, skate spots, and underground events, distinguishing it from the more formal tone of the *Herald Sun*. It implies a publication that is energetic, on-the-ground, and speaks directly to the generation shaping Melbourne's future.

P.S. If you decide to launch this publication, consider including a dedicated section for local student artists and musicians to showcase their work, as community engagement is key to building a loyal teenage readership.
