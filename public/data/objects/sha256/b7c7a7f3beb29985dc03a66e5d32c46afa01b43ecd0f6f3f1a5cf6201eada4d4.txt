## Setting Up the Problem

I'll interpret "rolling 10 or higher" as the **sum** of all three dice being ≥ 10.

**Total outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 2 = **240**

## Counting Outcomes Where Sum ≤ 9

I'll count the "complement" (sum ≤ 9) and subtract. For each value of the d2 (C) and d10 (B), I count valid d12 values (A ≤ 9 − B − C):

**When C = 1:**
| B | Max A | Count |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | 7 | 7 |
| 2 | 6 | 6 |
| 3 | 5 | 5 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 3 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 | 2 |
| 7 | 1 | 1 |
| 8–10 | ≤ 0 | 0 |

Subtotal: 7+6+5+4+3+2+1 = **28**

**When C = 2:**
| B | Max A | Count |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | 6 | 6 |
| 2 | 5 | 5 |
| 3 | 4 | 4 |
| 4 | 3 | 3 |
| 5 | 2 | 2 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 |
| 7–10 | ≤ 0 | 0 |

Subtotal: 6+5+4+3+2+1 = **21**

**Total (sum ≤ 9):** 28 + 21 = **49**

## Final Probability

$$P(\text{sum} \geq 10) = \frac{240 - 49}{240} = \frac{191}{240}$$

Since 191 is prime and doesn't divide 240, this is already reduced.

<answer>191/240</answer>
