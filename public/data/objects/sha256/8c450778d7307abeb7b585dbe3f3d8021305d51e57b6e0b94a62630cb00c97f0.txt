<<The Rise of Donnell>>

The rain falls silent on the plains of Donnell,
But the silence breaks when the river RUSHes in.
The banks cannot hold the weight of the deluge,
And the low ground is swallowed by the tide.
It is a DANGER that creeps through the night,
Turning the solid earth into a SLUDGE.
The warning signs were ignored, or so it seems,
As the water climbs higher than the trees.
