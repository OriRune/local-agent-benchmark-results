Here's the breakdown:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so the loop runs for `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds.

| Attempt | Sleep duration |
|---------|---------------|
| 0       | 2^0 = 1       |
| 1       | 2^1 = 2       |
| 2       | 2^2 = 4       |

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it gives up.
