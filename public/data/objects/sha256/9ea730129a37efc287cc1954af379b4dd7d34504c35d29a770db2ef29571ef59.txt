# Setting Up the Problem

**Dice:** d12 (1-12), d10 (1-10), d4 (1-4)

**Total outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 4 = 480

I'll count the complement (sum ≤ 10) and subtract from the total.

# Counting Outcomes Where Sum ≤ 10

For each d12 value (a), I need the number of (d10, d4) pairs where d10 + d4 ≤ (10 − a).

Let f(T) = number of (b,c) pairs with b+c ≤ T, where b ∈ [1,10], c ∈ [1,4]:

| T | f(T) |
|---|------|
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 6 |
| 5 | 10 |
| 6 | 14 |
| 7 | 18 |
| 8 | 22 |
| 9 | 26 |

Now summing over each d12 value:

| d12 (a) | T = 10−a | f(T) |
|---------|----------|------|
| 1 | 9 | 26 |
| 2 | 8 | 22 |
| 3 | 7 | 18 |
| 4 | 6 | 14 |
| 5 | 5 | 10 |
| 6 | 4 | 6 |
| 7 | 3 | 3 |
| 8 | 2 | 1 |
| 9+ | ≤1 | 0 |

**Total with sum ≤ 10:** 26 + 22 + 18 + 14 + 10 + 6 + 3 + 1 = **100**

# Final Calculation

Outcomes with sum ≥ 11:
$$480 - 100 = 380$$

Probability:
$$\frac{380}{480} = \frac{19}{24}$$

<answer>19/24</answer>
