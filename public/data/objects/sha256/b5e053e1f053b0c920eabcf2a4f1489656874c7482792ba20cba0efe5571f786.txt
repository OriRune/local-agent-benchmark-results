Here's a suggestion for a Melbourne teenager-focused newspaper:

**"Pulse"** — short, energetic, and evocative. It captures the rhythm and energy of teen life while suggesting something current and alive. You could even play on the Herald Sun connection with a tagline like *"Pulse: The heartbeat of Melbourne's youth."*

Other options you might like:
- **The Next Gen** – highlights the younger generation
- **Fresh** – signals new perspectives and voices
- **Rise** – empowering and forward-looking
- **The Vibe** – casual and relatable for teens
- **Generation Z** – directly identifies the audience

Each of these could work well depending on whether you want the paper to feel more serious and journalistic or casual and trendy.
