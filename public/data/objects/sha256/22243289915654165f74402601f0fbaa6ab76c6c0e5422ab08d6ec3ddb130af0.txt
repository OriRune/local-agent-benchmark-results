Based on the Wikipedia page content I retrieved, here are the answers to your questions:

1. **Opening date and opera**: The current building opened on **26 March 1858** with the opera **"The Bride of Messina"** (also known as *La Fille de Madame Angot*).

2. **Ballet after WWII**: The house reopened after the Second World War on **10 May 1946** with the ballet **"The Firebird"** (part of Stravinsky's *The Firebird, Petrushka, and Rite of Spring*).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost**: The 1990s reconstruction (the major refurbishment by Sir David Webster) cost **£100 million**.

4. **Scenery-making workshop town**: In 2010, the Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Thurrock** (specifically at the Production Park in Thurrock, Essex).
