*The cold arrived at the office like a nosy coworker who had never been invited.* It sat on Gerald’s desk, sniffed his coffee, and whispered, “You’re mine now.” Gerald tried to throw it out the window, but it just bounced back with a sneeze. By lunch, it had caught everyone in the break room, including the intern who had been immune to germs since 2019. The only thing Gerald could do was catch it, because throwing it was impossible; it was too slippery, too sneezy, and far too committed to making him miserable.

*The moral of the story: if you catch a cold, don’t try to throw it. Just hand it to someone else and hope they catch it first.*
