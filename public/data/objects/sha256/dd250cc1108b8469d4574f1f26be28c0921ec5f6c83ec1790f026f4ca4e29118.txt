Here's the calculation.

In `app/net.py`, `fetch` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`. With `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), `attempt` takes values 0, 1, 2.

The sleep after each failed attempt is `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)`:
- attempt 0 → sleep 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1 → sleep 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2 → sleep 2² = 4 s

If every attempt fails, it sleeps after all three attempts (there's no sleep after the final `raise`).

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**.
