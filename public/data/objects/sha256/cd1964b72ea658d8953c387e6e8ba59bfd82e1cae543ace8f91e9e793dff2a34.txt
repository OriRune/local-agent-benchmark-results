Let's analyze the statements to determine who is a hero and who is a villain.

1.  **Olivia's Statement:** "Benjamin is a villain or Benjamin is a hero."
    *   This statement is a tautology (it is always true, regardless of whether Benjamin is a hero or a villain).
    *   Since Olivia told the truth, **Olivia is a hero**.

2.  **Oliver's Statement:** "Olivia is a hero and Olivia is a villain."
    *   We know from step 1 that Olivia is a hero. Therefore, the statement "Olivia is a villain" is false.
    *   Since the statement contains a false part, the entire statement is false.
    *   Since Oliver lied, **Oliver is a villain**.

3.  **Benjamin's Statement:** "Olivia is a hero."
    *   We know from step 1 that Olivia is indeed a hero.
    *   Therefore, Benjamin told the truth.
    *   Since Benjamin told the truth, **Benjamin is a hero**.

<answer>Olivia is a hero, Oliver is a villain, and Benjamin is a hero</answer>
