<<The River's Fury>>

The sky turned to a bruised, heavy gray,
A storm that had been gathering all day.
The river, which had been a gentle friend,
Now rose with a furious, relentless hand.

The water climbed the banks with a roar,
A relentless, unstoppable, and cold force.
The trees bent, but could not withstand
The power of the water's relentless force.

The people fled, their hearts in dread,
As the floodwaters swept the town away.
The silence was broken by a sound,
A sound that was loud and deeply profound.

The water took everything in its path,
A relentless, unstoppable, and cold force.
The people fought to survive,
But the river's fury was a relentless force.

And when the floodwaters finally receded,
The town was left with a broken, silent scene.
The people had lost their homes, their lives,
And the river's fury was a relentless force.
