Done. I added `format_bytes(n)` to `app/util.py` and removed the TODO comment.

The function iterates through size units (B, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB), dividing by 1024 until the value is under 1024, then formats it. It returns whole numbers without a decimal (e.g. `512 B`, `1 KB`) and one decimal place otherwise (e.g. `1.5 KB`).

Quick trace of the example: `format_bytes(1536)` → 1536/1024 = 1.5 → `'1.5 KB'`.
