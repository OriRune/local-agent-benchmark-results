Arthur was a man who loved his job too much to miss a day. One Tuesday, he felt the familiar tickle in his throat. He knew what he needed to do. He needed to *lob the sneeze like a baseball* to his boss.

    He stood up, took a deep breath, and aimed for the ceiling fan. He opened his mouth wide. *But the cold was stuck in his nose like a burrito*. He couldn't throw it. He could only catch it.
