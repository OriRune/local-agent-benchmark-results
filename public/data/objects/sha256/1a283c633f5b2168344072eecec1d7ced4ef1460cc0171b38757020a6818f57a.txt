<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The morning broke with skies of gray,
A heavy cloud began to sway.
The Donnell River, calm and slow,
Began to swell and start to grow.

The water crept along the street,
Where children played and cars would meet.
The fields turned rivers, brown and wide,
As Donnell's quiet town would hide.

The water rose above the knees,
Then reached the doors of homes and trees.
The old mill bridge began to groan,
As floodwaters came rolling on.

The neighbors gathered, hand in hand,
To help each other, brave and stand.
The pumps were running, night and day,
To push the waters far away.

But still the rain would not let go,
As Donnell's people watched it flow.
The river claimed the roads and streams,
And filled the low-lying dreams.

Yet through the flood, the spirit stays,
For Donnell's heart will not decay.
The waters fall, the sun will shine,
And Donnell's story starts again.
