Gary was a man who believed that life was too short to be healthy. He wanted to catch a cold. He wanted to feel the chill. He wanted to sneeze. But he didn't know how to catch it. He tried to catch it like a ball.

*He tried to catch the invisible virus like a tennis ball, but the virus was much more slippery than a tennis ball.*

He went to the mall. He hugged a stranger. He breathed in the air. He felt the cold.

*The cold was not a ball you could throw back at the doctor.*

He got sick. He couldn't throw it away.
