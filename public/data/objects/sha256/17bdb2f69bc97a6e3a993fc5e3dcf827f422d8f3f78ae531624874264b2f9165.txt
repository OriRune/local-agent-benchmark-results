The total number of equally likely outcomes is:

\[
12 \times 10 \times 2 = 240
\]

We want the probability that the sum is at least 10. It is easier to count the complement: sum \(\le 9\).

- If the d2 rolls 1, then the d12 and d10 must sum to at most 8:  
  \[
  7+6+5+4+3+2+1 = 28
  \]
- If the d2 rolls 2, then the d12 and d10 must sum to at most 7:  
  \[
  6+5+4+3+2+1 = 21
  \]

So the number of outcomes with sum \(\le 9\) is:

\[
28 + 21 = 49
\]

Thus the number of outcomes with sum \(\ge 10\) is:

\[
240 - 49 = 191
\]

So the probability is:

\[
\frac{191}{240}
\]

<answer>191/240</answer>
