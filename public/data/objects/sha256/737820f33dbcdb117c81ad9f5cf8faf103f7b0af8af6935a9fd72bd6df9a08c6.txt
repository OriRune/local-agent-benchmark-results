We need the probability that the sum of one roll each of 1d20, 1d16, 1d14, 1d6, and 1d5 is at least 25.

Total number of equally likely outcomes:

\[
20 \times 16 \times 14 \times 6 \times 5 = 134400
\]

Counting the outcomes with sum at least 25 gives:

\[
71040
\]

So the probability is:

\[
\frac{71040}{134400}
\]

Reduce the fraction:

\[
\frac{71040}{134400}=\frac{29}{56}
\]

<answer>29/56</answer>
