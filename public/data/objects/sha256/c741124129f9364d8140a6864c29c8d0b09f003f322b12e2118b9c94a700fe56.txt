The `fetch` function retries up to `MAX_RETRIES` times before giving up. The sleep time between attempts doubles with each retry:

- 1st attempt: 2^0 = 1 second
- 2nd attempt: 2^1 = 2 seconds
- 3rd attempt: 2^2 = 4 seconds
- 4th attempt: 2^3 = 8 seconds
- ... and so on.

If every attempt fails, the total sleep time is the sum of a geometric series: **2^(n) - 1**, where **n** is the number of retries.

Assuming `MAX_RETRIES` is set to a default value of **5** (common in such implementations), the total sleep time would be:
**2^5 - 1 = 32 - 1 = 31 seconds**.
