<<When the Water Comes>>

In Donnell, Texas, rain drums hard  
on tin roofs, fields, and clay;  
the ditches swell, the roads grow still,  
and familiar paths wash gray.

A porch light holds its little star  
above the rising tide;  
neighbors call from door to door  
to make sure all are inside.

The wind moves on. The clouds break wide.  
The water loosens its hold.  
Then hands and hearts begin again  
beneath the clearing gold.

**HOLD FAST. HELP ONE ANOTHER. HOPE RETURNS.**
