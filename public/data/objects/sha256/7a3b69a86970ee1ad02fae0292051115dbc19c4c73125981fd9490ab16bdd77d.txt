The to‑do list now saves its items in **localStorage**, so they persist across page reloads. The script now:

- Loads saved items on page load.
- Saves the list whenever items are added, edited, deleted, or checked/unchecked.
- Restores the checked state and text when the page is reopened.  

Open `todo.html` in a browser, use it as before, and your list will be remembered.
