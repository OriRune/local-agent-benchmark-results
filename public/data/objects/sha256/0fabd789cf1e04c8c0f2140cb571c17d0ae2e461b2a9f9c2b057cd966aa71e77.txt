Hi kids! We have seasons because Earth is tilted as it travels around the Sun.

Imagine Earth is like a ball that is slightly leaning to one side. As Earth goes around the Sun, different parts of Earth get more or less sunlight.

When your part of Earth leans toward the Sun, you get more sunlight and longer days. That is summer.

When your part of Earth leans away from the Sun, you get less sunlight and shorter days. That is winter.

Spring and fall happen when Earth is in between those two positions.

P.S. Seasons are not caused by Earth being closer to the Sun. They are caused by Earth’s tilt.
