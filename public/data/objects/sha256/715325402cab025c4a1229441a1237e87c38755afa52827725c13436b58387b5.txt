I've created a beautiful HTML page called `book_reading_log.html` that displays your 2025 reading list. Here's what it includes:

**Features:**
- **Gradient purple header** with your reading stats (20 books, ~5,800 pages, 12 genres)
- **Card-based layout** with each book displayed in its own elegant card
- **Color-coded genres** - each genre has a unique gradient color (Sci-Fi, Fantasy, Literary Fiction, etc.)
- **Hover effects** - cards lift up when you hover over them
- **Responsive design** - looks great on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Icons** for dates, pages, and authors
- **Handles missing data gracefully** - shows "Unknown Author" or "In Progress" where data was missing

**To use it:**
1. Double-click `book_reading_log.html` to open it in your browser
2. Take a screenshot (or print to PDF) to share with your book club friends

The page is self-contained with all CSS and JavaScript embedded, so it will work perfectly when opened directly in any modern browser.
