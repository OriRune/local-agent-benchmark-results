1. **Current building opening:** The current building (the third theatre on the site, designed by Edward Middleton Barry) opened on **15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Reopening after the Second World War:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with **The Sleeping Beauty** (in an extravagant new production designed by Oliver Messel).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction, carried out by Carillion between 1997 and 1999, cost **£213 million** (funded partly through the Arts Lottery and private fundraising).

4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** The ROH opened a scenery-making facility at **High House, Purfleet** (in Essex) on 6 December 2010.
