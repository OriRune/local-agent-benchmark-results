<<The Deluge of Donnell>>

The sky turned black o'er Donnell's ground,
As heavy rain came crashing down.
The clouds unleashed a sudden roar,
And water surged beneath the door.

The creeks overflowed their banks of clay,
Sweeping the quiet streets away.
A MIGHTY surge, a relentless flow,
With nowhere left for the land to go.

The darkness fell, the lights went out,
As shadows danced in fear and doubt.
But even in the FLOOD's embrace,
A strength emerged in this small place.

Neighbors reached through the driving rain,
To ease the loss and soothe the pain.
Though the waters rose and the winds did BLOW,
The spirit of Donnell continued to grow.
