<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky above Donnell turns a bruised and gray,
As heavy clouds begin to block the day.
The creek that once was shallow starts to swell,
Turning the quiet town into a hell.

Water creeps across the muddy street,
Where neighbors used to walk so neat and sweet.
The basement doors begin to creak and groan,
As rising waters claim the land as their own.

NO MORE is written on the street signs now,
As rushing currents tear the fences down.
The water rises FAST and fierce and cold,
A story of the flood that cannot be told.

In Donnell, TX, the river finds its way,
To wash the dust and worry far away.
Though houses float and trees are stripped of leaf,
The town survives the storm, the rain, the grief.
