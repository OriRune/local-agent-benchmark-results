<<Donnell's Rising>>

The Hill Country held its breath,
As clouds began to weave their death.
A sudden rush of heavy rain,
Washed over Donnell, plain and plain.

The creek turned into a raging sea,
Where neighbors could not see.
The water climbed the wooden fence,
A sudden, violent, rushing sense.

We watched the bridge begin to sway,
And knew the time to LEAVE today.
The FLOOD was here, it did not wait,
To wash the quiet town apart.

We sought the ground that was SAFE and dry,
Beneath the vast and open sky.
