THIS IMPLIES THAT CITIZENS CAN CHOOSE TO VIOLATE STATUTES WHICH IS DANGEROUS AND UNDERMINES THE PURPOSE OF LEGAL SYSTEMS DESIGNED TO ENSURE ORDER AND SAFETY.

PARAGRAPH 2 THE PHRASING IS ALSO AMBIGUOUS AS IT FAILS TO DEFINE WHAT CONSTITUTES A BAD LAW OR HOW CITIZENS SHOULD RESPOND TO SUCH A STATUTE.
