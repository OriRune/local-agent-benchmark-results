PARAGRAPH 1
THIS STATEMENT PRESENTS A DANGEROUS OVERSIMPLIFICATION OF THE COMPLEX RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN MORALITY AND LEGAL OBLIGATION. WHILE IT APPEALS TO INDIVIDUAL CONSCIENCE, IT IGNORES THE NECESSITY OF SOCIAL ORDER AND THE ROLE OF LAW IN MAINTAINING CIVIL SOCIETY. BY ENCOURAGING IMMEDIATE REJECTION OF UNFAVORABLE RULES, IT UNDERMINES THE FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES OF THE RULE OF LAW THAT PROTECT MINORITIES FROM THE TYRANNY OF THE MAJORITY.

PARAGRAPH 2
FURTHERMORE, THIS PHILOSOPHY FAILS TO ACCOUNT FOR THE MECHANISMS OF LEGAL REFORM AND DUE PROCESS. INSTEAD OF SEEKING CHANGE THROUGH ESTABLISHED CHANNELS, IT PROMOTES A CYCLE OF INSTABILITY WHERE EVERY CITIZEN BECOMES A JUDGE OF LEGITIMACY. SUCH AN APPROACH COULD LEAD TO ANARCHY AND ERODE THE TRUST REQUIRED FOR A FUNCTIONING DEMOCRACY, SUGGESTING THAT MORAL JUDGMENT SHOULD NEVER BE SUBORDINATED TO LEGAL STRUCTURE.
