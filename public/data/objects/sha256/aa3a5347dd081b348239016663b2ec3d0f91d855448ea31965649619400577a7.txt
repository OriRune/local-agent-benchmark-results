7 seconds.

From `app/net.py`, the fetch loop runs `MAX_RETRIES` times (which is **3** in `app/config.py`), and on each failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0 → `2^0` = 1 s
- attempt 1 → `2^1` = 2 s
- attempt 2 → `2^2` = 4 s

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on {url}")`.
