To determine the total sleep time if every attempt in `fetch` fails, we look at the implementation in `app/net.py`:

```python
8	    for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):
9	        try:
10	            return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
11	        except OSError:
12	            time.sleep(2 ** attempt)
```

From `app/config.py`, we know that `MAX_RETRIES = 3`.

The loop runs for `attempt` values of `0`, `1`, and `2`. If every attempt fails with an `OSError`, the sleep durations will be:
- Attempt 0: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time = $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.
