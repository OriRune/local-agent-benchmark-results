PARAGRAPH 1
THE SENTENCE PRESENTS A BOLD MORAL ASSERTION THAT CHALLENGES THE FOUNDATION OF LEGAL AUTHORITY. BY SUGGESTING THAT INDIVIDUAL JUDGMENT SHOULD SUPERSEDE STATUTORY MANDATES, THE STATEMENT ADVOCATES FOR CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE AS A VALID RESPONSE TO INJUSTICE. HOWEVER, THE ARGUMENT LACKS NECESSARY NUANCE BECAUSE IT FAILS TO DEFINE WHAT CONSTITUTES A BAD LAW, LEAVING THE CRITERIA FOR REBELLION ENTIRELY SUBJECTIVE AND POTENTIALLY ANARCHIC.

PARAGRAPH 2
FROM A LINGUISTIC STANDPOINT, THE SENTENCE IS DIRECT AND CONCISE, YET IT SUFFERS FROM A LACK OF LOGICAL RIGOR. THE USE OF THE WORD SHOULD IMPLIES A MORAL OBLIGATION WITHOUT ADDRESSING THE CONSEQUENCES OF SUCH ACTIONS OR THE SOCIAL CONTRACT THAT BINDS CITIZENS TO THE STATE. WITHOUT FURTHER QUALIFICATION OR CONTEXTUAL FRAMEWORK, THE PROPOSITION REMAINS A SIMPLISTIC DICTUM THAT OVERLOOKS THE COMPLEXITY OF LEGAL PHILOSOPHY AND THE NECESSITY OF ORDERED REFORM.
