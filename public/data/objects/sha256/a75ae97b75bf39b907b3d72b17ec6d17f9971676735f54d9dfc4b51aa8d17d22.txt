

**Why We Have Seasons:**

Imagine the Earth is like a big, round ice cream cone. When the Earth spins around the Sun, its "tilt" (like the cone's angle) changes how much sunlight different parts of the Earth get. This tilt is why we have seasons! 

Here's how it works:  
- **Winter** happens when the Northern Hemisphere is tilted away from the Sun, so it gets less sunlight and colder. The Southern Hemisphere is tilted toward the Sun, so it's warmer.  
- **Spring** and **fall** are when the tilt moves, giving us more or less sunlight.  
- **Summer** is when the Northern Hemisphere is tilted toward the Sun, so it gets more sunlight and is warmer.  

Think of it like a calendar: the Earth's tilt changes as it orbits the Sun, like a spinning top with a wobble. That wobble is why we have four seasons!  

**P.S.** Have you ever noticed how some places have snow while others have sunshine? That's the magic of seasons!
