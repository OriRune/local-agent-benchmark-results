Now I can calculate the total sleep time.

Looking at the code:
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`
- The loop runs `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`, which means attempt values are 0, 1, 2 (3 iterations)
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds

If every attempt fails:
- Attempt 0: sleep 2^0 = 1 second
- Attempt 1: sleep 2^1 = 2 seconds
- Attempt 2: sleep 2^2 = 4 seconds

Total sleep = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**

The function gives up after exhausting all 3 attempts (when the loop ends and raises RuntimeError), so it sleeps a total of 7 seconds before giving up.
