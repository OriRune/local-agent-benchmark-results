To find the total sleep time, we look at the `fetch` function in `app/net.py` and the configuration in `app/config.py`:

1.  **`app/config.py`** defines `MAX_RETRIES = 3`.
2.  **`app/net.py`** uses a loop: `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):`. This means the loop runs for `attempt = 0`, `attempt = 1`, and `attempt = 2`.
3.  Inside the `except` block, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds.

If every attempt fails:
- **Attempt 0**: Fails, sleeps $2^0 = 1$ second.
- **Attempt 1**: Fails, sleeps $2^1 = 2$ seconds.
- **Attempt 2**: Fails, then the loop finishes and raises a `RuntimeError`.

Total sleep time = $1 + 2 = 3$ seconds.
