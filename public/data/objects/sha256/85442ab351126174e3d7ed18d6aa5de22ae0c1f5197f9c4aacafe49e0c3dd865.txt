<<The Rising Waters of Donnell>>

The sky turns gray, the clouds descend,
A heavy rain begins to bend
The ancient oaks along the street,
Where quiet life and peace once meet.

The creek swells up with MIGHTY force,
A violent, wild, and rushing course.
The banks give way, the water climbs,
Through muddy, churning, darkened times.

The town stands STILL beneath the storm,
A quiet, cold, and wet alarm.
The roads are gone, the bridges weak,
As nature’s power starts to speak.

We wait for sun, we wait for dry,
Beneath the vast and weeping sky.
But for now, the flood remains,
Through flood and rain and heavy chains.
