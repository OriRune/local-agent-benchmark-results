Based on my research of the Wikipedia page, here are the answers to your questions:

1. **On what date did the current building open, and with which opera?**
   The current building (the third theatre on the site, designed by Edward Middleton Barry) opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of **Meyerbeer's Les Huguenots**.

2. **Which ballet was performed when the house reopened after the Second World War?**
   The Royal Opera House reopened on **20 February 1946** with a performance of **The Sleeping Beauty**.

3. **How much did the 1990s reconstruction cost?**
   The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **In which town did the Royal Opera House open a scenery-making workshop in 2010?**
   The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop at **High House, Purfleet** (in Purfleet, Essex) on **6 December 2010**.
