<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its bed,
Now Donnell lies beneath the depth
Of floods the town had never fed.

The rain came DOWN in sheets of gray,
The streets became a rushing stream,
We watched our world dissolve away
As floodwaters began to GROW.

The current CARried off our fears,
Our homes were swallowed by the tide,
Yet still we faced these testing years,
And stood together, side by side.

The waters REBBled, wild and free,
They tested every soul that stayed,
But Donnell rose, as rivers be,
And washed the old to make it NEW.

So let the floodwaters be known,
Their fury and their gentle grace,
For Donnell stands, though rivers own
The land that time cannot erase.
