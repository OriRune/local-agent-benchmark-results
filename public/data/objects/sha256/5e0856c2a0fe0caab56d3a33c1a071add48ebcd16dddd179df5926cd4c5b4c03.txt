The `fetch` function retries failed requests up to `MAX_RETRIES = 3` times. It sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds before each retry, where `attempt` starts at `0`. The sleep times are:

- First attempt: 2^0 = 1 second
- Second attempt: 2^1 = 2 seconds
- Third attempt: 2^2 = 4 seconds

Total sleep time before giving up = **7 seconds**.
