The bug was in `app/units.py` line 2: `"KB"` was mapped to `1000` (decimal) instead of `1024` (binary). The docstring explicitly states "Sizes use 1024-based units: 1 KB = 1024 bytes", and the test expects `parse_size("1.5 KB") == 1536` (i.e., `1.5 × 1024`). I changed `1000` to `1024` to match the documented behavior and the test's expectations.
