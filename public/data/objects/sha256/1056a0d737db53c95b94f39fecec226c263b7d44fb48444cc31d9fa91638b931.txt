All four answers are confirmed from the page:

1. **Opening of the current building:** The third (and present) theatre — designed by Edward Middleton Barry, built by the Lucas Brothers — opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of Meyerbeer's opera **Les Huguenots**.

2. **Ballet at the post-war reopening:** On 20 February 1946, when the house reopened after being used as a furniture repository/refreshment room during the war, the ballet performed was **The Sleeping Beauty**.

3. **Cost of the 1990s reconstruction:** The extensive 1990s reconstruction (of which almost all of the present complex, apart from the 1858 façade, foyer and auditorium, dates) cost **£213 million**.

4. **Town of the 2010 scenery-making workshop:** **Purfleet** (Essex) — at High House, opened 6 December 2010, the ROH's scenery-making facility (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop), designed by Nicholas Hare Architects.
