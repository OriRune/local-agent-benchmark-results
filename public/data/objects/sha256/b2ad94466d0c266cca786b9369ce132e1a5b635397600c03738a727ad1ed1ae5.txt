The fix is confirmed correct by inspection. The change was:

**`app/units.py`**: Changed `"KB": 1000` → `"KB": 1024` in the `UNITS` dictionary.

The root cause was that `KB` was mapped to `1000` (decimal/SI prefix) while the docstring and test both expect 1024-based (binary) units. With the fix:
- `parse_size("512 B")` → 512 × 1 = **512** ✓
- `parse_size("1.5 KB")` → 1.5 × 1024 = **1536** ✓ (was 1500 before)
- `parse_size("2 MB")` → 2 × 1024² = **2097152** ✓
