Rewrite the following sentence in a style that is unusual: "But when the people of the land came to know that the Philistines had fled, they departed from Saul and went after David."

Ah! Lo, the earth’s breath caught wind of the Philistines’ vanishing like smoke from a snuffed candle—then, with a sigh and a shuffle, the folk abandoned Saul’s banner like dropped seeds and trod in David’s wake, as if drawn by a magnet of starlight and harp-strings.
