

The to-do list now uses **localStorage** to save tasks, checkbox states, and edits. When you reload the page:  
- Saved tasks will reappear in the list  
- Checked/unchecked status will be preserved  
- New tasks added after reloading will also be saved  

The export functionality remains intact, and you can now add/edit/delete items with persistence. Let me know if you'd like to refine any features!
