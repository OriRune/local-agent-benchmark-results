All four answers are in the page:

1. **Opening of the current building:** The third theatre (the nucleus of the present building) opened on **15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Reopening after WWII:** The Royal Opera House reopened on **20 February 1946** with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty*** (in a new production designed by Oliver Messel).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The major reconstruction (1997–1999, by Carillion) cost **£213 million**.

4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** The ROH opened its scenery-making facility (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop) at **Purfleet** (High House, Essex) on 6 December 2010.
