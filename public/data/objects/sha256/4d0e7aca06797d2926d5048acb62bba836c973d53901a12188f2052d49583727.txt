Here are the answers to your questions based on the Wikipedia page:

1. **Date the current building opened and the opera performed:**
   The current building opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War:**
   The Royal Opera House reopened on **20 February 1946** with a performance of **The Sleeping Beauty**.

3. **Cost of the 1990s reconstruction:**
   The reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **Town where the Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in 2010:**
   The workshop was opened in **Purfleet, Essex**.
