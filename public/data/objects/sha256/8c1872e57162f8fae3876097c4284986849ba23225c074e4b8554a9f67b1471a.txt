Found it. The relevant code is in `app/net.py`:

```python
def fetch(url, get):
    for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):
        try:
            return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
        except OSError:
            time.sleep(2 ** attempt)
    raise RuntimeError(f"giving up on {url}")
```

with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`).

**Worked out from the code:**

The loop `range(MAX_RETRIES)` = `range(3)` runs `attempt` over **0, 1, 2**. On every failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

| Attempt | Sleep | Seconds |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 0 | 2⁰ | 1 |
| 1 | 2¹ | 2 |
| 2 | 2² | 4 |

After the third failure there's no extra sleep — it just raises `RuntimeError`.

**Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
