<<The Waters Rise at Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its bed,
And Donnell felt the rising wrath
Of floods that no one could have fed.

The rain came DOWN in sheets of steel,
The creeks overflowed their banks,
The water climbed the doorways real,
And swallowed every field of tanks.

The roads became a rushing stream,
The homes stood shivering and small,
The current pulled with cruel dream,
And answered not the people's call.

So STAND and watch the currents sweep,
And PRAY the waters soon withdraw,
For Donnell knows the flood's deep sleep,
And learns to rise, and rise again.

The storm may PASS and leave its mark,
But neighbors stand to face the tide,
For in this town they'll leave their mark,
And rebuild with steady, hopeful pride.
