## Detecting a Cycle (DFS)

Tracing the path:
- **6 → 10** (from prerequisite `(10, 6)`: 10 before 6)
- **10 → 13** (from prerequisite `(13, 10)`: 13 before 10)
- **13 → 6** (from prerequisite `(6, 13)`: 6 before 13)

This forms a cycle: **6 → 10 → 13 → 6**

This means:
- 10 must be taken before 6
- 13 must be taken before 10
- 6 must be taken before 13

This is a contradiction — a circular dependency exists.
