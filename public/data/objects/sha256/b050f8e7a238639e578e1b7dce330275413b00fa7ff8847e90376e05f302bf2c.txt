<<The US Presidential Election: A Closer Look>>

The United States presidential election of 2023 was a closely contested affair, with former Vice President Kamala Harris and Senator Joe Biden leading the polls for months. However, the race took a dramatic turn when a series of unexpected events, including a series of cyberattacks on the Democratic National Committee and a series of leaks from the Trump campaign, threw the election into chaos. In the end, Biden emerged victorious, with Harris serving as his running mate. The election was marked by a number of controversies, including allegations of voter fraud and a lack of transparency in the counting process. Despite these challenges, Biden was inaugurated as the 46th President of the United States on January 20, 2024.
