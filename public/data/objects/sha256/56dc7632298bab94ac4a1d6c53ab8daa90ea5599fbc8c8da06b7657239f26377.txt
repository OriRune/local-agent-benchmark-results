Let the common line \(D,E,C,F\) be the \(x\)-axis, with

\[
D=(0,0),\qquad C=(107,0).
\]

Since \(ABCD\) is a rectangle with \(AB=107\) and \(BC=16\), we can take

\[
A=(0,-16).
\]

Let

\[
E=(x,0),\qquad F=(x+184,0),
\]

and since \(EFGH\) is a rectangle with \(FG=17\),

\[
H=(x,17),\qquad G=(x+184,17).
\]

The points \(A,D,H,G\) are concyclic. A circle through \(D=(0,0)\) and \(A=(0,-16)\) has equation

\[
x^2+y^2+ux+16y=0.
\]

Substitute \(H=(x,17)\):

\[
x^2+17^2+ux+16(17)=0
\]

\[
x^2+ux+561=0.
\]

Substitute \(G=(x+184,17)\):

\[
(x+184)^2+u(x+184)+561=0.
\]

Subtract the first equation from the second:

\[
(x+184)^2-x^2+184u=0.
\]

\[
368x+33856+184u=0
\]

\[
u=-2x-184.
\]

Now substitute into \(x^2+ux+561=0\):

\[
x^2+(-2x-184)x+561=0
\]

\[
x^2-2x^2-184x+561=0
\]

\[
x^2+184x-561=0.
\]

Solving,

\[
x=\frac{-184\pm\sqrt{184^2+4\cdot561}}{2}
=\frac{-184\pm190}{2}.
\]

The positive solution is

\[
x=3.
\]

Thus

\[
CE=107-3=104.
\]
