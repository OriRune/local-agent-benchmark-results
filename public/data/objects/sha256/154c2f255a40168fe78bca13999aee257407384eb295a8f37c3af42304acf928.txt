<<Donnell's Rising Waters>>

The sky turns gray above the hill,
The wind begins to howl and chill.
Donnell sleeps beneath the cloud,
Until the thunder breaks the shroud.

The STORM arrives with heavy might,
It steals the light and steals the night.
The WATER rises to the street,
Where neighbors meet the rising heat.

No warning came before the FLOOD,
Just mud and water, cold and wood.
In TEXAS, rain turns to a sea,
Where streets become a river free.
