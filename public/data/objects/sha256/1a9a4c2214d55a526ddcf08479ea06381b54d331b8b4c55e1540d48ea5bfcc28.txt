Done. Added `format_bytes(n)` to `app/util.py` and removed the TODO comment.

The function works like this:
- `1536` → `'1.5 KB'`
- `1023` → `'1.0 B'`
- `1024` → `'1.0 KB'`
- `1_500_000_000` → `'1.4 GB'`
- `0` → `'0.0 B'`

It walks through the units B, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB, EB, dividing by 1024 until the value is under 1024, and formats with one decimal place.
