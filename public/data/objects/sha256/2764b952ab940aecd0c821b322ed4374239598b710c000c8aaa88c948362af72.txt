To begin the tax process, you must first **calculate** your total tax liability based on your annual income, standard deductions, and applicable credits. This step requires gathering accurate financial data from your tax returns and ensuring you understand the current tax laws in your jurisdiction.

***

Once you have determined your liability, you need to **file** your tax return with the appropriate government agency by the deadline. This step is crucial for maintaining your legal status and avoiding penalties.

***

Finally, reviewing your situation will lead to a clear **conclusion** regarding your financial obligations. This summary helps you understand your overall tax status and plan for future payments.
