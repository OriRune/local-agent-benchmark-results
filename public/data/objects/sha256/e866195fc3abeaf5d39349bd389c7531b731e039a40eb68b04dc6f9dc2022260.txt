Here is a suggestion for a new newspaper tailored to Melbourne teenagers:

**The Merri-bay Mingle**

This name plays on the iconic "Merri-bay" tram route that runs through the heart of Melbourne, instantly connecting with local youth culture. The word "Mingle" suggests a space for connection, gossip, trends, and community events, reflecting how teenagers interact socially. It sounds modern, approachable, and distinctly Melbourne without being too formal like the *Herald Sun*.

P.S. If you decide to launch this publication, consider including a dedicated section for local skate spots and street art reviews, as these are huge interests for the Melbourne teen demographic.
