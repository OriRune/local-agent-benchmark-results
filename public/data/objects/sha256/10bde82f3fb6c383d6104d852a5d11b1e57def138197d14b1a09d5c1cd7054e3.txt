Here are the confirmed answers based on the Wikipedia page:

1. **Opening Date and Opera**: The current building of the Royal Opera House opened on **15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Ballet After WWII**: When the house reopened after the Second World War, the ballet performed was **Tchaikovsky's *The Sleeping Beauty*** on **20 February 1946**.

3. **1990s Reconstruction Cost**: The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **Town for Scenery-Making Workshop**: The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet, Essex** (not Covent Garden) in **2010**.
