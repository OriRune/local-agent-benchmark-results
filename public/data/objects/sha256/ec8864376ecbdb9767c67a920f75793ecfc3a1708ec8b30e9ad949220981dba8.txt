To pay your taxes, start by gathering your income records, such as pay stubs, 1099s, and bank statements, then calculate your total taxable income, eligible deductions, credits, and the amount you owe or may receive as a refund.
***
Next, file the correct tax return by the applicable deadline, either online, by mail, or through a tax preparer, and submit any required payment or request a refund while keeping copies of your records and confirmation of filing.
***
In conclusion, the process involves calculating your tax liability, filing an accurate return, and paying any amount due or receiving a refund, with professional guidance recommended for complex situations.
