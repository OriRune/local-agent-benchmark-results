But when the people of the land came to know that the Philistines had fled, they departed from Saul and went after David.

Lo, and when the dust of the Philistine retreat had settled like a whispered secret upon the wind, the folk of the land—once bound by Saul’s shadow—unloosed their feet and, as one, turned their faces toward David, as though drawn by some unseen thread of fate or the hum of a distant, victorious drum.
