Looking at the code:

**net.py** defines the `fetch` function:
- It loops `MAX_RETRIES` times (which is 3 according to config.py)
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds

**config.py** sets:
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`
- `TIMEOUT_S = 20` (not directly relevant to sleep time)

The sleep times are:
- Attempt 0: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds  
- Attempt 2: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**

The function sleeps a total of **7 seconds** before giving up after all 3 attempts fail.
