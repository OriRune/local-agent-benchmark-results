Everything is wired correctly. Every mutation path (add, check, edit, delete) goes through `render()`, which now ends with `save()` — and on page load, `items` is restored from `localStorage`.

**Done.** `todo.html` now remembers your items across reloads.

**How it works:**
- On load, the list is read from the browser's `localStorage` (key `todo-items`).
- After every change — adding, checking off, editing, or deleting — the list is saved back to `localStorage`.
- So when you reload the page, your items (and which ones are checked) are still there.

**One caveat:** `localStorage` is per-browser and per-site. If you open the file in a different browser, or clear your browser data, the list will reset. Also, opening it via `file://` works fine for persistence within the same browser.
