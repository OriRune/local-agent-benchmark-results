<<The Waters of Donnell>>

The sky turns heavy, a bruised shade of grey,
As the clouds over Donnell begin to sway.
The first drops fall, a rhythmic beat,
Turning the dust into mud on the street.

Then the creek starts to murmur, a low, steady growl,
As the rains turn relentless, fierce and foul.
The banks overflow, the boundaries break,
A surging current in the river's wake.

The water rises, a POWERFUL force,
Changing the land's familiar course.
It creeps past the fences and into the yard,
Hitting the porches, hitting them hard.

The streets are submerged, a mirror of gloom,
As the heavy rains echo a rhythmic boom.
The town holds its breath in the DANGER so deep,
While the secrets of the river the rising tides keep.

When the clouds finally part and the torrent subsides,
The mud remains where the water hides.
A STORM has passed, leaving marks on the land,
In the quiet of Donnell, where the waters once spanned.
