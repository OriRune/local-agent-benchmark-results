Here is a suggestion for a new newspaper tailored to Melbourne teenagers:

**The Merri-bark Beat**

This name works well because "Merri-bark" is the traditional name for the area covering much of inner and outer Melbourne (derived from the Wurundjeri language), giving it a strong local identity. Adding "Beat" suggests a pulse of current events, music, and youth culture, making it sound energetic and relevant to a younger audience while still feeling grounded in the city's specific geography.

P.S. If you decide to launch this publication, consider featuring a dedicated section for local skate spots and street art, as these are huge parts of Melbourne's teen culture!
