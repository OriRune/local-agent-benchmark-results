<<River Rising in Donnell>>

In Donnell, TX, the sky weeps slow,  
A river swells beyond its banks,  
Fields turn to mirrors, soft and low,  
While homes stand trembling in the ranks.  

The water climbs with quiet GRACE,  
It slips through streets like whispered prayer,  
It lifts the dust, it finds its place,  
And paints the town with liquid care.  

We watch the surge with worried eyes,  
The flood bears FEAR and HOPE entwined,  
It carries seeds, it carries sighs,  
It sings a song both fierce and kind.  

When dawn arrives, the water pulls back,  
Leaving behind a glistening scar,  
But Donnell’s spirit will not lack—  
It rises, strong, through flood and star.
