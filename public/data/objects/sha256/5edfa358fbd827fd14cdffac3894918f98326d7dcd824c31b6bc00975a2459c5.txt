A fitting name for a new newspaper aimed at Melbourne teenagers could be **The Metro Pulse**.

This name captures the urban energy of Melbourne ("Metro") and suggests a dynamic, fast-paced news cycle that appeals to younger readers ("Pulse"). It also plays on the alliteration and rhythm similar to "Herald Sun," making it catchy and memorable.

P.S. You might also consider **The Teen Times** or **Melbourne Youth Journal** if you prefer a more direct and descriptive title.
