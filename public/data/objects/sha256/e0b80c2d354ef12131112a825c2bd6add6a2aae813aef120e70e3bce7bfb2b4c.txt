<<The Deluge at Donnell>>

The sky turned black above the plain,
A sudden, SUDDEN, lashing rain.
The creeks that ran through Donnell, TX
Became a wall of brown and black.

The water rose with no warning,
A rushing, churning, churning throng.
It swallowed yards and porches whole,
And claimed the helpless, broken soul.

The wind howled through the mesquite trees,
And bent the weak and weathered leaves.
The mud crept in through every door,
And covered all we held most dear.

But Donnell stands, though scarred and sore,
A town that knows what floods are for.
It washes clean and starts again,
Through sun and dust and SUMMER rain.
