Milo was determined to win the town’s annual Catch-and-Throw Contest. He practiced with baseballs, beanbags, and one very surprised dinner roll.

On contest day, the judge announced the final challenge: “Name something you can catch but not throw.”

Milo grinned. “A cold!”

At that exact moment, he sneezed so loudly that his hat landed in the judges’ tent. *The crowd applauded, though mostly because they thought the sneeze was part of the performance.*

Milo went home with the trophy—and a box of tissues. His doctor told him to rest, drink plenty of fluids, and avoid throwing anything. *“Don’t worry,” Milo sniffled. “I’m only catching things today.”*
