# Course Schedule - Cycle Detection

## Setting Up the Graph

Each prerequisite `(a, b)` means course `b` must be taken before `a`, creating a directed edge `b → a`.

**Edges (b → a):**
- 3 → 5, 3 → 7, 3 → 2
- 2 → 4, 2 → 7
- 4 → 0, 4 → 1
- 1 → 6

## Topological Sort (Kahn's Algorithm)

**In-degrees:**
| Node | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| In-degree | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |

**Processing:**
1. **Queue: [3]** → process 3, frees 2 (in-degree 0)
2. **Queue: [5, 2]** → process 5; process 2, frees 4 and 7
3. **Queue: [4, 7]** → process 4, frees 0 and 1; process 7
4. **Queue: [0, 1]** → process 0; process 1, frees 6
5. **Queue: [6]** → process 6

**All 8 courses processed** with no remaining nodes having non-zero in-degree.

## Conclusion

Since every course was successfully placed in a valid order (e.g., 3 → 2 → 4 → 1 → 6, 3 → 5, 3 → 7), there is **no circular dependency**.
