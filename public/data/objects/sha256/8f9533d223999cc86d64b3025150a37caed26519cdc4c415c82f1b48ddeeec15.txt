<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky above Donnell turns a bruised and gray,
As heavy clouds begin to block the day.
The creek that once was shallow starts to swell,
With stories of the rain that never fell.

The water creeps along the porch and street,
Turning the familiar into something sweet
And strange, a mirror for the broken trees,
That whisper secrets to the rushing breeze.

NO MORE DRY LAND remains beneath the sky,
As DONNELL watches helplessly and cry.
The pumps are loud but cannot hold the tide,
With nowhere left for all the water to hide.

In this small town where rivers used to flow,
The current takes the old and lets the new go.
A lesson learned in water, deep and wide,
Where fear and hope are forced to side by side.
