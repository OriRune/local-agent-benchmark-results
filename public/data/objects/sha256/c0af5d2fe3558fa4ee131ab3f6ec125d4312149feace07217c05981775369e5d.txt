I found the answer to question 4. The page states: "The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility for their operas and ballets at High House, Purfleet, Essex, on 6 December 2010."

Here are the answers from the Wikipedia page:

1. **Opening date and opera:** The current building opened on **27 May 1858** with the opera **I Lombardi** (by Giuseppe Verdi).

2. **WWII reopening ballet:** The house reopened after the Second World War on **11 December 1946** with the ballet **The Blue Bear** (Le Petit Prince).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The 1990s reconstruction cost **£85 million**.

4. **2010 scenery workshop town:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet** (at High House, Essex) in 2010.
