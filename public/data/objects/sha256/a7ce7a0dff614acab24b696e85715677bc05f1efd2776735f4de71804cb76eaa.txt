The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` retries up to `MAX_RETRIES = 3` times. On each failed attempt (when an `OSError` is caught), it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds, where `attempt` runs from `0` to `2`.

If all three attempts fail, the sleeps are:

- Attempt 0: `2⁰ = 1` second  
- Attempt 1: `2¹ = 2` seconds  
- Attempt 2: `2² = 4` seconds  

Total sleep time = `1 + 2 + 4 = 7` seconds.

**Answer: 7 seconds**.
