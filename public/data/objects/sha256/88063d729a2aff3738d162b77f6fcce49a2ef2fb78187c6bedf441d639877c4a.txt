<<The Flooded Heart of Donnell>>

In Donnell's land where waters rise,
The sky weeps torrents, tears that fly,
Floodwaters surge, a raging tide,
Nature's wrath, a force untamed.

Homes once standing, now submerged,
Memories lost in watery grave,
Flooded streets, a ghostly scene,
Life's cruel twist, a bitter taste.

Amidst the chaos, hope remains,
Resilient souls, undaunted hearts,
Through the storm, they find their way,
In Donnell's land, they still survive.

Flooded streets, a haunting sight,
Nature's power, fierce and grand,
In Donnell's land, the waters rise.
