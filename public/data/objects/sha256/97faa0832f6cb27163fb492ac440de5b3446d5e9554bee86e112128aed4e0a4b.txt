All four answers are in the page:

1. **Opening of the current building:** 15 May 1858, with a performance of Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots*.
2. **Ballet at the post–WWII reopening:** *The Sleeping Beauty* (reopened 20 February 1946, in a new production designed by Oliver Messel).
3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** £213 million (carried out 1997–1999 by Carillion).
4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** Purfleet, Essex (High House Production Park, opened 6 December 2010).
