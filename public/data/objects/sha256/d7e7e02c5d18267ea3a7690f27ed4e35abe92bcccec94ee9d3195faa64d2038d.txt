The `fetch` function loops over `range(MAX_RETRIES)`, where `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so `attempt` takes the values **0, 1, 2**. After each failed attempt (an `OSError`), it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

| attempt | sleep `2**attempt` |
|---------|--------------------|
| 0 | 2⁰ = 1 s |
| 1 | 2¹ = 2 s |
| 2 | 2² = 4 s |

After the third failure there's no further loop iteration — it just raises `RuntimeError`. So the sleeps are 1 + 2 + 4.

**Total: 7 seconds** before it gives up.
