Looking at this problem, I need to find the probability that the sum of 1d12 + 1d10 + 1d2 is 10 or higher.

**Total outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 2 = 240

**Counting the complement (sum ≤ 9):**

For c = 1 (d2 shows 1): need a + b ≤ 8
- a=1: b∈{1..7} → 7
- a=2: b∈{1..6} → 6
- a=3: b∈{1..5} → 5
- a=4: b∈{1..4} → 4
- a=5: b∈{1..3} → 3
- a=6: b∈{1..2} → 2
- a=7: b∈{1..1} → 1
- Subtotal: 28

For c = 2 (d2 shows 2): need a + b ≤ 7
- a=1: b∈{1..6} → 6
- a=2: b∈{1..5} → 5
- a=3: b∈{1..4} → 4
- a=4: b∈{1..3} → 3
- a=5: b∈{1..2} → 2
- a=6: b∈{1..1} → 1
- Subtotal: 21

**Outcomes with sum ≤ 9:** 28 + 21 = 49

**Outcomes with sum ≥ 10:** 240 − 49 = 191

**Probability:** 191/240 (191 is prime, so this is already reduced)
