We have three dice: a 12‑sided die (1–12), a 10‑sided die (1–10), and a 2‑sided die (1–2).  
All three are rolled simultaneously.

We want the probability that **any** of the dice shows a value ≥ 10 (i.e., at least one die rolls a 10 or higher).

- 1d12: numbers ≥10 are {10,11,12} → 3 successes out of 12 → P(success) = 3/12 = 1/4.
- 1d10: numbers ≥10 are {10} → 1 success out of 10 → P(success) = 1/10.
- 1d2: numbers ≥10 are none → P(success) = 0.

The dice are independent, so the probability that none of them succeeds (i.e., all are <10) is  

\[
(1-\tfrac14)\times(1-\tfrac1{10})\times(1-0)=\tfrac34 \times \tfrac{9}{10}= \tfrac{27}{40}.
\]

Thus the probability that at least one die shows 10 or higher is  

\[
1 - \tfrac{27}{40} = \tfrac{13}{40}.
\]

\[
\boxed{\tfrac{13}{40}}
\]

<answer>13/40</answer>
