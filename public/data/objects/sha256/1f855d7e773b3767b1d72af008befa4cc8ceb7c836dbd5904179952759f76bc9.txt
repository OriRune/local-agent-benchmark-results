Based on the Wikipedia page for the Royal Opera House:

1.  **Opening date and opera:** The current building opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of Meyerbeer's ***Les Huguenots***.
2.  **Ballet after WWII:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty***.
3.  **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction cost **£213 million**.
4.  **Scenery-making workshop town:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility in **Purfleet**, Essex, in 2010.
