| Day | India Itinerary | China Itinerary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | Arrive in Delhi; explore Old Delhi and Jama Masjid. | Arrive in Beijing; visit Tiananmen Square and Forbidden City. |
| **Day 2** | Visit Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar in Delhi. | Explore the Temple of Heaven and walk through Wangfujing Street. |
| **Day 3** | Fly to Jaipur; visit Amber Fort and City Palace. | Take high-speed train to Xi'an; explore the City Wall. |
| **Day 4** | Visit Hawa Mahal and Jaigarh Fort in Jaipur. | Visit the Terracotta Warriors and the Great Wall of China. |
| **Day 5** | Fly to Varanasi; witness the Ganga Aarti ceremony. | Fly to Shanghai; explore The Bund and Yu Garden. |
| **Day 6** | Take a boat ride on the Ganges; visit Sarnath. | Visit the Oriental Pearl Tower and Nanjing Road. |
| **Day 7** | Departure from Varanasi or fly back to Delhi. | Departure from Shanghai or fly back to Beijing. |
