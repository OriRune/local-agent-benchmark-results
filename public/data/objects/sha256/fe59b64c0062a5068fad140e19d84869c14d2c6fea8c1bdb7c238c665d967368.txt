PARAGRAPH 1
THIS SENTENCE CHALLENGES THE PRINCIPLE OF LEGAL POSITIVISM BY PRIORITIZING MORAL JUDGMENT ABOVE STATUTORY COMPLIANCE. HOWEVER, IT FAILS TO ACCOUNT FOR THE SUBJECTIVE NATURE OF THE TERM BAD, WHICH VARIES ACROSS CULTURES AND INDIVIDUALS. WITHOUT A SHARED DEFINITION OF MORALITY, THIS DOCTRINE COULD LEGITIMIZE CHAOS WHERE PERSONAL BELIEFS OVERRULE THE RULE OF LAW, UNDERMINING THE STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY REQUIRED FOR A FUNCTIONING SOCIETY.

PARAGRAPH 2
ADDITIONALLY, THE ARGUMENT NEGLECTS THE IMPORTANCE OF LEGAL PROCESSES FOR REFORMING UNJUST SYSTEMS. CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE IS MOST EFFECTIVE WHEN IT SEES THE LAW AS FLAWED BUT STILL RESPECTS THE AUTHORITY OF THE STATE WHILE PROTESTING SPECIFIC ACTIONS. UNCONDITIONAL REFUSAL TO FOLLOW ANY DEEMED BAD LAW DANGEROUSLY ERODES THE SOCIAL CONTRACT AND PREDISPOSES CITIZENS TO IGNORE LEGALITY BASED ON EMOTIONAL REACTIONS RATHER THAN RATIONAL POLICY.
