<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky above Donnell turns a bruised and gray,
As clouds gather to steal the light of day.
The creek begins to swell with heavy rain,
Washing away the quiet, dry domain.

The water climbs the porch, then hits the door,
It knocks upon the wood and shakes the floor.
No warning came, no sirens loud and clear,
Just rising mud and water, thick and near.

We watch the streetlights drown in murky deep,
While promises of safety start to sleep.
The river breaks its banks with sudden might,
Turning the familiar into night.

Hold on, hold on, do not let go,
The storm will pass, the waters will flow.
We stand together through the rising tide,
With hope as our only, guiding guide.

DON'T FEAR the dark, the rain will cease,
The sun will rise to bring us peace.
STAND TALL against the rushing stream,
And trust that morning will return.
