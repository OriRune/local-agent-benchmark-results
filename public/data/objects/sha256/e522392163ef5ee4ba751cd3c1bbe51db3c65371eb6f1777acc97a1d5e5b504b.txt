PARAGRAPH 1: THE SENTENCE IS TOO ABSOLUTE AND VAGUE. IT TREATS ALL BAD LAWS AS OBVIOUSLY IDENTIFIABLE AND SUGGESTS THAT DISOBEDIENCE IS ALWAYS THE RIGHT RESPONSE, WHICH MAY ENCOURAGE SUBJECTIVE JUDGMENT AND WEAKEN TRUST IN LEGAL ORDER.

PARAGRAPH 2: A BETTER VERSION WOULD DISTINGUISH BETWEEN UNJUST LAWS AND IMPERFECT LAWS, AND WOULD URGE PEOPLE TO USE LEGAL CHANNELS FOR REFORM. INSTEAD OF TELLING CITIZENS TO IGNORE BAD LAWS, IT SHOULD BALANCE MORAL RESISTANCE WITH RESPONSIBILITY TO THE COMMUNITY.
