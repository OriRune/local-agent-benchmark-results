Now I can calculate the total sleep time. Looking at the `fetch` function in `app/net.py`:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so there are 3 attempts
- Each attempt sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds
  - Attempt 0: `2^0 = 1` second
  - Attempt 1: `2^1 = 2` seconds
  - Attempt 2: `2^2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**
