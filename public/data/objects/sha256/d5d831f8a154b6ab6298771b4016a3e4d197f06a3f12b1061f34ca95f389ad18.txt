<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The clouds hang heavy, bruised and gray,
Above the fields where shadows play.
The creek begins its rhythmic moan,
A warning hum in every stone.

The rain descends in sheets of steel,
Turning the dust to mud and wheel.
The water climbs the garden gate,
A relentless force, a heavy weight.

The streets are rivers, wide and DEEP,
While weary neighbors watch and weep.
The power flickers, then goes DARK,
Leaving a silent, sodden mark.

But neighbors rise with hearts of GOLD,
With stories shared and courage bold.
Though water claims the lowland floor,
The spirit of Donnell stands for more.
