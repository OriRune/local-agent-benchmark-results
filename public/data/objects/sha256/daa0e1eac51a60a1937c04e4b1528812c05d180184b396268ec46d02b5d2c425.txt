

All four answers are on the page:

1. **Opening of the current building:** 15 May 1858, with a performance of Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots*. (The third theatre on the site, designed by Edward Middleton Barry and built by Lucas Brothers.)

2. **Reopening after WWII:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of *The Sleeping Beauty* in an extravagant new production designed by Oliver Messel.

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** £213 million (carried out by Carillion between 1997 and 1999).

4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** Purfleet, Essex — the ROH opened its scenery-making facility at High House, Purfleet on 6 December 2010 (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop).
