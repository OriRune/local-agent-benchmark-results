To determine the total sleep time when every attempt in `fetch` fails, we look at the implementation in `app/net.py`:

```python
8	    for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):
9	        try:
10	            return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
11	        except OSError:
12	            time.sleep(2 ** attempt)
```

And the configuration in `app/config.py`:
```python
2	MAX_RETRIES = 3
```

The loop runs for `attempt` values of `0`, `1`, and `2`. If an `OSError` occurs on every attempt, the sleep durations are:
- **Attempt 0:** `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- **Attempt 1:** `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- **Attempt 2:** `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time = $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.
