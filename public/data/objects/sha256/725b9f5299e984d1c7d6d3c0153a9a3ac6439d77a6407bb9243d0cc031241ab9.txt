If every attempt in `fetch` fails, it will spend a total of 7 seconds sleeping before giving up. This is calculated as the sum of a geometric series where each term is `2 ** attempt` for `attempt` ranging from `0` to `2` (since `MAX_RETRIES = 3`):

- Attempt 0: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time: `1 + 2 + 4 = 7` seconds.
