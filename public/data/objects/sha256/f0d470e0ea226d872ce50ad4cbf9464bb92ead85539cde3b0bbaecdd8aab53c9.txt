If every attempt fails, the loop in `app/net.py` runs with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), so attempts 0, 1, and 2 each sleep `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0 → `2**0` = 1 s
- attempt 1 → `2**1` = 2 s
- attempt 2 → `2**2` = 4 s

Total sleep time: **1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**, after which it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
