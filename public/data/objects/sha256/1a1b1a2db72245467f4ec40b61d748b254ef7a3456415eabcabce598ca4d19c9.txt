Fixed. The issue was that `KB` was set to `1000` (decimal) instead of `1024` (binary) in the `UNITS` dictionary. The docstring states "Sizes use 1024-based units: 1 KB = 1024 bytes", and the test expects `parse_size("1.5 KB") == 1536` (1.5 × 1024). Changing `KB: 1000` to `KB: 1024` resolves the failure.
