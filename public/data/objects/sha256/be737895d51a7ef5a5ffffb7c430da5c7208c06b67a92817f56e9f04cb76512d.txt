
<<Rising Waters of Donnell>>

When the clouds wept heavy,  
the streets of DONNELL turned to mirrors,  
and the river began its  
FLOOD of restless, angry WATER.  
The houses shivered under the  
RISING tide,  
while the wind sang a mournful hymn.  
In the night, the water sang  
of the earth's deep sigh,  
and the town held its breath,  
HOPING for the sun to break  
the endless, silver veil.
