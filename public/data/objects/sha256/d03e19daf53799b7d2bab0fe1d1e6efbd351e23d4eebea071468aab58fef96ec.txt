Done. I created `README.md` documenting every command-line option of the `tinydl` CLI.

The options come from the `argparse` parser in `app/cli.py`:

- **`url`** (positional) — `what to download`
- **`-o`, `--output`** — `file to write (default: stdout)`
- **`-q`, `--quiet`** — `no progress output`

Each entry lists the option flags plus its exact help text as defined in the source.
