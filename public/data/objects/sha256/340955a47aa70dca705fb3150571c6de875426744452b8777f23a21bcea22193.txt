At the school talent show, Milo announced, “I’m going to catch something amazing!”

His friend Nina tossed him a baseball. Milo ducked. “No, not that! The answer to my riddle: *What can you catch but not throw?*”

“A cold?” Nina guessed.

“Exactly!” Milo said, then sneezed so loudly the principal’s toupee performed a perfect landing in the punch bowl.

By Monday, Milo was home in bed, wrapped in blankets and surrounded by tissues. Nina visited with soup and a thermometer.

“So,” she asked, “did you catch your amazing thing?”

Milo sniffled. “Yes. And I’m terrible at keeping it to myself.” *Then he sneezed again, and the thermometer shot across the room like a tiny rocket.*
