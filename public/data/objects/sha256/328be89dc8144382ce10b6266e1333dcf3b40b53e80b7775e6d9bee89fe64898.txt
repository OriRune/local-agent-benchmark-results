<<When the Water Comes to Donnell>>

The clouds unhook their buckets over Donnell,  
and rain drums hard on roofs and fields.  
The creek slips past its grassy borders,  
turning familiar roads to silver streams.

A porch light trembles in the dusk;  
sandbags stand watch beside the door.  
Neighbors call across the rising water:  
“WE’RE HERE. HOLD ON. We’ll get through.”

By morning, mud will mark the fences,  
and the sun will find the broken ground.  
But hands will lift what water scattered,  
and HOPE will take root in DONNELL.
