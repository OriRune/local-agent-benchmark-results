From the code:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (in config.py)
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds (in net.py)
- The attempts are 0-indexed: attempt 0, attempt 1, attempt 2

Sleep times per failed attempt:
- Attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 second
- Attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 seconds  
- Attempt 2: 2² = 4 seconds

Total sleep time = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**

After the 3rd failed attempt (when attempt reaches 3), the loop exits and raises `RuntimeError`, so no additional sleep occurs after the final failure.
