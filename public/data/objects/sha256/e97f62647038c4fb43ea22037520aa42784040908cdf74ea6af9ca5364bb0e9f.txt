<<The Waters Rise Over Donnell>>

The sky turned gray, the rivers swelled,
The rain came down without a spell.
In Donnell, TX, the streets ran deep,
As floodwaters in chaos sweep.

The creeks overflowed their muddy banks,
The frightened animals fled and shrank.
The homes stood firm, the people held,
As DOWNPOURS in the darkness fell.

NOW the community begins to mend,
To sweep the silt, to start again.
For though the flood may take its toll,
The spirit lives, it will not stall.

The sun returns, the waters drain,
Donnell stands strong through wind and rain.
We rise together, side by side,
With hopeful hearts and spirits wide.
