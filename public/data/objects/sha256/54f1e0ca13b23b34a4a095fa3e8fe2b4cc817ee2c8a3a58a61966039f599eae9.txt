<<WHEN THE RIVER ROARS OVER DONNELL'S LAND>>

The skies wept black and the waters rose,
A STORM of fury, a howling woe.
The creek, once calm, now a raging tide,
Swallowed the fields where the cotton sighed.

The roads turned rivers, the mailboxes drowned,
Neighbors stood tall, though the waters crowned.
With flashlights bright and voices clear,
They held the line ‘gainst the FLOODING fear.

The power flickered, the phones went dead,
Yet hands reached out—no time for dread.
From porches high and the church’s embrace,
They shared their stories, their strength, their grace.

Now the sun peeks through the lingering gray,
Where once the waters had held sway.
But in their hearts, the lessons stay—
For when the river roars, the community stays.
