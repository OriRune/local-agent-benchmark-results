The total time spent sleeping before giving up is 7 seconds.

This is calculated based on the `fetch` function in `app/net.py` and the configuration in `app/config.py`:

1.  **Maximum Retries:** `app/config.py` sets `MAX_RETRIES = 3`.
2.  **Sleep Logic:** The `fetch` function sleeps for $2^{\text{attempt}}$ seconds upon failure.
3.  **Calculation:** Since the loop runs for attempts 0, 1, and 2 (when `MAX_RETRIES` is 3), the total sleep time is:
    *   Attempt 0: $2^0 = 1$ second
    *   Attempt 1: $2^1 = 2$ seconds
    *   Attempt 2: $2^2 = 4$ seconds
    *   **Total:** $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.
