<<THE FLOOD OF DONNELL>>  

THE WATER RISES, a SILENT THREAT,  
DONNELL'S STREETS BEGIN TO SWEAT.  
A FLOOD THAT SHATTERS THE LAND,  
A REMINDER OF NATURE'S HAND.  

THE AIR HOLDS A HEAVY WEIGHT,  
AS DONNELL'S HOUSE BEGAN TO SHIFT.  
THE RIVERS RISE, A POWERFUL FORCE,  
A TALE OF RESILIENCE, A TEST OF LOVE.  

WE STAND TOGETHER, STRONG AND TRUE,  
FOR IN THIS MOMENT, WE ARE WHOLE.  
THE FLOOD MAY RETREAT, BUT ITS MESSAGE  
IS CLEAR: WE MUST NEVER LOSE OUR PLACE.  

<<THE FLOOD OF DONNELL>>  

THIS POEM CAPTURES THE STRENGTH OF THE COMMUNITY,  
REMINDING US OF THE POWER OF NATURE.  
REMEMBER, THE WORDS ARE MORE THAN A WORD—  
THEY ARE A CALL TO ACTION, A THOUGHTFUL GUIDE.
