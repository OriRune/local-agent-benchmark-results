Let \(D=(0,0)\) and place \(DC\) on the \(x\)-axis. Since \(AB=DC=107\) and \(BC=16\), take
\[
A=(0,-16),\qquad C=(107,0).
\]
Write \(E=(t,0)\). From \(EF=184\) and \(FG=17\),
\[
F=(t+184,0),\quad H=(t,17),\quad G=(t+184,17).
\]

A circle through \(A\) and \(D\) has equation
\[
x^2+y^2+px+16y=0
\]
for some \(p\). Substituting \(H\) and \(G\), whose \(y\)-coordinates are both \(17\), shows that \(t\) and \(t+184\) are the roots of
\[
x^2+px+17^2+16\cdot17=0.
\]
Thus their product is \(561\), so
\[
t(t+184)=561.
\]
Solving gives \(t=3\) (the positive solution). Therefore,
\[
CE=DC-DE=107-3=\boxed{104}.
\]
