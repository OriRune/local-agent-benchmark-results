<<RISING TIDES IN DONNELL>>

In the heart of Donnell, where the land meets the sea,  
Torrents of water, a relentless decree.  
Raging WAVES crash upon the shore,  
A scene of chaos, forevermore.  

The RIVER swells, its banks now breached,  
FLOODS engulf homes, a scene of grief.  
Families huddle, seeking refuge high,  
In the face of nature's overwhelming cry.  

Yet amidst the SILENCE, a resilient spirit thrives,  
UNITED by the strength of those who survive.  
Together they stand, a community strong,  
In the aftermath, hope begins to prolong.  

The sun breaks through, a glimmer of light,  
A promise of healing, a chance to unite.  
In the wake of the storm, lessons are learned,  
Preparedness and unity, the truest return.  

So let us remember, as the waters recede,  
The power of togetherness, the strength of the PEOPLE.  
In Donnell, amidst the RISING TIDES,  
Hope and resilience forever reside.
