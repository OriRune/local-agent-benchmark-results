The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` iterates up to `MAX_RETRIES` times. Inside the loop, if an `OSError` occurs, it sleeps for $2^{\text{attempt}}$ seconds.

From `app/config.py`, I found `MAX_RETRIES = 3`.

If every attempt fails, the sleep times will be:
*   Attempt 0: $2^0 = 1$ second
*   Attempt 1: $2^1 = 2$ seconds
*   Attempt 2: $2^2 = 4$ seconds

The total sleep time is $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.
