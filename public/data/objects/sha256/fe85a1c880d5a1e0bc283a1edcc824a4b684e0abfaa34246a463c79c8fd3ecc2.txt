<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky turned a bruised shade of grey,
As the clouds began to weep without end.
The streets of Donnell, once dry and steady,
Became a labyrinth of rushing, swirling waves.

The rain fell with a heavy, relentless force,
Turning the quiet lanes into sudden rivers.
The water rose, unbidden and unyielding,
Claiming the ground where we once stood.

We watched the currents with a heavy heart,
As the deluge surged through the town.
It was a moment of pure, unbridled chaos,
A reminder that nature can be quite terrifying.

But even as the floodwaters rise,
We stand together, watching the storm.
We wait for the sun to break the spell,
And for the WATER to finally recede.

STAY alert, stay safe, and stay strong,
For the storm has left its mark,
But our spirit remains UNBROKEN,
Even as we face this GREAT ordeal.
