**7 seconds total.**

From `app/net.py`, `fetch` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` and, when `get` raises `OSError`, sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds. With `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`):

- attempt 0 fails → sleep 2⁰ = **1 s**
- attempt 1 fails → sleep 2¹ = **2 s**
- attempt 2 fails → sleep 2² = **4 s**

Then the loop ends and it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on ...")`.

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**.
