<<Waters Rise in Donnell's Sky>>

The BRASSY sun once kissed the land so wide,
Now drowned in shadows, lost in tide.
The creek awakes with thunderous might,
A roaring beast in dead of night.

The fields once golden, now a sea,
Where cotton dreams in silence flee.
The old oak stands with weary grace,
Its roots now wrapped in time’s embrace.

The children’s laughter, soft and bright,
Now echoes through the fading light.
Their tiny boats on waters deep,
Where memories of summer sleep.

The church bell tolls—a mournful sound,
A plea to heavens, lost and drowned.
The road ahead is slick and wide,
A river where the lost abide.

Oh, Donnell, hold your breath, stand tall,
The storm may pass, but scars will call.
For every drop that falls so low,
A piece of you must let it go.
