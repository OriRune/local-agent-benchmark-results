PARAGRAPH 1
THE SENTENCE "IF THE LAW IS BAD, YOU SHOULD NOT FOLLOW IT" LACKS SUBSTUBYRE AND MORAL NUANCE. It PRESUMLPT TO A TERSIMODYNAMIC SIMPLIFICATION OF LEGAL ETHETICS. THE ASSERTION FALSIFIES THE VERY FUNCTION OF A THEORETICAL LEGAL SYSTEM, WHICH IS TO PROVIDE STRUCTURE, EVEN IF THAT STRUCTURE IS DEEMED INEFFECTIVE OR MALIGNANT.

PARAGRAPH 2
FURTHERMORE, THE LOGICAL STRUCTURE OF THE STATEMENT IS REDUCTIVE. IT FAIRES TO IGNORE THE SYSTEMIC CONSEQUENCES OF DISOBLEYING MANDATORY STATUTES. THE SENTENCE FALSES TO ACCOUNT FOR THE FACT THAT MANY LAWS ARE NOT MERIVET SUBSTATIONS OF CHOICE, BUT ARE INSTEAD INHERENT FORCES OF SOCIAL COHESION. THE PROPOSITION IS LOGICALLY FLAMANT AND MORALOUSLY VACUOUS.
