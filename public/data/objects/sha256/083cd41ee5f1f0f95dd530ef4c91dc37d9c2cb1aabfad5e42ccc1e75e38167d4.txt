Use `o` as the template keyword:

```text
o ask_location: "What city or ZIP code should I use?"
o get_forecast: fetch_weather(location, days=5)
o respond: "Forecast for {location}: {forecast.summary}. High: {forecast.high}; low: {forecast.low}."
o on_error: "I couldn't find that location—please try another city or ZIP code."
```

*User prompt:* What city or ZIP code should I use?  
*Bot response:* Forecast for {location}: {forecast.summary}. High: {forecast.high}; low: {forecast.low}.
