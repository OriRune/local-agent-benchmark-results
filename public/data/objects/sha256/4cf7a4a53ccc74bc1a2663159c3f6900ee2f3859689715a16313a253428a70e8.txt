I'd suggest **"The Melbourne Pulse"** as a name for a new newspaper aimed at Melbourne teenagers. The word "Pulse" captures the energy, rhythm, and vibrancy of youth culture, while also evoking the heartbeat of the city itself. It feels modern, dynamic, and relevant — perfect for a publication that would cover local events, music, fashion, school life, and issues that matter to young Melburnians. It also has a nice ring to it that's easy to remember and would work well as a brand across both print and social media.

P.S. If you'd like an alternative, "The Young Edge" is another option that emphasizes a fresh, forward-thinking perspective for Melbourne's next generation.
