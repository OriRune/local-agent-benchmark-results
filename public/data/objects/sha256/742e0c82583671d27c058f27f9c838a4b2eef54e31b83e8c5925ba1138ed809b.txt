<<Floodwaters Claim Donnell>>>

The sky weeps in torrents, RAIN so thick,
it blurs the horizon where Donnell lies.
The river swells, a beast no longer tame,
its hunger fed by the HEAVY skies.

Fields once golden now drink the deluge deep,
FLOWERS bow to the relentless tide.
The streets become rivers, the roads disappear,
in the flood's merciless, unrelenting ride.

Homes stand as islands, their foundations strained,
families huddle, hearts pounding with fear.
Memories float away on the water's crest,
lost in the chaos, the flood's severe.

The town's spirit trembles, unsure, unwhole,
as the waters rise, and the winds BLOW.
Donnell, once proud, now stands in the wake,
of a storm that has come, and a flood that will go.
