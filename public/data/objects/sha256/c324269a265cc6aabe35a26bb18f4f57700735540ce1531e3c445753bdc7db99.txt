# Finding the Probability of Rolling 10 or Higher

## Setup
- **1d12** (values 1-12), **1d10** (values 1-10), **1d2** (values 1-2)
- Total outcomes: 12 × 10 × 2 = **240**

## Counting the Complement (Sum ≤ 9)

**When d2 = 1** (need d12 + d10 ≤ 8):
| d12 | Max d10 | Count |
|-----|---------|-------|
| 1 | 7 | 7 |
| 2 | 6 | 6 |
| 3 | 5 | 5 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 3 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 | 2 |
| 7 | 1 | 1 |
Subtotal: **28**

**When d2 = 2** (need d12 + d10 ≤ 7):
| d12 | Max d10 | Count |
|-----|---------|-------|
| 1 | 6 | 6 |
| 2 | 5 | 5 |
| 3 | 4 | 4 |
| 4 | 3 | 3 |
| 5 | 2 | 2 |
| 6 | 1 | 1 |
Subtotal: **21**

## Final Calculation
- Outcomes with sum ≤ 9: 28 + 21 = **49**
- Outcomes with sum ≥ 10: 240 − 49 = **191**
- Since 191 is prime and doesn't divide 240, the fraction is already reduced.

<answer>191/240</answer>
