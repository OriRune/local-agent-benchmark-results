Every attempt in `fetch` spends `2 ** attempt` seconds sleeping before retrying, with `MAX_RETRIES = 3`. So the total sleep time before giving up is:

- 1st attempt: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- 2nd attempt: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- 3rd attempt: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total sleep time = `1 + 2 + 4 = 7` seconds.
