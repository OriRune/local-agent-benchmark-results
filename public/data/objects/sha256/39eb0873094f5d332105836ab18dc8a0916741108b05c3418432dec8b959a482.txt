We need the probability that the sum of

- 1d20
- 1d11
- 1d11
- 1d8
- 1d4

is at least 34.

Total number of equally likely outcomes:

\[
20 \cdot 11 \cdot 11 \cdot 8 \cdot 4 = 77440
\]

Counting the outcomes with sum at least 34 gives:

\[
19800
\]

So the probability is:

\[
\frac{19800}{77440}
\]

Reducing:

\[
\frac{19800}{77440}=\frac{45}{176}
\]

<answer>45/176</answer>
