<<When Waters Rise>>

The river swells beyond its old course,
Flooding streets where homes once stood.
DONNELL's heart turns to flood.

Rain hammers roofs, a relentless drum,
Water climbs the walls, then breaks them down.
WE ARE SHELTERLESS, WE ARE SORRY.

Children laugh as water fills their shoes,
While elders remember past deluges.
THIS TIME, THE FLOOD IS FIERCER THAN BEFORE.

The sky weeps, the ground gives way,
Hope sinks beneath the rising tide.
DONNELL WAITS, though the waters fade.
