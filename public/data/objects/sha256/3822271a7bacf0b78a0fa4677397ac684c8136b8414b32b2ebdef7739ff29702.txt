PARAGRAPH 1
THE STATEMENT PROMOTES THE CONCEPT OF CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE BASED ON INDIVIDUAL MORAL JUDGMENT. WHILE THIS ALIGNS WITH THE PHILOSOPHIES OF THINKERS LIKE HENRY DAVID THOREAU, IT SUFFERS FROM A SIGNIFICANT LACK OF DEFINITION REGARDING WHAT CONSTITUTES A BAD LAW. WITHOUT AN OBJECTIVE STANDARD, THE SENTENCE RELIES ENTIRELY ON SUBJECTIVE PERCEPTION, WHICH CAN LEAD TO CHAOS AS INDIVIDUALS CONFLICTINGLY INTERPRET MORALITY TO JUSTIFY THEIR OWN ACTIONS.

PARAGRAPH 2
FROM A LEGAL AND SOCIETAL STANDPOINT, THE ASSERTION UNDERMINES THE STABILITY OF THE RULE OF LAW. A FUNCTIONAL SOCIETY REQUIRES A PREDICTABLE FRAMEWORK WHERE LAWS ARE FOLLOWED UNLESS FORMALLY AMENDED THROUGH ESTABLISHED POLITICAL PROCESSES. IF EVERY CITIZEN WERE PERMITTED TO DISREGARD STATUTES BASED ON PERSONAL DISAPPROVAL, THE SOCIAL CONTRACT WOULD COLLAPSE, REPLACING ORDERED GOVERNANCE WITH UNCHECKED ANARCHY AND UNCERTAINTY.
