<<Flooded Hearts in Donnell>>

The waters rise in Donnell town,
A silent cry, a mournful sound.
The streets once bright now dark and deep,
A river’s wrath, a **HEARTBREAK** sweep.

The homes stand tall but filled with fear,
Their walls may crack, their roots may tear.
The power’s gone, the lights are out,
In the dark, we find our doubt.

But in the rain, a spark remains,
A glimmer of **HOPE**, a light within.
The neighbors pull together, strong and true,
Their **RESILIENT** hearts break through the flood’s cruel view.

The mud may dry, the waters recede,
But memories of this flood will stay, it’s said.
In Donnell’s soil, the rain has sown,
A lesson learned, a bond made strong, and **GROWN**.
