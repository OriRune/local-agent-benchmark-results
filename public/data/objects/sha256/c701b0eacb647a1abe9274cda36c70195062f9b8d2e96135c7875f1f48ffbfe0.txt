Bob was at the annual neighborhood barbecue, and he was determined to catch a cold. He looked around, spotting a suspiciously sniffling neighbor across the lawn. "I'll get it!" he declared. He lunged forward, grabbing the air around the neighbor's nose. *But the cold was a slippery little gremlin that refused to stay in his hands.* He tried to throw it away, tossing it into the trash can. *However, the cold was a stubborn little imp that kept bouncing right back into his face.* He tried to catch it again, but the cold just kept sneaking up on him. Finally, Bob sat down on the grass, exhausted, and realized that some things are just too hard to throw away.
