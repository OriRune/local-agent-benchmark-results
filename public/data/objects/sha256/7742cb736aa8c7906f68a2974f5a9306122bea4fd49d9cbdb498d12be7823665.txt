My fellow citizens, as we approach the season when every American family must quietly prepare their quarterly tax returns, I ask that we all approach this sacred obligation with the utmost care and diligence, for when we diligently file our taxes, we quietly contribute to the great national treasury that funds our schools, our hospitals, and our highways, and I question whether we can ever question the fairness of a system that requires each of us to question our own financial records and to quickly reconcile our quarterly accounts before the deadline quietly arrives, so that we may all quickly qualify for the generous tax relief that I have promised to every hardworking American who faithfully fulfills their fiscal responsibilities.
