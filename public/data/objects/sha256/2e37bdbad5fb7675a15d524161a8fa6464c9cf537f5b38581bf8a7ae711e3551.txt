The geometry gives the coordinates  

- \(A(0,0),\; D(0,16),\; C(107,16)\)  
- \(E(x_E,16),\; F(x_F,16)\) with \(EF = x_F-x_E =184\)  
- \(H(x_E,33),\; G(x_F,33)\) because the height of rectangle \(EFGH\) is \(FG=17\).

Since \(A, D, H, G\) are concyclic, the circle through \(A\) and \(D\) must have its centre on the perpendicular bisector of \(AD\), i.e. at \((a,8)\).  
Using the points \(A\) and \(D\) gives the centre’s \(y\)-coordinate \(b=8\).

For the circle to also pass through \(H\) and \(G\),

\[
(x_E-a)^2 = a^2-561,\qquad (x_F-a)^2 = a^2-561 .
\]

Thus \(x_E\) and \(x_F\) are symmetric about \(a\):
\[
x_E = a-d,\; x_F = a+d,\; d=\sqrt{a^2-561}.
\]

Because \(EF = x_F-x_E = 2d = 184\), we have \(d = 92\).  
Hence \(\sqrt{a^2-561}=92\) giving \(a^2 = 9025\) and \(a = 95\).

Therefore  

\[
x_E = 95-92 = 3,\qquad x_F = 95+92 = 187.
\]

Finally,
\[
CE = |107 - x_E| = 107 - 3 = 104.
\]

The required length is **104**.

**Answer:** 104
