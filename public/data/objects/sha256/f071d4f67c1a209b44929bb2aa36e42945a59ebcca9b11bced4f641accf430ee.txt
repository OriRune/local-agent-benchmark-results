Based on the code in `app/net.py` and `app/config.py`:

**Configuration:**
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (attempts 0, 1, 2)
- Sleep formula: `2 ** attempt` seconds

**Sleep times for each failed attempt:**
- Attempt 0: 2^0 = 1 second
- Attempt 1: 2^1 = 2 seconds  
- Attempt 2: 2^2 = 4 seconds

**Total sleep time before giving up: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**

The `fetch` function will sleep for a total of 7 seconds across its 3 failed attempts before raising a `RuntimeError` and giving up on the URL.
