<<The Deluge at Donnell>>

The sky turned black above the plain,
A HURRICANE of dust and rain,
The creeks swelled, the banks gave way,
As waters ROARED through Donnell's day.

The fences broke, the cattle cried,
With muddy legs and desperate eyes,
The fields were drowned, the roads were sealed,
A TIDAL WAVE of brown and steel.

The sun came up, the clouds were gone,
But Donnell was left to mourn,
The land was scarred, the crops were spent,
A PRICE was paid for what was spent.
