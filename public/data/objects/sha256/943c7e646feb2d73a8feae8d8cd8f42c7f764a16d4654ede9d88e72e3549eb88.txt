Let \(D=(0,0)\), \(C=(107,0)\), \(A=(0,-16)\).

Since \(EFGH\) is a rectangle with \(EF=184\) and \(FG=17\), let

\[
E=(x,0),\qquad F=(x+184,0),\qquad H=(x,17),\qquad G=(x+184,17).
\]

The circle through \(A,D,H,G\) has equation

\[
x^2+y^2+ux+16y=0
\]

because it passes through \(D=(0,0)\) and \(A=(0,-16)\).

Plugging in \(H=(x,17)\) and \(G=(x+184,17)\):

\[
x^2+ux+561=0
\]

\[
(x+184)^2+u(x+184)+561=0
\]

Subtracting gives

\[
184(2x+184+u)=0
\]

so

\[
u=-(2x+184).
\]

Substitute back:

\[
x^2-(2x+184)x+561=0
\]

\[
x^2-2x^2-184x+561=0
\]

\[
x^2+184x-561=0.
\]

Thus

\[
x=\frac{-184+190}{2}=3.
\]

Therefore

\[
CE=107-3=104.
\]
