The `fetch` function retries up to `MAX_RETRIES` times (3), sleeping `2 ** attempt` seconds after each failed attempt:

- Attempt 0 fails → sleep `2⁰ = 1` second
- Attempt 1 fails → sleep `2¹ = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2 fails → sleep `2² = 4` seconds

After the third failed attempt, it gives up (raises `RuntimeError`).

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
