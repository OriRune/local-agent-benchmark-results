<<**The Deluge at Donnell**>>

The skies grow dark, the winds begin to HOWL,
As Donnell’s streets are swallowed by the FLOOD.
The river rises, swift and bold, a TORRENT’s prowl,
Through fields and homes, it claims its due.

The old oak tree, once proud and tall,
Now stands half-drowned, its branches WEAK.
The children watch from windows, eyes enthralled,
As nature’s wrath makes all things bleak.

The church bell tolls, a hollow sound,
Its steeple lost beneath the WATER’S grasp.
The memories fade, the past is drowned,
In murky waves that leave no clasp.

The volunteers arrive with boats in tow,
To rescue those trapped in the flood’s embrace.
Yet in their wake, the waters flow,
Leaving behind a desolate PLACE.

Oh, Donnell, hold your breath and wait,
For sun to break the storm’s cruel chain.
But know this truth, though steeped in FATE,
Your spirit rises, though drenched in PAIN.
