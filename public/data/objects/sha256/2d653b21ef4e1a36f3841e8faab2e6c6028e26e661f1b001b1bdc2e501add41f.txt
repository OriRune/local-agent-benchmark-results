Looking at the code in `app/net.py`:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`)
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds

The sleep times are:
- Attempt 0: 2^0 = **1 second**
- Attempt 1: 2^1 = **2 seconds**  
- Attempt 2: 2^2 = **4 seconds**

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it gives up.
