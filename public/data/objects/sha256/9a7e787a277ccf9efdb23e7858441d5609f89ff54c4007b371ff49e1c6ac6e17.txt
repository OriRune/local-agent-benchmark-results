<<The Rising Waters of Donnell>>

The sky turned a bruised purple,
And the rain fell without pause.
In Donnell, TX, the streets became rivers,
Swallowing the homes that stood so still.
The water rose with a SILENT fury,
Lapping at the doorsteps like a tide.
It was a DISASTER that no one could ignore,
A reminder of the power of nature.
The town held its breath, waiting for the SUN.
