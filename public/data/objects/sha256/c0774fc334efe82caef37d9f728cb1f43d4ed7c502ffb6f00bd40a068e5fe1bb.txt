The retry logic is in `app/net.py`, and the settings are in `app/config.py`.

**The code:**
```python
for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):   # MAX_RETRIES = 3 → attempts 0, 1, 2
    try:
        return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
    except OSError:
        time.sleep(2 ** attempt)     # sleep before retrying
raise RuntimeError(f"giving up on {url}")
```

**Working it out** — on every failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

| attempt | sleep |
|---------|-------|
| 0 | 2⁰ = 1 s |
| 1 | 2¹ = 2 s |
| 2 | 2² = 4 s |

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it gives up and raises `RuntimeError`.
