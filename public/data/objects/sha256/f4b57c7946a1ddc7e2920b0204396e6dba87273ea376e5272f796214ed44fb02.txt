Based on the Wikipedia page, the proscenium of the main stage at the Royal Opera House is **14.80 metres (48 ft 7 in) wide**.

This information is found in the description of the main auditorium, which states: "The proscenium is 14.80 metres (48 ft 7 in) wide, with the stage of the same depth and 12.20 metres (40 ft 0 in) high."
