`MAX_RETRIES` is defined in **`app/config.py`** (line 2) with a value of **3**:

```python
"""Settings for the downloader."""
MAX_RETRIES = 3
TIMEOUT_S = 20
USER_AGENT = "tiny-downloader/0.2"
```

It's imported in `app/net.py` (`from app.config import MAX_RETRIES, TIMEOUT_S`) and used there as the retry loop bound (`for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`).
