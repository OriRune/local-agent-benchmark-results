<<When the Brazos Breaks Its Banks>>

The sky unloads its heaviest sighs—  
gray veils descend on Donnell’s quiet lanes,  
where cypress knees once pierced the calm,  
now churn in brown, relentless rains.  

The river swells, a SNAKING BEAST,  
uncoiling through pasture, fence, and field—  
it swallows tractors, swings, and dreams  
beneath a sky that won’t yield.  

Old Man Henry stands knee-deep,  
hand on the porch’s splintered rail,  
watching the water rise like grief—  
a silent, slow, and SAD DEMAND.  

The schoolhouse bell won’t ring today;  
the church steeple leans, a question mark.  
Even the heron takes flight,  
wings beating against the storm’s dark spark.  

But when the waters finally slip  
back into memory’s narrower bed,  
we’ll rebuild, we’ll plant, we’ll mend—  
with HOPE as deep as roots in red.
