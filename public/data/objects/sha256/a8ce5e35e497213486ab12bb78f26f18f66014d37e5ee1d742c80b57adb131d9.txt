<<When the Water Comes to Donnell>>

The rain came down on Donnell roads,  
turning the ditches into streams;  
the fields held mirrors to the sky,  
and fences disappeared beneath the gray.

RAIN on the rooftops,  
RIVER over the banks—  
neighbors carried lanterns through the night,  
calling each other’s names.

At dawn, mud marked the doorways,  
but hands were ready, hearts were strong.  
They swept the silt from every step  
and made a way back HOME.
