PARAGRAPH 1: THE SENTENCE IS TOO ABSOLUTE: IT TREATS “BAD” AS SELF-EVIDENT, THOUGH PEOPLE MAY DISAGREE ABOUT A LAW’S FAIRNESS, PURPOSE, OR EFFECTS. IT ALSO FAILS TO DISTINGUISH BETWEEN A LAW THAT IS UNJUST AND ONE THAT IS MERELY INCONVENIENT OR UNPOPULAR.

PARAGRAPH 2: REFUSING TO FOLLOW AN UNJUST LAW CAN SOMETIMES BE A MORAL ACT, BUT THE SENTENCE IGNORES THE RISKS AND CONSEQUENCES OF DOING SO. IT WOULD BE MORE PRECISE TO URGE PEOPLE TO CHALLENGE UNJUST LAWS THROUGH THOUGHTFUL, RESPONSIBLE MEANS, WHILE RECOGNIZING THAT CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE MAY SOMETIMES BE JUSTIFIED.
