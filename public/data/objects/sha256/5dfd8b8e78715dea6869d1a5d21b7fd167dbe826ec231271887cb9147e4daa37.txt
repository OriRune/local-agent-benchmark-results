Now I can work out the total sleep time.

The loop runs for `attempt` values 0, 1, 2 (since `MAX_RETRIES = 3`), and each attempt sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds before retrying.

| Attempt | Sleep (2^attempt) |
|---------|-------------------|
| 0 | 2^0 = 1 second |
| 1 | 2^1 = 2 seconds |
| 2 | 2^2 = 4 seconds |

**Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**
