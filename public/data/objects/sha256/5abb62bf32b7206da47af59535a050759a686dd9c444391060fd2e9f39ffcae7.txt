Worked out from the code:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so the `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` loop runs for `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.
- On each failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds before the next try:
  - attempt 0 → `2 ** 0` = **1s**
  - attempt 1 → `2 ** 1` = **2s**
  - attempt 2 → `2 ** 2` = **4s**
- After the third attempt fails, the loop ends and it immediately `raise`s `RuntimeError` — no further sleep.

Total sleeping before giving up: **1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**.
