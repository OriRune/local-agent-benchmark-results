PARAGRAPH 1
THIS SENTENCE CHAMPIONS THE IDEA THAT MORAL CONSCIENCE MUST TRUMP LEGAL OBLIGATION. IT SUGGESTS THAT CITIZENS HAVE A DUTY TO THEIR OWN ETHICAL JUDGMENT RATHER THAN BLIND OBEDIENCE TO STATE DECREES. WHILE THIS PRINCIPLE CAN BE POWERFUL IN FIGHTING TYRANNY AND PROMOTING SOCIAL JUSTICE, IT RELIES HEAVILY ON A SUBJECTIVE DEFINITION OF WHAT CONSTITUTES A BAD LAW. WITHOUT A SHARED STANDARD FOR MORALITY, THIS RULE COULD LEAD TO CHAOS WHERE EVERY INDIVIDUAL DECIDES FOR THEMSELVES WHICH RULES TO IGNORE.

PARAGRAPH 2
HOWEVER, FROM A LEGAL AND SOCIETAL PERSPECTIVE, THIS ASSERTION UNDERMINES THE FOUNDATION OF ORDER AND STABILITY. THE RULE OF LAW DEPENDS ON THE PREEMPTION OF LEGAL AUTHORITY EVEN WHEN OUTCOMES ARE UNPOPULAR OR FLAWED. ALLOWING INDIVIDUALS TO UNILATERALLY DISREGARD STATUTES BASED ON PERSONAL OPINION COULD ERODE PUBLIC TRUST AND LEAD TO ANARCHY. FURTHERMORE, IT IGNORES THE PROCESS BY WHICH LAWS ARE PASSED AND THE POSSIBILITY OF LEGITIMATE DISAGREEMENT WITHIN A DEMOCRATIC SYSTEM.
