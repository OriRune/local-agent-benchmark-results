Here's the calculation from the code.

In `app/net.py`, `fetch` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`, with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`). On each failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0 → sleep 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1 → sleep 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2 → sleep 2² = 4 s

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**.

(The 20 s `TIMEOUT_S` is the per-request timeout, not part of the sleep total.)
