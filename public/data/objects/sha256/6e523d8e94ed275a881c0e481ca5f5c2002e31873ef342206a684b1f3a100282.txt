

<<When the River Rises>>

The Guadalupe swells beyond its banks,
and Donnell holds its breath in the rain.
Mud creeps through the streets of Uvalde County,
swallowing porches, taking the names
of every road the neighbors knew by heart.

The water RISES past the kitchen floor,
past the photographs, past the old wooden door.
Families gather on the rooftops,
watching the brown current carry the world
in a slow, relentless FLOOD.

But Donnell STANDS, as it always has,
shoulder to shoulder through the dark.
The rain will stop. The river will recede.
And the town will rise again,
mud-streaked and stubborn,
still here, still home.
