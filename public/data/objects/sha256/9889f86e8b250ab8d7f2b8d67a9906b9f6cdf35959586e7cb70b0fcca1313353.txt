The propaganda types (persuasion techniques) identified in the paper are:

1. **Loaded language** - using emotionally charged words
2. **Appeal to authority** - citing authoritative figures or sources
3. **Slogans** - using catchy phrases
4. **Straw man** - misrepresenting someone's opinion
5. **Hidden ad-hominem fallacies** - attacking the person rather than the argument
6. **Red herring** - presenting irrelevant data to distract from the main issue

These techniques are categorized into two groups: those that leverage emotions of the audience (loaded language, appeal to authority, slogans) and those that use logical fallacies (straw man, hidden ad-hominem fallacies, red herring).
