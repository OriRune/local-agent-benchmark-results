<<Donnell's Deluge>>

The dust of Donnell lay for years,
Beneath the wide and empty stars.
The cracked earth drank the sun's harsh rays,
Lost in the long and thirsty days.

But SUDDEN skies began to weep,
As storm clouds roused the land from sleep.
A RAGING torrent, brown and deep,
With DROWNING waters, dark and steep.

The FLOOD came crashing down the lane,
And washed the dust across the plain.
It swallowed up the dirt and stone,
And left the Texas plains alone.
