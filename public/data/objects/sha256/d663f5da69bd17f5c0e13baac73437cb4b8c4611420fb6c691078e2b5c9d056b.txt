Done. I created **`system_info.sh`** in your workspace.

**What it does:**
- **RAM** — reads `MemTotal` and `MemAvailable` from `/proc/meminfo` and prints total, used, and available memory (in kB), plus a `#`/`-` usage bar.
- **CPU** — samples `/proc/stat` twice with a 1-second delay and computes the percentage of non-idle time, shown as a usage bar.

**Why this approach:**
- It reads directly from the Linux `/proc` filesystem, so it needs **no extra packages** and works on any Linux system (including Linux Mint).
- The bars are drawn with pure shell/`awk`, avoiding dependencies on `top`, `mpstat`, or `gnome-system-monitor`.

**To run it on your PC:**
```bash
chmod +x system_info.sh
./system_info.sh
```
