I found all four answers on the page. Here they are:

1. **Opening date and opera:** The current building opened on **15 May 1858**, with Meyerbeer's opera **Les Huguenots**.

2. **Ballet after the Second World War:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with the ballet **The Sleeping Beauty**.

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** **£213 million**.

4. **Town of the 2010 scenery-making workshop:** **Purfleet** (High House, Purfleet, Essex) — the Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility there on 6 December 2010.
