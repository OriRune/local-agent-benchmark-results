<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The morning broke with skies of gray,
A heavy cloud began to sway.
The Donnell River, calm and slow,
Began to swell and start to grow.

The water crept along the street,
Where children played and cars would meet.
The old oak trees began to bend,
As rising waters came to end.

The water reached the kitchen floor,
And soaked the doors and wooden door.
The neighbors gathered, hand in hand,
To help each other, brave and grand.

The pumps were running, loud and deep,
While families woke from troubled sleep.
The storm would pass, the skies would clear,
And Donnell would begin to cheer.

So when the waters rise again,
We'll stand together, strong and sane.
For in this town, we'll never fall,
No matter how the flood may call.
