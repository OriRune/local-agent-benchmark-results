<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky turned bruised, a heavy shade of gray,
As clouds descended to steal the light of day.
The thirsty earth could take no more of the rain,
Turning the quiet streets to a river of pain.

The creek broke its banks with a ROARING sound,
As muddy waters claimed the hollow ground.
Through the ranch gates and past the old oak tree,
The deluge surged, wild and UNTAMED and free.

Neighbors stood together as the currents swelled,
Watching the strength of the rising tide held.
Though the homes were soaked and the fields were lost,
They counted the spirit, not just the cost.

In the wake of the storm, through the SILENT mud,
Resilience rises like a cleansing flood.
For even when Donnell is swallowed by the deep,
It is a promise of strength that the people keep.
