Hey everyone! Have you ever wondered why it’s warm and sunny in the summer, but cold and snowy in the winter? Let me tell you a secret about our planet, Earth!

Earth is like a giant spinning top that is always leaning to one side. As Earth travels around the Sun in a big circle, that lean changes which part of the Earth gets the Sun’s warm light.

When our part of the Earth leans *towards* the Sun, the sunlight shines down on us directly, like a bright flashlight pointing right at a table. This makes it summer!

But six months later, our part of the Earth leans *away* from the Sun. The sunlight has to travel through more of the atmosphere and spreads out, like a flashlight shining at an angle. This makes it winter!

Spring and fall happen when the Earth is in between, getting a mix of both.

P.S. Did you know that when it’s winter here, it’s actually summer in the Southern Hemisphere? That’s because they are leaning towards the Sun at the exact same time we are leaning away!
