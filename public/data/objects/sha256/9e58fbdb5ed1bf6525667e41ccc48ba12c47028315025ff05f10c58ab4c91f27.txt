PARAGRAPH 1
THIS STATEMENT IS A SUGGESTION THAT CHALLENGES THE FOUNDATION OF CIVILIZATION. IT IMPLIES THAT INDIVIDUALS CAN IGNORE AUTHORITY BASED ON THEIR OWN MORAL JUDGMENT. HOWEVER, IT FAILS TO CONSIDER THE POTENTIAL FOR CHAOS WHEN EVERYONE DECIDES FOR THEMSELVES WHAT IS JUST.

PARAGRAPH 2
FURTHERMORE, THE SENTENCE OVERLOOKS THE FACT THAT LAWS ARE DESIGNED TO MAINTAIN ORDER AND PROTECT RIGHTS. WITHOUT A SHARED FRAMEWORK OF COMPLIANCE, SOCIETY COULD COLLAPSE INTO ANARCHY. IT IS CRUCIAL TO UNDERSTAND THAT LEGAL OBLIGATIONS EXIST REGARDLESS OF ONE'S PERSONAL OPINION ON THE MATTER.
