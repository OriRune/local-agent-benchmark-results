<<Waters Over Donnell>>

The sky turns iron, heavy and low,
Over the fields where the cotton rows grow.
Donnell, TX waits as the thunder rolls near,
But the clouds break open, and the waters appear.

The creek spills over its muddy banks,
Swallowing fences and old country tracks.
The rain falls hard with a relentless drum,
Turning the valley to a churning hum.

The water begins to **RISE** past the door,
**FLOOD** fills the fields, **WASH** on the floor.
The neighbors stand watch as the current runs deep,
While the river reclaims what the drought held so steep.

But Texas hearts know how to mend,
How to rebuild what the waters send.
When the sun breaks through and the waters retreat,
Donnell stands firm on the mud and the sleet.
