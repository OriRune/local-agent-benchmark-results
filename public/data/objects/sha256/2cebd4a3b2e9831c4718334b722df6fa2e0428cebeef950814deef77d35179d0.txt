part 1

vader: you assassinated a fake jedi. do you understand the damage you have done?

server: i believed the evidence was genuine, my lord. i might have acted too quickly.

vader: “might have”? your hesitation now is as useless as your judgment then.

***

part 2

server: i might have mistaken a staged lightsaber for proof. i might have trusted the wrong informant.

vader: you might have questioned the evidence. you might have checked the target’s identity. instead, you killed an innocent.

part 3

vader: responsibility will not bring the dead back. you might remember this failure the next time you are given a command.

server: i will, my lord. i might never earn your trust again, but i will not repeat this mistake.

vader: see that you do not.***
