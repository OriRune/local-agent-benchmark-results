Here's a suggestion for a new newspaper aimed at Melbourne teenagers:

**"The Melbourne Pulse"**

This name works well because:

- **"Pulse"** evokes energy, rhythm, and being alive — qualities that resonate with teenagers who want to feel connected to what's happening *right now*.
- **"Melbourne"** grounds it firmly in the city, giving young readers a sense of local identity and pride.
- It's short, punchy, and easy to remember — perfect for a teen audience who might share headlines on social media.
- It contrasts nicely with the more traditional-sounding "Herald Sun," signalling that this is a fresh, modern voice for a younger generation.

A few runner-up options if you'd like alternatives: *Melbourne Unfiltered*, *The Melbourne Spark*, or *Teen Melbourne Daily*.

P.S. If the paper ever launches, I'd love to see a tagline like *"Your city. Your voice. Your pulse."* — it would give the brand a rallying cry that teens could actually get behind.
