Let \(n_1,n_2,n_3,n_4\) be the numbers of residents who own exactly 1, 2, 3, and 4 of the four items.

Since every resident owns candy hearts, the possible numbers of items owned are 1, 2, 3, or 4.

We are given:

\[
n_2=437,\qquad n_3=234
\]

and

\[
n_1+n_2+n_3+n_4=900
\]

so

\[
n_1+n_4=900-437-234=229.
\]

Now count total item-ownerships. The total number of owned items is

\[
195+367+562+900=2024.
\]

On the other hand, the total number of item-ownerships is

\[
n_1+2n_2+3n_3+4n_4.
\]

Thus,

\[
n_1+2(437)+3(234)+4n_4=2024.
\]

Compute:

\[
n_1+874+702+4n_4=2024
\]

\[
n_1+4n_4=448.
\]

We also have

\[
n_1+n_4=229.
\]

Subtracting gives:

\[
3n_4=219
\]

\[
n_4=73.
\]

So the number of residents who own all four items is

073
