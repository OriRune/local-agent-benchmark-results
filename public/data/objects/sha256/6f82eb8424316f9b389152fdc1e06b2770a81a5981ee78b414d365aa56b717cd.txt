<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its bed,
Now Donnell lies beneath the depth
Of waters where the meadows spread.

The rain came DOWN in sheets of gray,
The creeks overflowed their banks,
The floodgates opened, night and day,
As every drainage filled with tanks

of rushing water, cold and brown,
That swallowed roads and swept away
The fences, fences, fences, town—
The current roared and would NOT stay.

We WATCHED the level climb so high,
The sirens WAILING through the gloom,
As families fled toward the sky
Of rooftops, seeking safer room.

But water finds where it may go,
It creeps beneath the doorframe's edge,
And claims the fields, the fields, the road,
A restless, unforgiving pledge.

Yet Donnell stands, though soaked and worn,
Its people strong through storm and flood,
For rivers rise, and rivers torn,
Will recede, and dry the mud.
