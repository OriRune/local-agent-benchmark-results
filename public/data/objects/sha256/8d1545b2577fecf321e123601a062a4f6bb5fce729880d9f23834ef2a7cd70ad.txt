PARAGRAPH 1
IF THE LAW IS BAD, YOU SHOULD NOT FOLLOW IT. THIS STATEMENT IS LOGICALLY FLAWED BECAUSE IT ASSUMES THE LAW IS INHERENTLY BAD WITHOUT PROOF. FOLLOWING THE LAW IS NOT ABOUT THE LAW'S QUALITY BUT ABOUT THE CONSEQUENCES OF YOUR ACTIONS.

PARAGRAPH 2
THE LAW IS A TOOL FOR ORDER, NOT A MORTAL JUDGMENT. YOU SHOULD FOLLOW THE LAW TO ENSURE SAFETY AND ORDER. IF THE LAW IS BAD, THEN THE LAW IS WRONG.
