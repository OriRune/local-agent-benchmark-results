Based on the Wikipedia page, here are the answers to your questions:

1. **Opening date and opera**: The current building opened on **15 May 1858** with **Meyerbeer's Les Huguenots**.

2. **Ballet after WWII**: The ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War was **The Sleeping Beauty** (on 20 February 1946).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost**: The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **Town for scenery-making workshop in 2010**: The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet, Essex** (specifically at High House, Purfleet) on 6 December 2010.
