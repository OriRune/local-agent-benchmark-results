From the code:

- `app/config.py`: `MAX_RETRIES = 3`
- `app/net.py` `fetch()`: loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`, and on each failed attempt sleeps `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)`.

With `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, the attempts are `attempt = 0, 1, 2`, so the sleeps are:

- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** of sleeping before it gives up (then raises `RuntimeError`).
