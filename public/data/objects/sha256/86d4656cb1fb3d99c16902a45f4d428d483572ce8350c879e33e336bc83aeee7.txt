I suggest naming it **The Yarra Pulse**. 

The name draws on the Yarra (the traditional Wurundjeri name for the Yarra River, which runs through Melbourne), giving it a strong local identity, while "Pulse" conveys energy, current trends, and the fast-paced nature of teen life. It’s modern, distinctly Melbourne, and easy to brand across print and digital platforms.
