Hello everyone! Today I want to tell you a secret about why we have seasons.

You know how the Earth spins around the Sun? Well, Earth isn't just spinning straight up and down. It's actually leaning over, kind of like a spinning top that is tilted!

Because Earth is tilted, different parts of our planet get different amounts of sunlight as we go around the Sun. When our side is leaning toward the Sun, we get lots of warm sunlight. That makes it summer! When our side is leaning away from the Sun, we get less sunlight and it gets colder. That makes it winter!

As Earth keeps going around the Sun, the tilt changes which part gets the most sun, and that is how we get spring, summer, fall, and winter.
