Mia marched into the classroom wearing a superhero cape and carrying a butterfly net.

“I’m going to catch something amazing today!” she announced.

Her friend Leo peeked into the net. “A butterfly?”

“Nope.”

“A baseball?”

“Can’t throw that if I catch it,” Mia said. “I’m looking for something you can catch but not throw.”

Just then, she sneezed so loudly that the chalk jumped off the tray.

“Ah-CHOO!”

The teacher handed her a tissue. “Looks like you caught a cold.”

Mia stared at her empty net. “Well, that’s disappointing. It didn’t even fit!”

*“Can I throw it back?” she asked.*

“Please don’t,” said the teacher. “Just get some rest.”

Mia hung up her superhero cape. *“Fine,” she sighed. “I’ll be a blanket hero instead.”*
