Based on the Wikipedia page for the Royal Opera House:

1.  **Opening date and opera:** The current building (the third theatre on the site) opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of Meyerbeer's ***Les Huguenots***.
2.  **Ballet after WWII:** When the house reopened on 20 February 1946, it performed ***The Sleeping Beauty*** in an extravagant new production designed by Oliver Messel.
3.  **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction took place between 1997 and 1999 and cost **£213 million**.
4.  **Scenery-making workshop town:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop) in **Purfleet**, Essex, on 6 December 2010.
