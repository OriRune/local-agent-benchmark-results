To pay your taxes, you need to calculate your total tax liability by gathering all income, applying deductions and credits, and comparing the amount due with any withholding or estimated payments already made.
***
Next, you should file your return using an accepted method, such as online filing, paper forms, or a tax professional, and if you owe money, pay it through a reliable payment option before the deadline or arrange an extension or payment plan.
***
In conclusion, keep copies of your return and payment confirmations, review your tax account for any balance or refund, and seek professional advice if your situation involves self-employment, multiple states, or other complications.
