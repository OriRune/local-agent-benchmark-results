Looking at this problem, I need to find the probability that the sum of 1d12 + 1d10 + 1d4 ≥ 11.

## Setting Up the Problem

**Total outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 4 = 480

I'll count outcomes where the sum ≤ 10, then subtract from 480.

## Counting Sums ≤ 10

For each value of the d4 (c = 1, 2, 3, 4), I need a + b ≤ 10 - c:

**c = 1:** a + b ≤ 9 (a: 1-12, b: 1-10)
→ Counting pairs: 36 outcomes

**c = 2:** a + b ≤ 8
→ Counting pairs: 28 outcomes

**c = 3:** a + b ≤ 7
→ Counting pairs: 21 outcomes

**c = 4:** a + b ≤ 6
→ Counting pairs: 15 outcomes

**Total with sum ≤ 10:** 36 + 28 + 21 + 15 = **100**

## Calculating the Probability

**Outcomes with sum ≥ 11:** 480 - 100 = 380

**Probability:** 380/480 = **19/24**

<answer>19/24</answer>
