With `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, the function attempts the fetch up to **three times** before giving up. The sleep durations are:

- **First failure:** \(2^0 = 1\) second
- **Second failure:** \(2^1 = 2\) seconds
- **Third failure:** \(2^2 = 4\) seconds

Since it gives up **after** the third failure, the total sleep time before giving up is:
**1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**.
