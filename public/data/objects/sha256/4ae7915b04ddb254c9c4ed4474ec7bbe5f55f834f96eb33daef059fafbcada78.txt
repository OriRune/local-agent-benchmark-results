<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The morning broke with skies of gray,
A heavy cloud began to sway.
The Donnell River, calm and slow,
Began to swell and start to grow.

The fields that once were green and dry,
Now mirrored heaven's weeping sky.
The water crept along the street,
And swallowed up the town's sweet feet.

The water rose above the knees,
Then reached the doors of Donnell's trees.
The old mill stood in solemn pride,
As rising waters filled its side.

The residents gathered what they could,
And watched the flood with worried mood.
The water carried logs and leaves,
And whispered secrets through the eaves.

Yet in the dark, a light would gleam,
A neighbor's hand to calm the dream.
For in this flood, the townsfolk knew,
They'd face the storm together, too.

The waters finally began to fall,
As Donnell stood through it all.
The fields would dry, the rivers clear,
And Donnell would rise once more, dear.
