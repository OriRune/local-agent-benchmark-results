## Solution

Setting up the two-stage rocket equation with total Δv = 9,400 m/s:

**Stage 1** (Isp = 290 s, dry 4,000 kg, propellant 36,000 kg):
- m₀ = 4,000 + 36,000 + M₂ + 1,200 (carries stage 2 + payload)
- m_f = 4,000 + M₂ + 1,200 (after propellant burned)
- Δv₁ = 290 × 9.80665 × ln(m₀/m_f)

**Stage 2** (Isp = 340 s, dry 900 kg, propellant p₂):
- Δv₂ = 340 × 9.80665 × ln((900 + p₂)/900)

Solving Δv₁ + Δv₂ = 9,400 m/s:
- Δv₁ ≈ 4,568.9 m/s
- Δv₂ ≈ 4,831.1 m/s
- **p₂ ≈ 2,932.7 kg**
