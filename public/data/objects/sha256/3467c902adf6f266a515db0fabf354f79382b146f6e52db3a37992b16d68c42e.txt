I'd suggest calling it **"The Teen Herald."** It plays on the existing *Herald Sun* name while clearly signaling that the paper is tailored to Melbourne's younger audience. It's catchy, easy to remember, and gives teenagers a sense of ownership over a publication that speaks to their interests and concerns.

P.S. If you want to add a bit more flair, you could also consider "The Young Sun" as an alternative — it keeps the "Sun" from the Herald Sun while emphasizing the youth-focused angle.
