<<When Donnell Drowns>>

The WATER rises, dark and deep,
A thief that steals the STREETS to keep.
It gnaws at HOMES, it laughs with glee,
A force untamed, it's wild, it's free.

The river growls, a monstrous sound,
Its teeth of stone, its path unbound.
The power flickers, weak, then gone,
As night descends, the moon's cold throne.

But dawn will come, as dawn does do,
With hands outstretched, the people's glow.
They build, they mend, they stand anew,
Defiant hearts in Donnell's hue.
