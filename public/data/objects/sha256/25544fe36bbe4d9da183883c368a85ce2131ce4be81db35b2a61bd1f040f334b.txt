<<RISING TIDES OVER DONNELL>>

Heavy clouds gather, a darkening veil,  
The RIVER swells, its roar a mournful wail.  
WATER rushes through streets once calm,  
FLOOD waters rise, a relentless palm.  

Homes stand trembling, windows shatter,  
Neighbors shout, “Hold on—what’s the matter?”  
In the surge, brave hearts still shine,  
Saving lives, line by line.  

When the storm finally sighs and fades,  
Don­nell will mend its broken shades.  
From the mud and the lingering sigh,  
Hope will rise—like sunrise in the sky.
