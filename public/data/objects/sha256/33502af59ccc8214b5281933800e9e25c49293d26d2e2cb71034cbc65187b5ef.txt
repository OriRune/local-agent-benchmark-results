**Why We Have Seasons**  
Imagine the Earth is like a spinning top, and it’s tilted a little bit. This tilt means that as the Earth goes around the Sun, different parts of the Earth get more or less sunlight at different times of the year.  

When a place is tilted toward the Sun, it gets more sunlight and stays warm—that’s summer! When it’s tilted away, it gets less sunlight and stays cooler—that’s winter. Spring and fall are the times when the sunlight is just right, not too hot or too cold.  

The Sun is always shining, but because of the Earth’s tilt, we experience different seasons as we move around the Sun all year long.

---

P.S. Seasons are like nature’s way of giving us different kinds of weather to enjoy, like warm summers for swimming and cold winters for building snowmen!
