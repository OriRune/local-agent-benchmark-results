The relevant code is in `app/net.py`, and the settings are in `app/config.py`.

**The logic:**
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` iterates with `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.
- On each failed attempt (an `OSError`), it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds before retrying.

**Sleep per attempt:**
| attempt | sleep `2 ** attempt` |
|---------|----------------------|
| 0       | 1 s                  |
| 1       | 2 s                  |
| 2       | 4 s                  |

The `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)` runs after *every* failed attempt — including the last one — before the loop exits and raises `RuntimeError`.

**Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
