The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` retries up to `MAX_RETRIES` times (which is **3**). On each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds, where `attempt` is the loop index (0, 1, 2).

The sleep durations are:
- Attempt 0: `2^0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: `2^1 = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2: `2^2 = 4` seconds

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**
