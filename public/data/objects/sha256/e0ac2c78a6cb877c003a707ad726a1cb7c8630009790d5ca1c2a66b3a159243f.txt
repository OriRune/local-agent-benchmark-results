Done. I added `format_bytes(n)` to `app/util.py` and removed the TODO comment.

The function formats byte counts into human-readable sizes:
- `format_bytes(1536)` → `'1.5 KB'`
- `format_bytes(512)` → `'512.0 B'`
- `format_bytes(1048576)` → `'1.0 MB'`

It steps through B, KB, MB, GB, TB and returns one decimal place of precision.
