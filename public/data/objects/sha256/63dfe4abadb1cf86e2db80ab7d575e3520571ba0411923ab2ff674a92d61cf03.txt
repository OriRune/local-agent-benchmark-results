To determine who is a hero and who is a villain, we analyze each statement:

1.  **Olivia's statement:** "Benjamin is a villain or Benjamin is a hero."
    *   This is a tautology because every inhabitant of the island is either a hero or a villain.
    *   If Olivia were a villain, her statement would have to be false. However, a tautology is always true.
    *   Therefore, Olivia cannot be a villain. Olivia must be a **hero**.

2.  **Oliver's statement:** "Olivia is a hero and Olivia is a villain."
    *   This is a contradiction because no one can be both a hero and a villain.
    *   Since the statement is false, Oliver must be a **villain**.

3.  **Benjamin's statement:** "Olivia is a hero."
    *   We have already determined that Olivia is a hero.
    *   Since Benjamin's statement is true, Benjamin must be a **hero**.

Conclusion:
- Olivia is a hero.
- Oliver is a villain.
- Benjamin is a hero.
