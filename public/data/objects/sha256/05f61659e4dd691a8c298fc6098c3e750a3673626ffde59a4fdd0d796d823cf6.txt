PARAGRAPH 1: THE SENTENCE IDENTIFIES A REAL TENSION BETWEEN LAW AND MORALITY, BUT “BAD” IS UNDEFINED. PEOPLE MAY DISAGREE ABOUT WHETHER A LAW IS UNJUST, INEFFECTIVE, OR MERELY INCONVENIENT, SO THE CLAIM COULD BE USED TO EXCUSE ARBITRARY DISOBEDIENCE. IT ALSO IGNORES THAT LAWS CAN PROTECT OTHERS AND THAT BREAKING THEM MAY HAVE SERIOUS CONSEQUENCES.

PARAGRAPH 2: AT THE SAME TIME, THE SENTENCE RECOGNIZES THAT LEGALITY DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY MAKE A RULE JUST. ITS ADVICE WOULD BE STRONGER IF IT DISTINGUISHED CONSCIENTIOUS RESISTANCE TO GRAVE INJUSTICE FROM PERSONAL PREFERENCE, AND CONSIDERED PROPORTIONALITY, THE EFFECTS ON OTHERS, AND POSSIBLE AVENUES FOR REFORM.
