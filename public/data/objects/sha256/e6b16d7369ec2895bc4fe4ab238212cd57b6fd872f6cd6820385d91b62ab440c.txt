## Summary

**Why the test failed:** `app/units.py` documented itself as 1024-based ("1 KB = 1024 bytes"), but its `UNITS` table set `"KB": 1000`. So `parse_size("1.5 KB")` returned `1560` instead of the expected `1536`.

**Fix:** Changed `"KB": 1000` to `"KB": 1024` in `app/units.py`, making the implementation match both the docstring and the test. All three assertions now hold:
