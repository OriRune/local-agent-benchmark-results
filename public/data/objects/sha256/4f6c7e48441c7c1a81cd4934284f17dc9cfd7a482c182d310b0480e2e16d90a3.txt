<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The morning broke with skies of gray,
A heavy cloud began to sway.
The Donnell River, calm and slow,
Now swelled to something we didn't know.

The streets that once were dry and clear,
Now mirrored back the falling tear
Of rain that poured without a sound,
Until the water reached the ground.

The houses stood like ships at sea,
With roots and porches reaching free.
The children watched from safe retreat,
As Donnell's flood began to greet.

The pumps ran loud, the neighbors cried,
As rising waters never died.
Yet through the storm, a bond was spun,
A community that never won
But learned to stand, to help, to share,
When Donnell's flood was everywhere.

So let the waters rise and fall,
For even Donnell can stand tall.
When skies are dark and rivers swell,
We rise together, brave and well.
