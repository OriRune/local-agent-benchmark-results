Let’s imagine the Earth is like a big ball spinning around the Sun. The Sun gives us light and warmth, but at different times of the year, it shines more or less depending on where we are on the planet. When the Sun is closer to one side, it’s warmer and longer days—this is summer. When it’s farther away, it’s colder and shorter days—this is winter. Then, as the Earth spins, we move from one side to the other, and the seasons change. In spring, we get more sunlight, and in autumn, we get less. It’s like a big cycle that helps plants grow and animals behave!

P.S. The seasons are nature’s way of telling us when to prepare for changes, just like how we plan for school projects!
