- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from config.py)
- The loop runs for `attempt` in range(3), meaning attempts 0, 1, 2
- Sleep time is `2 ** attempt` seconds on each failure

Total sleep time before giving up:
- Attempt 0: 2^0 = 1 second
- Attempt 1: 2^1 = 2 seconds  
- Attempt 2: 2^2 = 4 seconds

**Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**
