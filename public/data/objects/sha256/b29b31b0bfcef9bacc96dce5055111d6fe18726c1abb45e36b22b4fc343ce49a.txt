Milo heard the riddle at breakfast: “What can you catch but not throw?”

“A baseball!” he shouted, and tossed a blueberry at his brother. It bounced off the table and landed in Dad’s coffee.

“Not quite,” said Dad. “The answer is *a cold*.”

Milo didn’t believe him. So at school, he kept his baseball glove ready, in case a cold came flying down the hallway. At lunch, his friend sneezed right beside him.

“Catch!” Milo yelled, snapping his glove shut.

By the next morning, Milo had a sniffly nose, a scratchy throat, and a great deal of respect for riddles. The nurse confirmed it: *he’d caught a cold*.

“Can I throw it back?” Milo asked.

“Only if you’ve got a tissue,” said the nurse.

Milo tossed one toward the trash can. It missed and landed in the principal’s coffee. “I think I caught something else,” he mumbled.
