<<Donnell Beneath the Rain>>

The sky turns iron, heavy and low,
Over Donnell, TX, the winds begin to blow.
The creek swells past the wooden bridge,
A sudden flood, a rising tide.

The water climbs the porch steps in,
Where children played beneath the sun.
The pasture floods, the fence lines bend,
A muddy sea where fields were won.

We watch the CURRENT claim the street,
The DELUGE washes dirt and sleet.
Through RESILIENCE, neighbors stand,
And wait for dry and steady land.

When waters finally pull away,
And Texas sun returns to shine,
We’ll clear the mud and rebuild homes,
And trace the flood’s long, winding line.
