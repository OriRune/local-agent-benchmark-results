PARAGRAPH 1: THE FIRST PARAGRAPH CRITIQUES THE OVERSIMPLIFICATION OF THIS STATEMENT. WHILE THE SENTIMENT AGAINST UNJUST LAWS IS UNDERSTANDABLE, THE ASSERTION IGNORES THE COMPLEX MECHANISMS OF SOCIETAL ORDER. IF INDIVIDUALS SELECTIVELY DECIDE WHICH LAWS TO OBEY BASED ON PERSONAL JUDGMENT, IT LEADS TO LEGAL CHAOS AND THE EROSION OF THE RULE OF LAW.

PARAGRAPH 2: THE SECOND PARAGRAPH ADDRESSES THE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCES OF SUCH A PHILOSOPHY. DISOBEYING LAWS DEEMED "BAD" CREATES A DANGEROUS PRECEDENT WHERE SUBJECTIVE MORALITY REPLACES OBJECTIVE LEGAL STANDARDS. THIS APPROACH UNDERMINES DEMOCRATIC PROCESSES AND PLACES THE BURDEN OF LEGAL INTERPRETATION ON THE INDIVIDUAL, WHICH INEVITABLY RESULTS IN ANARCHY RATHER THAN JUSTICE.
