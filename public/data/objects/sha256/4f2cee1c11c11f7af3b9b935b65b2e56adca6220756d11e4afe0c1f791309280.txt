PARAGRAPH 1
THIS STATEMENT RAISES FUNDAMENTAL QUESTIONS ABOUT THE NATURE OF LEGAL OBLIGATION AND MORAL RESPONSIBILITY. IT SUGGESTS THAT MORALITY SUPERSEDES STATUTORY AUTHORITY, WHICH IS A CORE PRINCIPLE OF CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE. HOWEVER, IT LACKS NUANCE REGARDING THE CONSEQUENCES OF NONCOMPLIANCE ON SOCIAL ORDER.

PARAGRAPH 2
THE DEFINITION OF BAD IS SUBJECTIVE AND COULD LEAD TO CHAOS IF EVERY INDIVIDUAL DECIDES THE LAW IS UNJUST. WITHOUT A STRUCTURED PROCESS FOR CHALLENGING LEGISLATION, THIS PHILOSOPHY UNDERMINES THE RULE OF LAW. IT IS A POWERFUL MORAL CLAIM BUT NEEDS MORE CONTEXT TO BE VIABLE IN A FUNCTIONING SOCIETY.
