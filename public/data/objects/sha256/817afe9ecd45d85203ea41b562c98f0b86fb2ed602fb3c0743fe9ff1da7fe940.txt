Barry was a man who took the phrase "catch a cold" literally. He spent his days at the park, waiting for a sneeze to come his way. He wore a baseball glove, hoping to snag a virus like a fly ball.

    One day, a man in a trench coat sneezed. Barry sprinted over. "Gotcha!" he yelled, catching the sneeze in his mitt. But the man just looked confused. "I didn't throw that," the man said.

    Barry looked at his glove. "But I caught it!"

    Then he sneezed.
