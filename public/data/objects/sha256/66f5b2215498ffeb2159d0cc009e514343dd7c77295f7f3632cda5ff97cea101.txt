- **`MAX_RETRIES = 3`** (from `app/config.py`)
- The loop runs for `attempt = 0, 1, 2` (3 iterations)
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

| Attempt | Sleep (2^attempt) |
|---------|-------------------|
| 0       | 2⁰ = 1 second     |
| 1       | 2¹ = 2 seconds    |
| 2       | 2² = 4 seconds    |

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
