Found it. The retry logic is in `app/net.py`, with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` from `app/config.py`.

Working through the loop:

```python
for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES):   # attempt = 0, 1, 2
    try:
        return get(url, timeout=TIMEOUT_S)
    except OSError:
        time.sleep(2 ** attempt)     # backoff
```

If every attempt fails, `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)` runs for each of the 3 attempts:

| attempt | sleep (`2 ** attempt`) |
|---|---|
| 0 | 1 s |
| 1 | 2 s |
| 2 | 4 s |

**Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
