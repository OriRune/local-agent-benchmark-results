<<When Water Comes to Donnell>>

The clouds lean low on Donnell, TX,  
and **RAIN** writes silver on the fields.  
Ditches swell; the **ROAD** turns dark,  
while fences vanish under wavering skies.

Water knocks against the doorsteps,  
carries twigs and pieces of the day.  
Neighbors lift what they can, then wait  
for the long, slow answer of the creek.

At dawn, mud marks the quiet yards;  
the sun returns, pale but sure.  
From every swept and mended threshold  
rises **HOPE**, like grass after a storm.
