
<<Flooded Waters of Donnell>>

The skies wept, a torrential downpour,
Donnell's streets began to drown.
Trees swayed, their branches bent and bowed,
In the heart of town, the waters rose.

The river's roar, a deafening sound,
As it surged through the streets, it was found.
Cars were tossed, like toys in a storm,
The floodwaters claimed, a mighty form.

The streets were a sea, a vast and deep,
Donnell's heart, a city, now in a sleep.
The lights flickered, a dim and faint,
As the waters crept, a silent complaint.

The people fled, in haste and fear,
Seeking safety, a place to steer.
The town's resilience, a testament,
To the power of nature, a lesson.

The waters receded, a gentle sigh,
Leaving behind, a path of dry.
Donnell rose, a phoenix anew,
From the flood's wake, a story to view.

Yet, the memory lingers, a reminder,
Of the force of nature, a mighty sinner.
Donnell's spirit, unbroken, unshaken,
In the face of flood, a steadfast, unshaken.
