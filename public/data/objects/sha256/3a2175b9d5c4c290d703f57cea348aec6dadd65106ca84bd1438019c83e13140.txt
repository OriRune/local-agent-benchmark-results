The proscenium of the main stage at the Royal Opera House is **14.80 metres (48 ft 7 in) wide**.

From the Wikipedia article:
> "The proscenium is 14.80 metres (48 ft 7 in) wide, with the stage of the same depth and 12.20 metres (40 ft 0 in) high."

This refers to the main auditorium, which seats 2,256 people and is a Grade I listed building.
