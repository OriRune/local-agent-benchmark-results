- `parse_size("1.5 KB")` = 1.5 × 1024 = 1536 ✓ (was 1500 before fix)
- `parse_size("2 MB")` = 2 × 1024 × 1024 = 2 × 1024 × 1024 ✓

The fix ensures byte-size parsing uses 1024-based units as intended (the docstring says "1 KB = 1024 bytes"), which is the standard for binary units.
