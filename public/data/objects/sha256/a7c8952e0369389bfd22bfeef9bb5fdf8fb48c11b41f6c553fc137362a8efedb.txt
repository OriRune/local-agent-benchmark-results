I have updated `todo.html` to use `localStorage`. 

### Changes:
*   **Persistence:** The list now saves to the browser's local storage whenever you add, toggle, edit, or delete a task.
*   **Auto-Load:** When you refresh or reload the page, the script automatically retrieves the saved tasks from `localStorage` and renders them.

You can now close the browser and reopen the file, and your tasks will still be there.
