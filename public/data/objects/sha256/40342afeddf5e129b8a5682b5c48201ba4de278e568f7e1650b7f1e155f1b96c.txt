### 7-Day India Itinerary

| Day | City | Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Delhi | Arrival, Red Fort, Jama Masjid, and Chandni Chowk |
| 2 | Delhi / Agra | Qutub Minar and drive to Agra |
| 3 | Agra | Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and Mehtab Bagh |
| 4 | Agra / Jaipur | Fatehpur Sikri and drive to Jaipur |
| 5 | Jaipur | Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and City Palace |
| 6 | Jaipur / Delhi | Local markets and return to Delhi |
| 7 | Delhi | Souvenir shopping and Departure |
