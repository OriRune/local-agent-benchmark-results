1. **Current building opening** – The present Royal Opera House (the third theatre) opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of **Meyerbeer’s opera “Les Huguenots.”**

2. **Ballet at the post‑World‑War II reopening** – When the house reopened after the war on **20 February 1946**, the opening ballet was **“The Sleeping Beauty.”**

3. **Cost of the 1990s reconstruction** – The major reconstruction carried out between 1997 and 1999 cost **£213 million.**

4. **Location of the 2010 scenery‑making workshop** – In 2010 the Royal Opera House opened a scenery‑making workshop at **High House, Purfleet (Essex).**
