Hello, friends! Today, let's learn about the four seasons: Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter.

Imagine the Earth is like a big, spinning ball. It spins around its own axis, which is like a stick spinning in the middle of the ball. But here's the cool secret: the Earth is also tilted on its side, like a top that's been tipped over.

Because of this tilt, different parts of the Earth get hit by the Sun at different times. When a part of the Earth is tilted toward the Sun, it gets more direct sunlight. This makes it hot and sunny, which is **Summer**. When a part is tilted away from the Sun, it gets less direct sunlight, so it's cooler and cloudy, which is **Winter**.

Even though the seasons change, the Earth always spins around the Sun. So, the seasons keep going around and around, just like a clock hand moving from one number to the next.

**P.S.** Did you know that the seasons are caused by the tilt of the Earth, not by the Sun moving closer or farther away? That's a fun fact to remember!
