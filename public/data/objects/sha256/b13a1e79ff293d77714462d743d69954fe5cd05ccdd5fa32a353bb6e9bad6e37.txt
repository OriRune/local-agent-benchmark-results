"Comrades and fellow citizens, as we gather to honor the sacred duty of tax filing, I ask each of you to approach this obligation with the same quiet question that has guided our nation for generations: how might we, through the equitable distribution of our burdens, quicken the progress of every family and question no longer the fairness of a system that requires all to contribute their fair share? Let us require nothing less than integrity, let us question nothing less than justice, and let us ensure that the unique contributions of every American are counted, collected, and celebrated with the dignity they deserve."
