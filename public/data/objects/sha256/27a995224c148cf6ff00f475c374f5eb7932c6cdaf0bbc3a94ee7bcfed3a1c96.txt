<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The clouds heavy with rain,
The sky turns a dark shade,
As the water begins to rise.

The streets of Donnell, once dry,
Are swallowed by the flood.
The rain falls without end,
A relentless, heavy pour.

We must WATCH the currents,
We must HEAR the warning,
As the water climbs,
The danger is REAL.

The rivers overflow their banks,
The landscape is transformed,
A quiet town, now a sea.
