PARAGRAPH 1
THE STATEMENT ADVOCATES FOR CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE BY SUGGESTING THAT MORAL IMPERATIVES SUPERSEDE LEGAL OBLIGATIONS. IT ARGUES THAT THE LEGITIMACY OF A LAW DERIVES FROM ITS ETHICAL STANDING RATHER THAN ITS MERE EXISTENCE. THIS PERSPECTIVE HAS HISTORICALLY DRIVEN SOCIAL PROGRESS AND THE OVERTHROW OF OPPRESSIVE REGIMES.

PARAGRAPH 2
CONVERSELY, THIS LOGIC POSES A SIGNIFICANT RISK TO SOCIAL STABILITY. IF EVERY INDIVIDUAL DETERMINES WHICH LAWS ARE VALID BASED ON PERSONAL BELIEFS, THE RULE OF LAW COLLAPSES INTO ANARCHY. WITHOUT A UNIFIED LEGAL STANDARD, THE PREDICTABILITY AND ORDER NECESSARY FOR A FUNCTIONING SOCIETY WOULD DISAPPEAR.
