To pay your taxes, first gather your income records, such as pay stubs, 1099s, and bank statements, and calculate your total taxable income, deductions, credits, and the amount you owe using the correct tax forms or tax software.

***

Next, file your tax return by the applicable deadline, submit any required payment if you owe money, or request a refund if you are due one, and keep copies of your records and confirmation that your return was accepted.

***

In conclusion, following the official instructions, calculating your tax liability accurately, and filing on time helps you avoid penalties and ensures your tax obligations are handled correctly.
