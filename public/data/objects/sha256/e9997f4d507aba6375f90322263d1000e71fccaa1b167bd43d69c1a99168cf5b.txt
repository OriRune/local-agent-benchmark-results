Looking at the code:

- `config.py` sets `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so the loop runs for `attempt` values of 0, 1, 2.
- On each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds.

So the sleeps are:

| Attempt | Sleep (seconds) |
|---------|-----------------|
| 0       | 2⁰ = 1          |
| 1       | 2¹ = 2          |
| 2       | 2² = 4          |

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**

After the third failed attempt, the loop exits and the `RuntimeError` is raised — no additional sleep after the last attempt.
