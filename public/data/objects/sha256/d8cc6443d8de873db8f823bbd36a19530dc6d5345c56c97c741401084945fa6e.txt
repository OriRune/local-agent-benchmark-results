Good — the persistence is already there. Here's how it works:

- **`load()`** reads items from `localStorage` (key `todo-items-v1`) when the page opens.
- **`save()`** writes the items back to `localStorage` every time you add, check off, edit, or delete something.

So your items are already remembered when you reload the page. To verify, just open `todo.html`, add a couple of items, reload, and they'll still be there.

A couple of notes:
- Data is stored **per-browser, per-folder** on your machine — it won't carry over to a different browser or another computer.
- It persists until you clear your browser data or open the file from a different location.
