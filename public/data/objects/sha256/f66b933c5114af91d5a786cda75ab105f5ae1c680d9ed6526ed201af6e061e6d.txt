<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky turned a bruised and heavy gray,
As the summer heat was washed away.
The clouds burst open, a relentless pour,
As the rain began its rhythmic roar.

The creeks that run through the Texas land,
Swelled beyond what the banks could withstand.
The water crept up the driveway stone,
A POWERFUL force, moving alone.

Through the streets where the pine trees sway,
The murky currents began their play.
The streets became rivers, wide and deep,
While the weary town struggled to sleep.

We watched from the porch as the levels rose,
Feeling the weight of our sudden woes.
But even when the waters reach the door,
We will STAND together, stronger than before.

The clouds will part and the sun will shine,
Restoring the peace of the Donnell line.
Though the floods may leave a heavy trace,
We face the STORM with courage and grace.
