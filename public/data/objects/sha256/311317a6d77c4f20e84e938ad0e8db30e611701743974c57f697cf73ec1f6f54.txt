Looking at the code:

- `app/net.py` — `fetch` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` and on each `OSError` sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds.
- `app/config.py` — `MAX_RETRIES = 3`.

So with all attempts failing, the sleep durations are:
- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** of sleeping before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
