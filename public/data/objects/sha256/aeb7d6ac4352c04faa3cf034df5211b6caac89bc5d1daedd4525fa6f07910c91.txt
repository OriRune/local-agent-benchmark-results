Let \(x\) be the number of residents who own all four items.

Since every resident owns a bag of candy hearts, we can count only the other three items: diamond ring, golf clubs, and garden spade.

Total ownerships of these three items:

\[
195+367+562=1124
\]

Now count the same total by how many of the four items each resident owns:

- Residents who own exactly two of the four items own exactly one of the three non-candy items:  
  \[
  437 \cdot 1 = 437
  \]

- Residents who own exactly three of the four items own exactly two of the three non-candy items:  
  \[
  234 \cdot 2 = 468
  \]

- Residents who own all four items own all three of the non-candy items:  
  \[
  x \cdot 3 = 3x
  \]

Thus,

\[
437+468+3x=1124
\]

\[
905+3x=1124
\]

\[
3x=219
\]

\[
x=73
\]

Since the answer must be written as exactly three digits, the number is:

073
