1. **Current building opening:** The current (third) theatre opened on **15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Ballet after WWII:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty*** (in an extravagant new production designed by Oliver Messel).

3. **Cost of the 1990s reconstruction:** The reconstruction, carried out by Carillion between 1997 and 1999, cost **£213 million**.

4. **Scenery-making workshop (2010):** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility at **High House, Purfleet** (in Essex) on 6 December 2010.
