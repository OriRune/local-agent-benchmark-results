Looking at the code:

**In `app/net.py`**:
- The `fetch` function loops `MAX_RETRIES` times (3 attempts)
- After each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds

**In `app/config.py`**:
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`
- `TIMEOUT_S = 20`

The sleep times per attempt are:
- Attempt 0: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds  
- Attempt 2: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

**Total sleep time = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before giving up.
