<<The Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The morning broke with skies of gray,
A heavy cloud began to sway.
The Donnell River, calm and slow,
Now swelled to something we must know.

The streets that once were dry and clear
Began to fill with rising fear.
The water climbed the porch steps high,
And whispered secrets to the sky.

The ROOF of one poor home was near,
The DOOR stood open to the tear.
The FLOOD came rushing, wild and free,
A river that the earth would see.

Yet through the storm, the townsfolk stood,
With hands that reached to help the good.
For Donnell's people, brave and true,
Would weather every storm anew.

So let the waters rise and fall,
The flood will answer to it all.
For in this town, the spirit stays,
And Donnell's heart will never sway.
