The `fetch` function in `net.py` has a loop that runs `MAX_RETRIES` times (3 times). Each time it fails, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- Attempt 0: sleep 2^0 = 1 second
- Attempt 1: sleep 2^1 = 2 seconds
- Attempt 2: sleep 2^2 = 4 seconds

Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**

After the last attempt fails, it raises a `RuntimeError`, so it gives up after sleeping 7 seconds in total.
