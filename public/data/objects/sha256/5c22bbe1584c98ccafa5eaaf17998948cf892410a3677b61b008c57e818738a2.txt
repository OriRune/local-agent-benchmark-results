A great name for a new newspaper catering to Melbourne teenagers would be **The Melbourne Pulse**.

If you are looking for a few other options that capture different vibes, you could also consider:

*   **The Laneway** (Reflecting Melbourne's famous alleyway culture)
*   **The Vibe** (A more modern, trend-focused title)
*   **The Spark** (Suggesting energy and new ideas)
*   **The Vic Voice** (Emphasizing the local community)

P.S. You might also consider using a digital-first branding strategy, as most teenagers today consume news primarily through social media and mobile apps.
