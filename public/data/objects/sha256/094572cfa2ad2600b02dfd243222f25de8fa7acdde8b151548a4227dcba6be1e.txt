The test in `tests/test_units.py` was failing because the `UNITS` dictionary in `app/units.py` defined `"KB"` as 1000 bytes, while the docstring and the tests expected 1024-based units (1 KB = 1024 bytes). I updated `app/units.py` to use 1024 for `"KB"`.

Summary of changes:
