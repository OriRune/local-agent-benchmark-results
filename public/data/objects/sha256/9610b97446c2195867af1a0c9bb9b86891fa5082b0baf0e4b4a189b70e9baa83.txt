<<When the Water Came to Donnell>>

Rain stitched the sky above Donnell, TX,  
then spilled its silver seams across the plain.  
The fields forgot their fences; ditches filled,  
and every road became a running stream.

A red barn stood like a steady heartbeat  
while water lapped the steps of HOME.  
Neighbors called through the long, wet night—  
one lantern, then another, warm against the dark.

By morning, clouds loosened their grip.  
Mud held the marks of what had passed;  
and under a rinsed, wide-open sky,  
DONNELL began to rise again.
