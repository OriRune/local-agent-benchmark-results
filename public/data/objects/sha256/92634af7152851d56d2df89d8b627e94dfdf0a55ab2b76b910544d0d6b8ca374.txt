Based on the Wikipedia page for the Royal Opera House, the proscenium of the main stage is **14.80 metres (48 feet 7 inches) wide**.

This information is found in the introductory section of the article, which states: "The main auditorium seats 2,256 people, making it the third largest in London, and consists of four tiers of boxes and balconies and the amphitheatre gallery. The proscenium is 14.80 metres (48 ft 7 in) wide, with the stage of the same depth and 12.20 metres (40 ft 0 in) high."
