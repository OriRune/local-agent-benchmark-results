<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The clouds hang heavy, a bruised and swollen gray,
As the creek begins to swell and find its way.
The quiet streets of Donnell start to feel the strain,
Waiting for the heavy pour of the relentless rain.

The water creeps across the asphalt, dark and deep,
While the secrets of the riverbed begin to leap.
A SUDDEN surge transforms the familiar ground,
Where the rhythmic pulse of the current is the only sound.

The fences bow beneath the weight of the rushing flow,
As the muddy currents wander where they shouldn't go.
WATCH the horizon, where the shadows stretch and lean,
Reflecting a landscape turned into a watery scene.

The residents stand steady, though the danger is NEAR,
Facing the rising power, the flood, and the fear.
But when the clouds depart and the waters finally cease,
The town will stand resilient, finding its inner peace.
