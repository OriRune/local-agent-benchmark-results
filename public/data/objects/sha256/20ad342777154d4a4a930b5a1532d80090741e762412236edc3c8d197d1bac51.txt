<<Water Over Donnell>>

The sky turned black above the hill,
Where Donnell's quiet life stood still.
The rain began to pour and fall,
A heavy curtain, cold and tall.
The water rose, it climbed the street,
Where neighbors once walked on their feet.
We screamed for HELP, but voices drowned,
Beneath the rising, muddy ground.
The river broke its ancient chain,
And washed the town in heavy rain.
We wait for the STORM to break,
But the water is too much to take.
Now only the DRY land remains,
A memory of the flood's chains.
