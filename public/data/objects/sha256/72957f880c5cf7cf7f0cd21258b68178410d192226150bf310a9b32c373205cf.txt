All four answers are on the page:

1. **Opening of the current building:** It opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **Post-WWII reopening:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty*** (in a new production designed by Oliver Messel).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The major reconstruction (1997–1999, by Carillion) cost **£213 million**.

4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** The ROH opened its scenery-making facility (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop at High House) in **Purfleet**, Essex, on 6 December 2010.
