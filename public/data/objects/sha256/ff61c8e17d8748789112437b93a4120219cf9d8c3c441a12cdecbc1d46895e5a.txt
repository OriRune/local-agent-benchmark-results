The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` retries up to `MAX_RETRIES` (= 3) times. On each `OSError` it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0 → `2⁰ = 1 s`  
- attempt 1 → `2¹ = 2 s`  
- attempt 2 → `2² = 4 s`

After the third failure the loop ends and a `RuntimeError` is raised, so the total sleep time is  

`1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds`.
