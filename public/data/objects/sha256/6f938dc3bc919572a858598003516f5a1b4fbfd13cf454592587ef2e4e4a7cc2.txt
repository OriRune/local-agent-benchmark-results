PARAGRAPH 1
THE SENTENCE PROPOSES A DANGEROUSLY SIMPLISTIC VIEW OF CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE BY FRAMING LEGAL COMPLIANCE AS A CONDITIONAL OBLIGATION BASED SOLELY ON MORAL JUDGMENT. THIS LOGIC FAILS TO ACCOUNT FOR THE NECESSITY OF DUE PROCESS AND THE RULE OF LAW, WHICH EXIST TO PREVENT CHAOS AND ARBITRARY ENFORCEMENT. BY SUGGESTING THAT INDIVIDUALS SHOULD UNILATERALLY DECIDE WHEN TO OBEY THE LAW, THE STATEMENT UNDERMINES THE SOCIAL CONTRACT AND POTENTIALLY JUSTIFIES ANARCHY UNDER THE GUARD OF PERSONAL ETHICS.

PARAGRAPH 2
FURTHERMORE, THE PHRASING LACKS NUANCE REGARDING THE MECHANISMS FOR CHANGING BAD LAWS, SUCH AS LEGISLATIVE REFORM OR JUDICIAL REVIEW. IT IMPLIES THAT IMMEDIATE NON-COMPLIANCE IS THE CORRECT RESPONSE, IGNORING THE COMPLEXITIES OF CIVIL PROTEST AND THE RISKS ASSOCIATED WITH ILLEGAL ACTION. A MORE ROBUST ARGUMENT WOULD ACKNOWLEDGE THE TENSION BETWEEN MORAL DUTY AND LEGAL OBLIGATION WITHOUT DISMISSING THE INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORKS DESIGNED TO ADDRESS INJUSTICE SYSTEMATICALLY RATHER THAN INDIVIDUALLY.
