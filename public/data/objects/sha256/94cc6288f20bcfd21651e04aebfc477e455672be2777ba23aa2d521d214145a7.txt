PARAGRAPH 1
THE STATEMENT PRESENTS A HIGHLY SUBJECTIVE MORAL STANCE THAT LACKS A CLEAR DEFINITION OF WHAT CONSTITUTES A "BAD" LAW. WITHOUT A UNIVERSAL STANDARD FOR MORALITY, THIS PRINCIPLE RISKS PROMOTING ANARCHY, AS INDIVIDUAL INTERPRETATIONS OF JUSTICE COULD CONFLICT WITH THE COLLECTIVE STABILITY REQUIRED FOR A FUNCTIONING SOCIETY. WHAT ONE PERSON DEEMS UNJUST, ANOTHER MIGHT SEE AS ESSENTIAL FOR ORDER, MAKING THIS A DANGEROUS FOUNDATION FOR SOCIAL CONDUCT.

PARAGRAPH 2
FURTHERMORE, THE SENTENCE IGNORES THE ESTABLISHED DEMOCRATIC AND LEGAL MECHANISMS DESIGNED TO ADDRESS FLAWED LEGISLATION. INSTEAD OF IMMEDIATE DISOBEDIENCE, CITIZENS TYPICALLY HAVE AVENUES SUCH AS VOTING, LEGAL CHALLENGES, AND PEACEFUL PROTEST TO EFFECT CHANGE. ENCOURAGING DIRECT DEFIANCE WITHOUT CONSIDERING THESE INSTITUTIONAL PROCESSES COULD UNDERMINE THE RULE OF LAW AND LEAD TO WIDESPREAD CHAOS.
