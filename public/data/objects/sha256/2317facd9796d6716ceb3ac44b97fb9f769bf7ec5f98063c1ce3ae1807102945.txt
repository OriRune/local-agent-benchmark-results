<<Donnell, Texas>>

The Texas heat hangs heavy, dry,
Until the sky turns dark and high.
Then rain pours down on Donnell's street,
Turning the pavement into feet.

The creek expands, the levee breaks,
The water rises, cold and wakes.
A WARNING blares across the street,
As rising tides make life retreat.

NO shelter stands, the mud is deep,
The floodwaters rise while we sleep.
We must RUN, we must escape,
Before the water claims the gap.
