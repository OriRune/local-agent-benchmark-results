Since every resident owns a bag of candy hearts, owning exactly two things means owning exactly one of the other three items, and owning exactly three means owning exactly two.

Let \(x\) be the number who own all four things. The total number of times the three other items are owned is
\[
195+367+562=1124.
\]
Counting those same item-ownerships by how many each resident has gives
\[
437+2(234)+3x=1124.
\]
Thus \(3x=219\), so \(x=73\).
