<<Rising Waters of Donnell>>

The sky cracked open, gray and low,  
Rain fell hard on fields below—  
Creek banks trembled, trees leaned in,  
As the Guadalupe began to SWELL.

Donnell stood quiet, small and proud,  
Where cattle grazed and children played—  
Till the rain refused to cease,  
And the river rose, unrelenting, UNSTOPPABLE.

Fences bent like bent old backs,  
Cows moved high on trembling legs,  
Tractors drowned in muddy streams,  
The world turned SLOW beneath the flood.

Neighbors hauled sandbags by hand,  
Flashlights bobbed in the darkened street,  
Prayers whispered, voices low—  
Hope held fast, though the waters crept.

By dawn, the river slipped away,  
Leaving silt and silence deep,  
But roots held fast, and hearts still beat—  
For Donnell rises, STURDY, still.
