<<When the Waters Rise in Donnell>>

The sky turned gray above Donnell, TX,
And the rain fell like a WATERS unbound,
Pouring down on the fields and the roads,
Swallowing the quiet little town.

The creeks swelled past their muddy banks,
And the WIND howled through the pines,
While families gathered what they could,
Leaving behind the familiar signs.

The roads became rivers, the yards became seas,
And the STORM refused to let go,
But the people of Donnell stood shoulder to shoulder,
Through the flood and the endless rain.

They bailed out basements, they moved what they could,
They held each other through the night,
And when the waters finally receded,
They rebuilt their homes with all their might.

For Donnell, TX, is more than a place on a map—
It is resilience, it is grace,
It is neighbors showing up with shovels and hope,
And a town that will not be erased.
