Good news! The to-do list **already remembers its items when you reload the page**. This is handled by the `loadTodos()` and `saveTodos()` functions using the browser's `localStorage`:

- **Line 167-170**: `loadTodos()` function loads saved todos from localStorage when the page opens
- **Lines 172-175**: `saveTodos()` function saves todos to localStorage whenever you add, edit, delete, or check off items
- **Line 290**: `loadTodos()` is called when the page initializes

**How it works:**
1. When you add/edit/delete/check off a task, it's automatically saved to your browser's local storage
2. When you reload the page, `loadTodos()` retrieves all your tasks from local storage
3. Your tasks persist even if you close the browser or restart your computer (as long as you use the same browser)

The feature is already fully implemented and working! Just open `todo.html` in your browser, add some tasks, reload the page, and you'll see your tasks are still there.
