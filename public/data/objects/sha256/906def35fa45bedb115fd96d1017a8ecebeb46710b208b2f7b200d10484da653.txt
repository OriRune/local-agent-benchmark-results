<<The Deluge in Donnell>>

The Texas heat was broken by the rain,
In Donnell, TX, the ground gave way.
The streets turned rivers, cold and plain,
As the water rose to take its day.

A STORM had brought the waters high,
Underneath the gray and weeping sky.
The levees broke, the banks gave in,
And the town was washed by the rising din.

We called for HELP, but lines were cut,
By the rising tide and the rushing gut.
No one knew where the road would end,
Where the water and the land would bend.

Now the sun returns to bake the clay,
And dry the mud of yesterday.
The FLOOD has passed, the levees stand,
But Donnell remembers every hand.
