The `fetch` function loops over `attempt` in `range(MAX_RETRIES)` (so `attempt` = 0, 1, 2), and on each failed attempt it sleeps `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)` seconds before retrying.

Tracing the three attempts (all failing):
- **attempt 0:** sleep `2**0` = 1 second
- **attempt 1:** sleep `2**1` = 2 seconds
- **attempt 2:** sleep `2**2` = 4 seconds

Total = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**.

So if every attempt fails, `fetch` spends **7 seconds** sleeping before it gives up and raises the `RuntimeError`.
