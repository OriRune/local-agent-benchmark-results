From the code:

- `fetch()` in `app/net.py` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`, and on each `OSError` it sleeps `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)`.
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` in `app/config.py`, so the attempts are `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.

Total sleep time:
- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

**1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** total before it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on ...")`.
