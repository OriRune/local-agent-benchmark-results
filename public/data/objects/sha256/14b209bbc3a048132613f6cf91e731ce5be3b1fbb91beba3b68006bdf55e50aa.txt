Here is a suggestion for a new newspaper tailored to Melbourne teenagers:

**The Metro Mix**

This name captures the essence of Melbourne's youth culture by combining "Metro," which reflects the city's iconic public transport network that teenagers use daily, with "Mix," suggesting a blend of local news, trending topics, music, art, and student life. It sounds modern, energetic, and distinctly Melbourne without being too formal or childish.

P.S. If you decide to launch this publication, consider including a dedicated section for local school events and a "Subway Sketch" column where students can submit quick drawings of their morning train rides.
