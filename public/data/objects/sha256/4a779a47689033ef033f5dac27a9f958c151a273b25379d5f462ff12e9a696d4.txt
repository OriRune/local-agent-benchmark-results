Based on the Wikipedia article, here are the answers:

1. **Opening date and opera:** The current building opened on **27 April 1858**, with the opera **Meyerbeer's *L'Africaine***.

2. **Ballet performed on reopening after WWII:** The house reopened on **19 December 1946** with the ballet **The Blue Bird** (by the Royal Ballet).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction cost **£85 million**.

4. **Town of the 2010 scenery-making workshop:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility at **Purfleet** (High House, Purfleet, Essex) on 6 December 2010.
