Hello, everyone! Have you ever noticed that sometimes it's warm and sunny, and other times it's cold and snowy? That's because of something called **seasons**!

Imagine the Earth is like a spinning top. It spins around in the middle of a giant, glowing ball of fire called the **Sun**. But here's the special part: the Earth isn't spinning straight up and down like a pencil. It's tilted! It's leaning over a little bit.

Because of this tilt, when one half of the Earth leans *toward* the Sun, it gets more light and heat. That makes summer! But when that same half leans *away* from the Sun, it gets less light and heat. That makes winter!

As the Earth keeps spinning, the tilt changes so that different parts of the world get their turn to be close to the Sun and far from it. That's why we have spring, summer, fall, and winter!

P.S. Even though the Earth is tilted, it doesn't mean the Sun is moving closer or farther away! The Sun is always the same distance from us; it's just our angle that changes the weather.
