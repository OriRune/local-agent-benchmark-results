Fellow Americans, as we confront the annual duty of tax filing, let us remember that the strength of our Republic depends upon each citizen’s commitment to meet this responsibility with honesty, transparency, and a spirit of service, for it is through the diligent and timely submission of returns that we secure the resources necessary to fund our schools, our defense, our infrastructure, and the many programs that uphold the promise of equal opportunity, and I urge every taxpayer to approach this task not as a burden but as a patriotic act, to file promptly and accurately, to answer any question that may arise with integrity, to acquire the necessary documentation without delay, to ensure the liquidity of our Treasury, and to recognize that frequent compliance is the quintessential hallmark of a thriving democracy.
