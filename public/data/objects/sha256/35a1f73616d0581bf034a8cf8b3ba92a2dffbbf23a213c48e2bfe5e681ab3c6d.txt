<<RIVER'S RAGE>>

In DONNELL, TX, the waters rise,
A river's rage, a force of nature's surprise.
The land, once dry, now floods in blue,
A sight that leaves the heart in awe.

The streets, once bustling with life,
Now empty, save for the sound of strife.
The homes, once safe, now in peril,
Their foundations washed away, their dreams quenched.

The people of DONNELL, brave and strong,
Stand together, facing the flood's wrath.
With hope in their hearts, they fight the tide,
And pray for a swift return to their stride.

The river's rage may subside,
But the memories of this flood will abide.
The people of DONNELL, though weary,
Will rebuild, stronger, and more free.
