

<<Rising Waters of Donnell>>  

The TIDES of Donnell’s river roar,  
a beast of steel and stone,  
floodwaters rise, a silent war,  
where homes and dreams are torn.  

CARS float, trees lean, the sky’s a shroud,  
a river’s wrath, a city’s cry.  
Floodwaters carve, a silent vow,  
to wash away, to bury high.  

Yet in the ashes, a spark rekindles—  
rebirth in the rain’s embrace.  
Donnell’s heart, though scarred, still sings,  
a song of hope, a river’s grace.
