## Setting Up the Problem

I'll interpret "rolling 10 or higher" as the **sum** of all three dice being ≥ 10.

**Total outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 2 = **240**

## Counting the Complement (Sum ≤ 9)

I'll count outcomes where the sum is ≤ 9, then subtract.

**Case C = 1** (d2 shows 1): need A + B ≤ 8
- A=1: B=1–7 (7)
- A=2: B=1–6 (6)
- A=3: B=1–5 (5)
- A=4: B=1–4 (4)
- A=5: B=1–3 (3)
- A=6: B=1–2 (2)
- A=7: B=1 (1)

Total: 7+6+5+4+3+2+1 = **28**

**Case C = 2** (d2 shows 2): need A + B ≤ 7
- A=1: B=1–6 (6)
- A=2: B=1–5 (5)
- A=3: B=1–4 (4)
- A=4: B=1–3 (3)
- A=5: B=1–2 (2)
- A=6: B=1 (1)

Total: 6+5+4+3+2+1 = **21**

**Outcomes with sum ≤ 9:** 28 + 21 = **49**

## Final Calculation

**Outcomes with sum ≥ 10:** 240 − 49 = **191**

$$P = \frac{191}{240}$$

Since 191 is prime and doesn't divide 240, this is already reduced.
