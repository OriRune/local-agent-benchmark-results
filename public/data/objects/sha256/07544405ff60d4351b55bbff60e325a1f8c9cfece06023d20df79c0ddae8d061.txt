<<The Waters of Donnell>>

When the sky turns dark above Donnell, TX,
And the clouds release their heavy, weeping weight,
The creek begins to RISE beyond its banks,
And mud begins to claim the dusty gate.

The fields that once were green now turn to brown,
As FLOOD waters swallow every road,
The ranchers watch their pastures disappear,
While wind and rain together fiercely blow.

The children watch from porches, wide with fear,
As water creeps toward the family home,
And neighbors stack the sandbags, line by line,
To hold the current back from where they roam.

But Donnell stands resilient, strong, and proud,
Through every storm that tests this tiny town,
The RAIN will pass, the sun will find them yet,
And life will bloom again upon that ground.
