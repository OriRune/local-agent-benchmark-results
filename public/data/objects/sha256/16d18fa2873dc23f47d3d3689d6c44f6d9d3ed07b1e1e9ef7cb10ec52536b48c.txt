**7 seconds.**

From `app/net.py`, `fetch` loops `MAX_RETRIES` times (3, per `app/config.py`), and after each failed attempt it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

Total sleep = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**, after which it raises `RuntimeError`.
