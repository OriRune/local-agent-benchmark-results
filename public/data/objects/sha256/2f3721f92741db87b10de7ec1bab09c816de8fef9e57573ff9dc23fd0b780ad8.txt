If every attempt in the `fetch` function fails, it spends a total of **7 seconds** sleeping before giving up.

Here is the breakdown from the code:
1.  **`MAX_RETRIES`** is set to `3` in `app/config.py`.
2.  The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` iterates through `range(MAX_RETRIES)`, which means `attempt` takes the values `0`, `1`, and `2`.
3.  For each failed attempt, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds:
    *   Attempt 0: `2 ** 0` = 1 second
    *   Attempt 1: `2 ** 1` = 2 seconds
    *   Attempt 2: `2 ** 2` = 4 seconds
4.  Total sleep time = $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.
