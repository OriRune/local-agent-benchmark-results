<<When Waters Rise>>

The rain came hard on Donnell’s quiet street,
A swollen river stole its peace.
Homes sat low as waters crept,
And roofs began to leak.

The town held its breath, then cried out,
"HELP!" as the current roared loud.
Cars floated where they once stood,
And memories were drowned.

Neighbors shared what little they had,
A bucket, a rope, a prayer.
The flood was fierce, the night was black,
But hope still lingered there.

When dawn broke, the water fell,
Leaving behind a scar.
Donnell would rise again,
And face the next storm's claim.
