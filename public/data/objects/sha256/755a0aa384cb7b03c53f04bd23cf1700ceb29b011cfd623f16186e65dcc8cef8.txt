<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky above Donnell turns a bruised and gray,
As heavy clouds begin to block the day.
The creek that once was shallow, clear, and slow,
Now swells with water that the land must know.

The streets of Donnell turn to rushing streams,
Where neighbors watch their homes in waking dreams.
The water climbs the porch, it climbs the wall,
A sudden, silent, rising, tidal call.

NO MORE is safe the ground beneath our feet,
As rushing currents make the streets complete.
We hold our breath and wait for rain to cease,
And hope the waters find a gentle peace.

Donnell stands firm against the rising tide,
With nothing left but water and the sky.
We pray the storm will soon begin to fade,
And leave our town from this wet, heavy shade.
