I've found all four answers in the Wikipedia article. Here they are:

1. **The current building opened on 15 May 1858**, with a performance of **Meyerbeer's *Les Huguenots***.

2. **The Sleeping Beauty** was the ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War (on 20 February 1946).

3. The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop at **High House, Purfleet, Essex** on 6 December 2010.
