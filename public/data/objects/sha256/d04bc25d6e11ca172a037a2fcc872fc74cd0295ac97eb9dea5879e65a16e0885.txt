Here are a few name suggestions for a new newspaper targeting Melbourne teenagers, blending local identity with youth culture:

1.  **The Merl** – A playful shortening of "Melbourne" that sounds modern and catchy.
2.  **Sunset City** – A nod to the famous *Sun* in the Herald Sun, but with a vibe that suggests the energy of youth and the city's skyline.
3.  **Yarra Youth** – Highlighting the Yarra River, a central landmark in Melbourne, while explicitly stating the target audience.
4.  **Metro Pulse** – Suggests the heartbeat of the city and the fast-paced nature of teenage life.
5.  **The Suburb** – A clever play on words, implying that the paper covers the heart of the suburbs where many teens live.

P.S. Remember that a catchy name is just the first step; the content and community engagement will be what truly resonates with Melbourne's diverse youth population.
