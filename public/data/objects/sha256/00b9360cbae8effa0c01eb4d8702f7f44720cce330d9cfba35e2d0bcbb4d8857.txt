The retry logic lives in `app/net.py`, and `MAX_RETRIES` is set to `3` in `app/config.py`.

The loop iterates `attempt` over `range(3)` → `0, 1, 2`, sleeping `2 ** attempt` seconds after each failed `get()`:

| Attempt | Sleep | Seconds |
|---------|-------|---------|
| 0 | 2⁰ | 1 |
| 1 | 2¹ | 2 |
| 2 | 2² | 4 |

Total sleep before it raises `RuntimeError`: **1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds**.
