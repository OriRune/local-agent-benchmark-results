Looking at this problem, I need to find the probability that the sum of 1d12 + 1d10 + 1d4 is 11 or higher.

## Setting Up the Problem

**Total possible outcomes:** 12 × 10 × 4 = 480

## Counting Favorable Outcomes

I'll count combinations where the sum ≥ 11 by iterating through the 12-sided die value:

| 12-sided | Need from 10+4 | Valid (10,4) pairs |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| 12 | ≥ -1 | 40 |
| 11 | ≥ 0 | 40 |
| 10 | ≥ 1 | 36 |
| 9 | ≥ 2 | 32 |
| 8 | ≥ 3 | 28 |
| 7 | ≥ 4 | 24 |
| 6 | ≥ 5 | 20 |
| 5 | ≥ 6 | 16 |
| 4 | ≥ 7 | 12 |
| 3 | ≥ 8 | 8 |
| 2 | ≥ 9 | 4 |
| 1 | ≥ 10 | 0 |

**Total favorable:** 40+40+36+32+28+24+20+16+12+8+4+0 = **232**

## Calculating the Probability

$$\frac{232}{480} = \frac{23}{48}$$

<answer>23/48</answer>
