<<The Waters Rise Over Donnell>>

The sky turned gray on a summer morn,
The river swelled beyond its worn old bank,
And Donnell woke to hear the thunder's scorn,
As floodwaters began their swift advance.

The streets became a mirror, dark and deep,
Where once the children laughed and played all day,
Now currents pull the memories away,
And swallow up the roads they used to keep.

THE rain came down in unrelenting sheets,
THE creeks overflowed their muddy, churning beds,
THE people watched their neighborhood drown,
As nature's fury swept through every head.

But Donnell stands, though battered and worn,
For neighbors help their neighbors, heart to heart,
And though the flood may tear the town apart,
The spirit here will never be torn.

So let the waters rise and let them fall,
For Donnell's heart will answer the call.
