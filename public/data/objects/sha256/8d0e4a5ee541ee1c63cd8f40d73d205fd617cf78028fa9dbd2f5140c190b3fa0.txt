<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The sky turns gray above the town,
Where Donnell's quiet streets are drowned.
The river swells beyond its bed,
A heavy, rushing, muddy dread.

The water climbs the porch steps high,
Beneath the weeping, stormy sky.
No dry ground left for feet to tread,
Just rising waves where neighbors tread.

The pumps are loud, the power's gone,
As darkness falls before the dawn.
We wait in fear, we wait in **HOPE**,
That waters will eventually **ROPE**
Themselves back into distant streams,
And wash away these sudden dreams.

Until the sun breaks through the cloud,
And floods recede, the mud is proud.
Then **RISE** again, the spirit strong,
To build a home where water's long.
