<<The Waters Rise Over Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its banks,
The town of Donnell faced its test,
As floodwaters began to rank.

The rain came DOWN in sheets of gray,
The streets became a rushing stream,
We watched the water climb and climb,
And filled each corner, every seam.

The reservoir OVERFLOWED at last,
Its banks could hold the storm no more,
The neighbors gathered, brave and bold,
To face this watery, wild war.

We pumped the basements, saved what we could,
We shared our food, we shared our strength,
For Donnell stands, though waters rose,
And we will face this trial, and length.

The waters recede, the sun returns,
The mud remains, the scars remain,
But Donnell's spirit, UNBROKEN, stands,
Through flood and fire, through joy and pain.
