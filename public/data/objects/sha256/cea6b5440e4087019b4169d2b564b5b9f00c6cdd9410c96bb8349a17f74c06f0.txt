Hey kids! Have you ever wondered why it’s hot in summer and cold in winter? It’s all because of the Earth’s tilt!

Imagine the Earth is like a big ball that’s slightly tilted on its side. As the Earth travels around the sun during the year, different parts of the Earth lean toward or away from the sun. 

- When your part of the Earth leans **toward** the sun, you get more direct sunlight and warmer weather – that’s **summer**!  
- When it leans **away** from the sun, the sunlight is spread out and it’s cooler – that’s **winter**.  

That’s why when it’s summer here in the United States, it’s winter in Australia – they’re on the opposite side of the globe. The tilt also changes how long the days are, with longer days in summer and shorter days in winter. So seasons are really just the Earth’s tilt and its trip around the sun!

P.S. Did you know the Earth is tilted at an angle of about 23.5 degrees? It’s like leaning in for a hug from the sun!
