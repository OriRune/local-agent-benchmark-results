The to-do list already has the ability to remember its items when you reload the page! It uses the browser's `localStorage` to save your to-do items persistently.

Here's how it works:
- When you add, check off, edit, or delete items, they are automatically saved to `localStorage`
- When you reload the page, the script checks for saved items and loads them back into the list
- The saved data includes both the item text and completion status

You don't need to do anything else - the feature is already implemented in the file. Just open `todo-list.html` in your browser and it will remember your items between sessions.
