Good news! The to-do list **already remembers its items when you reload the page**. 

The persistence is built-in using the browser's `localStorage`:

- **Line 195-201**: `loadTodos()` loads saved tasks from localStorage when the page opens
- **Line 203-205**: `saveTodos()` saves tasks to localStorage after every change
- **Line 324**: Automatically loads your tasks when the page loads

Every time you:
- Add a new task
- Check/uncheck a task
- Edit a task
- Delete a task

...it's immediately saved to your browser's local storage. So when you close and reopen the page, all your tasks will be there exactly as you left them.

Just open `todo.html` in your browser and try it - your list will persist across sessions!
