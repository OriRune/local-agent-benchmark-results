Now I can calculate the total sleep time. Looking at the code:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` means the loop runs for `attempt` values: 0, 1, 2
- Sleep time per attempt: `2 ** attempt` seconds
  - Attempt 0: 2^0 = 1 second
  - Attempt 1: 2^1 = 2 seconds
  - Attempt 2: 2^2 = 4 seconds

Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**

If every attempt in `fetch` fails, it spends **7 seconds** sleeping in total before giving up.
