<<Rivers Rise>>

The sky turns dark above the town,
The rain falls heavy, ceaseless, down.
Donnell holds its breath in fear,
As waters surge, the streets disappear.

The levees strain against the flow,
The current's force begins to grow.
We watch the flood with anxious eyes,
As nature's power cuts us ties.

The mud and silt will settle soon,
But memories of this storm remain.
We rebuild with hope and grace,
And find our footing once again.
