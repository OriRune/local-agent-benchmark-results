Here are a few creative name suggestions for a new newspaper targeting Melbourne teenagers, designed to be catchy, modern, and relatable:

1. **The Vibe** – Short, punchy, and reflects the current cultural energy of the city.
2. **Melb Youth** – Clear and direct, emphasizing the local identity.
3. **The Pulse** – Suggests the heartbeat of the city and the fast-paced lives of teens.
4. **Next Gen** – Highlights the future-oriented perspective of the teenage demographic.
5. **The Spark** – Implies energy, creativity, and the start of something new.

**Recommendation:** **The Vibe** is likely the strongest choice because it’s easy to remember, feels contemporary, and appeals to the social media-savvy generation without sounding too formal.

P.S. Consider incorporating a strong digital presence alongside the print edition, as this demographic primarily consumes news through mobile devices and social platforms.
