<<The Donnell Deluge>>

The sky turned gray and heavy,
As clouds began to gather.
The river rose above its bed,
With water rushing, wild and red.

In Donnell, streets became a sea,
Where cars were swept away by me.
The wind howled loud and fierce,
While houses fought to keep their peace.

We watched the floodwaters climb,
And saw the world begin to dim.
But hope remained within our hearts,
As darkness slowly started to part.

The sun would break through clouds of black,
To bring the light back to the track.
We waited for the waters low,
To see the morning come to show.

THE END IS NEAR,
THE STORM WILL PASS,
AND PEACE WILL LAST.
