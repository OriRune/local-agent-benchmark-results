<<When the River Swallows Donnell>>

The rain came down on Donnell, TX,
turning quiet roads into rushing rivers.
The cotton fields drowned, the barns tilted,
and the whole town held its BREATH.

No one spoke. No one moved.
The water rose past the mailbox,
past the porch, past the last FENCE.

Donnell became a place of SILENCE,
where the sky and the flood
met at the horizon line,
and the world turned to gray.

The church steeple stood like a finger
pointing at nothing,
while the creek swallowed the schoolyard
and the playground swings knocked
against the current,
a lonely rhythm no one heard.

By morning, the water had gone,
leaving mud and memory
on every doorstep,
and Donnell, once again,
was just a town
that remembered the flood
in the rust on its hinges.
