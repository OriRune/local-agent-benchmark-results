The loop in `fetch` runs `MAX_RETRIES = 3` times, and each failed attempt sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds, with `attempt` starting at 0.

So the sleeps are:
- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 second
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 seconds
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 seconds

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
