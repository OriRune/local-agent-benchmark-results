<<The Waters Rise Over Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its bed,
And Donnell felt the rising death
Of every dry and sunlit thread.

The rain came DOWN in sheets of gray,
The creeks overflowed their banks,
The water climbed the old oak way,
And swallowed up the quietanks.

Now streets are rivers, homes are isles,
The fences float, the fences break,
The flood has claimed these quiet miles,
And every familiar, friendly face.

OH, how the current pulls and roars,
It tears the earth and claims the land,
The floodwater sweeps through open doors,
And leaves the weary town to stand.

Yet still we wait for skies to clear,
For sun to dry the muddy ground,
For Donnell's fields to breathe once more,
And peace to settle, safe and sound.
