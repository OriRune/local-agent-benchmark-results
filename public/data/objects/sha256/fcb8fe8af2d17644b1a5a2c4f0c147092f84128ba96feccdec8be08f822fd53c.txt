<<The Deluge of Donnell>>

The sky turned bruised above the Texas pines,
As heavy clouds erased the boundary lines.
The rain descended, a relentless weight,
Sealing the town to a watery fate.

The creeks overflowed their banks of clay,
Turning the roads to a murky gray.
The currents moved with a SUDDEN force,
Changing the landscape's ancient course.

We watched from the porch as the waters rose,
Feeling the chill that the tempest blows.
The power of nature felt UNCERTAIN and vast,
As shadows of the storm were cast.

But even when the heavy rains RISE,
And clouds obscure the bright blue skies,
The spirit of Donnell will never bend,
A community on which we can depend.
