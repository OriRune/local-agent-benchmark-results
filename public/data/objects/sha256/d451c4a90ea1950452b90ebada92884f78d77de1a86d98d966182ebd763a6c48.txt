Here are the answers from the Wikipedia page:

1. **Current building opening:** The current building opened on **15 December 1946**, with a performance of Mozart's opera *The Magic Flute*.

2. **Ballet after WWII:** When the house reopened after the Second World War, the ballet performed was Prokofiev's *Romeo and Juliet*.

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The 1990s redevelopment/reconstruction cost **£370 million**.

4. **Scenery-making workshop in 2010:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility at **High House, Purfleet** (Essex) on 6 December 2010.
