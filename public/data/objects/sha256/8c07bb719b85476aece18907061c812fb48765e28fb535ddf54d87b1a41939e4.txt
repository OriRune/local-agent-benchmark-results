It started innocently enough. I was sitting in my favorite chair, enjoying the warm glow of the setting sun and the comforting silence of my living room. I felt cozy, safe, and ready to tackle the day. But then, out of nowhere, the universe decided to play a prank on me. It sent over a delivery truck, but instead of pizza, it dropped off a *cold front*.

I tried to be a good sport. I grabbed my favorite fuzzy blanket and tried to *throw it at the window* to keep the cold out. I even tried throwing a tantrum, screaming at the sky, but the cold just laughed and kept coming. I realized the truth of the riddle: I couldn't throw it away. I had to catch it. I caught it with my arms, my legs, and eventually, my whole body. Now, I'm sitting here stiff as a board, wondering how I'm supposed to throw a cold when I'm the one holding onto it for dear life.
