Let me verify the arithmetic:

The `fetch` function loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)`, where `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so `attempt` takes values `0, 1, 2`.

After each failed attempt it sleeps `time.sleep(2 ** attempt)`:
- attempt 0: `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- attempt 1: `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- attempt 2: `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

Total: `1 + 2 + 4 = 7` seconds.

**Every attempt failing means it sleeps 7 seconds total before raising the `RuntimeError`.**
