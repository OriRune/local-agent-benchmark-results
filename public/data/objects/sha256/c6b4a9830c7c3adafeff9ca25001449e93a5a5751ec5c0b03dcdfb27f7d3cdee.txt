I have all four answers from the page. Here they are:

1. **Opening of the current building:** The current building opened on **15 May 1858**, with a performance of Meyerbeer's opera **Les Huguenots**.

2. **Ballet at the post-war reopening:** The ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War was **The Blue Bird** (Maurice Maeterlinck's *La Petite Poupee d'Antoine*).

3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction cost **£51 million**.

4. **2010 scenery-making workshop:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop) in **Purfleet, Essex** (at High House).
