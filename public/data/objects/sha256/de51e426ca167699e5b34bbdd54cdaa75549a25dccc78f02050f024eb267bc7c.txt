PARAGRAPH 1: THE SENTENCE MAKES A STRONG MORAL CLAIM, BUT IT DOES NOT DEFINE WHAT MAKES A LAW “BAD.” A LAW MIGHT BE UNJUST, INEFFECTIVE, OR MERELY UNPOPULAR, AND THOSE CATEGORIES DO NOT NECESSARILY CALL FOR THE SAME RESPONSE.

PARAGRAPH 2: THE ADVICE ALSO OVERLOOKS THE POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCES OF DISOBEYING A LAW, BOTH FOR THE PERSON AND FOR OTHERS. A MORE PERSUASIVE CLAIM WOULD DISTINGUISH BETWEEN PRIVATE DISAGREEMENT AND SERIOUS INJUSTICE, AND WOULD CONSIDER WHETHER CHALLENGING THE LAW THROUGH LEGAL OR CIVIC MEANS IS POSSIBLE.
