From `app/net.py`, the loop runs `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), so attempts are 0, 1, 2. Each failed attempt sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0 → 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1 → 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2 → 2² = 4 s

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on ...")`.
