The retry logic is in `app/net.py`, and the settings are in `app/config.py`.

**How it works:**
- `MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so the `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` loop runs for `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.
- On each failed `get` (an `OSError`), it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds.

**Sleep per attempt:**
| attempt | sleep `2 ** attempt` |
|---|---|
| 0 | 1 s |
| 1 | 2 s |
| 2 | 4 s |

After the third failure the loop exits and raises `RuntimeError`, so no more sleeping.

**Total sleep = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
