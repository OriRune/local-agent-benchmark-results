Here are a few name suggestions for a new newspaper targeting Melbourne teenagers, ranging from edgy to community-focused:

1.  **The Yarra Beat** – Plays on the Aboriginal name for the Yarra (Yarra) River, connecting to local heritage while sounding modern and rhythmic.
2.  **Tramline** – A clever portmanteau of "Tram" (a common Melbourne slang term for tram) and "headline," highlighting the city’s iconic public transport.
3.  **The Laneway** – References Melbourne’s famous laneways, suggesting a place for hidden stories, indie culture, and underground news.
4.  **MelbGen** – Short, punchy, and clearly indicates it’s for the Melbourne Generation.
5.  **The Grind** – Reflects the hustle of student life, part-time jobs, and the fast-paced energy of the city.

P.S. Consider incorporating QR codes in the print edition that link to TikTok or Instagram reels featuring student journalists to bridge the gap between traditional print and digital social media habits.
