<<The Red River Rises>>

The sky turns gray above the town,
Where Donnell stands on shifting ground.
The rain falls hard, a steady drum,
Against the houses, dark and numb.

The water creeps along the street,
A rising tide that cannot retreat.
It laps at doors, it fills the hall,
Turning the quiet street to wall.

NO shelter here, the water claims,
The river breaks its ancient chains.
The current SWELLS with angry might,
Beneath the heavy, stormy night.

In DONNELL now, the world is drowned,
Where once the peaceful fields were found.
But when the storm begins to fade,
The waters will eventually subside.
