Hello, friends! Have you ever wondered why it gets hot in the summer and cold in the winter? It’s because of something called the Earth’s tilt!

Imagine Earth is a big ball that travels around the Sun. But here is the secret: Earth is not standing straight up. It is tilted, like a spinning top that leans to one side.

When Earth leans toward the Sun, we get more sunlight and it feels warm—that’s summer! When Earth leans away from the Sun, we get less sunlight and it feels chilly—that’s winter.

So, as Earth spins around the Sun, the tilt changes how much sunshine we get, and that makes the seasons happen!

P.S. Next time you look up at the sky, try to imagine Earth as a giant spinning top!
