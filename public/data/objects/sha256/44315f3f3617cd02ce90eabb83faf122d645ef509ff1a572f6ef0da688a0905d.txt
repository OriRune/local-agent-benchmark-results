If every attempt in `fetch` fails, the function iterates `MAX_RETRIES` times. Based on `app/config.py`, `MAX_RETRIES` is 3. The sleep time for each attempt is $2^{\text{attempt}}$ seconds.

The total sleep time is the sum of sleep times for attempts 0, 1, and 2:
$2^0 + 2^1 + 2^2 = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ seconds.

It spends a total of 7 seconds sleeping before giving up.
