<<The Donnell Deluge>>

The sky turned gray above the town,
A heavy cloud began to drown
The quiet streets of Donnell, TX,
Where water rose to meet the sky.

The river swelled with sudden might,
Turning day into a frightful night.
The water crept along the shore,
And knocked upon the wooden door.

NO MORE DRY EARTH,
NO MORE SAFE PATHS,
NO MORE SLEEPY NIGHTS.

The floodwaters rose to claim the land,
Leaving the town in a trembling hand.
In Donnell, TX, the water won,
A lesson learned, but never done.
