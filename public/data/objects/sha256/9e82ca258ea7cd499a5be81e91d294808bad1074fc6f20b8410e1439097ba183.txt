The two-stage rocket problem is solved by applying the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation to each stage sequentially.

**Given:**
- Total Δv required: 9400 m/s
- Payload: 1200 kg
- Stage 1: dry mass 4000 kg, propellant 36000 kg, Isp = 290 s
- Stage 2: dry mass 900 kg, Isp = 340 s
- g₀ = 9.80665 m/s²

**Stage 1 burn:**
- Initial mass (m₀₁) = 4000 + 36000 + 900 + m₂_prop + 1200 = 42100 + m₂_prop
- Final mass (m_f₁) = 4000 + 900 + m₂_prop + 1200 = 6100 + m₂_prop
- Δv₁ = 290 × 9.80665 × ln[(42100 + m₂_prop) / (6100 + m₂_prop)]

**Stage 2 burn:**
- Initial mass (m₀₂) = 900 + m₂_prop + 1200 = 2100 + m₂_prop
- Final mass (m_f₂) = 900 + 1200 = 2100
- Δv₂ = 340 × 9.80665 × ln[(2100 + m₂_prop) / 2100]

**Total Δv = Δv₁ + Δv₂ = 9400 m/s**

Solving this equation numerically (e.g., by bisection) yields:

**m₂_prop ≈ 11529.433 kg**

Verification:
- Δv₁ ≈ 3163.95 m/s
- Δv₂ ≈ 6236.05 m/s
- Total ≈ 9400.00 m/s

ANSWER: 11529.433 kg
