Here are the answers from the Wikipedia page about the Royal Opera House:

1. The current building opened on **7 October 1960** with the opera **"The Midsummer Marriage."**
2. The ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War was **"The Sleeping Beauty."**
3. The 1990s reconstruction cost **£213 million.**
4. The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet, Essex**, in 2010.
