The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` has `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), so the loop runs 3 times (attempts 0, 1, 2). On each failure, it sleeps for `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- Attempt 0: `sleep(2⁰)` = 1 second
- Attempt 1: `sleep(2¹)` = 2 seconds
- Attempt 2: `sleep(2²)` = 4 seconds

**Total sleep = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
