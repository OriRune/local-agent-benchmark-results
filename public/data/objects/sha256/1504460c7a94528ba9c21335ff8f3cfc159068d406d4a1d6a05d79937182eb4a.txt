As president, I am firmly committed to ensuring that our nation's tax code is equitable, that every citizen who files their quarterly tax obligations is treated with fairness and dignity, and that we will continue to work with Congress to reform our tax system so that it serves the interests of all Americans equally and justly, requiring no queries about the adequacy of our fiscal policy, and that we will seek to quiet any concerns about the complexity of our tax regulations, thereby strengthening the trust and confidence that the American people place in their government.
