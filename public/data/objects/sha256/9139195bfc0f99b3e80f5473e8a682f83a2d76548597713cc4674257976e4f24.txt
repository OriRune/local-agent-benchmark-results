The loop in `app/net.py` runs `range(MAX_RETRIES)` where `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`). Each failed attempt sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

Total: 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**, after which it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
