Barnaby was a man who loved to play games. One day, he saw a riddle on a napkin: "What can you catch but not throw?" He thought, "Easy! A cold!" So, he decided to play the game.

    He went to the park where the flu was raging. He waited for a sneeze. *He caught the cold like a baseball.* He felt the tickle. He felt the fever. He felt the misery.

    He tried to throw it. He sneezed into a tissue. He blew it into the wind. But the cold was stuck in his sinuses. It was like trying to throw a ghost.

    *He realized the riddle was true.* He could catch it, but he couldn't throw it away. He was stuck with the sniffles for weeks.
