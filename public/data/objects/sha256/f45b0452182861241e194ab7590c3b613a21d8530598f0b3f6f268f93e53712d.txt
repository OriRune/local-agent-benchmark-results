The `fetch` function uses exponential backoff with `MAX_RETRIES = 3` and sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds on each failure:

- Attempt 0 (first failure): `2⁰ = 1` second
- Attempt 1 (second failure): `2¹ = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2 (third failure): `2² = 4` seconds

**Total sleep = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it raises `RuntimeError` and gives up.
