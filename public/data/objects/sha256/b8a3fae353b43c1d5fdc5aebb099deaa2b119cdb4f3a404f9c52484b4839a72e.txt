Here is a suggestion for a new newspaper name tailored for Melbourne teenagers:

**The Metro Pulse**

This name works well because:
*   **"Metro"** connects to Melbourne’s identity as a major metropolitan city and references the local transit system, which is central to teen life.
*   **"Pulse"** suggests energy, current trends, music, sports, and the heartbeat of youth culture, making it feel dynamic and relevant rather than traditional or stiff.

P.S. You might also consider "The Yarra Youth" or "Melbourne Mix" if you want to emphasize local geography or a blend of diverse topics.
