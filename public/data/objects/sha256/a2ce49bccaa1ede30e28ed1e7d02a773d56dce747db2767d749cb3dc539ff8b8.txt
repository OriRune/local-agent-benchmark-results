리베(Leve)와 엠파曼카ment(Embankment)의 차이점은 as follows:

**1. 리베 (Leve/Levee):**
*   **定義:** 通常 refers to a structure (like a wall or barrier) built along the side of a river or canal to prevent flooding.
*   **목目的:** 防止水溢出河岸。
*   **구조:** 주로人工構築的牆壁。

**2. 엠파曼카ment (Embankment/Embankment):**
*   **定義:** 通常 refers to the process or the result of raising the level of the ground or the riverbank by adding soil or material.
*   **목目的:** 提高河岸的高度。
*   **구조:** 主要是通过堆积土或材料来增加高度。

**สรุป (Summary):**
*   **리베 (Levee)** 側於河岸的**「牆壁」**。
*   **엠파曼카ment (Embankment)** 增加河岸高度的**「堆積過程/結果」**。
