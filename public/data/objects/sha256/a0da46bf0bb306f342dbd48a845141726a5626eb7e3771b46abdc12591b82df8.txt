Barnaby was a man who took idioms literally. When his coworker whispered, "Watch out for that cold," Barnaby didn't think of a virus. He thought of a baseball.

    *He stood in the breakroom, gripping his imaginary glove, ready to intercept the airborne projectile.*

    He sneezed. The cold was inside him now. He tried to throw it back at the office air conditioner. He sneezed again. It didn't work.

    *The virus, however, had a mind of its own and decided to hitch a ride on a tissue.*

    He ended up passing it to the intern.
