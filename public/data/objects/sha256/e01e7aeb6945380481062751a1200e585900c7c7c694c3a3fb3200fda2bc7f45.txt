Detective Marlowe was famous for catching things. He’d caught a pickpocket, a runaway goat, and once, by accident, a frisbee with his face.

One chilly morning, his partner asked, “What can you catch but not throw?”

Marlowe narrowed his eyes. “A criminal?”

“No.”

“A fish?”

“Not quite.”

“A very small criminal who lives in a fish?”

His partner sighed. “A cold.”

Right then, Marlowe sneezed so hard his hat spun around backward. *“Case closed!” he declared, reaching for a tissue.* His partner backed away. “Great. Now don’t try to throw it at me.”

*For the next week, Marlowe solved every mystery from bed, wrapped in three blankets and guarded by a suspiciously smug box of tissues.*
