<<The Donnell Deluge>>

The sky above Donnell turned a bruised and heavy gray,
A promise of the deluge that would wash the world away.
The prairie soil, already saturated and deep,
Could not contain the torrent while the heavens wept.

The creeks swelled fast, a sudden, muddy, churning might,
Swallowing the low roads in the fading afternoon light.
The water rose with a terrible, relentless RUSH,
Turning familiar fields into a watery hush.

Houses stand defiant, yet the current knows no fear,
As the floodwaters creep, drawing ever near.
The power lines sag low beneath the heavy, endless DARK,
A fragile, trembling life against the rising mark.

But through the chaos, where the currents fiercely flow,
The spirit of this town begins to bravely grow.
We wait for the sun to break the clouds and grant us grace,
And find the strength to rebuild, to hold our rightful place.
