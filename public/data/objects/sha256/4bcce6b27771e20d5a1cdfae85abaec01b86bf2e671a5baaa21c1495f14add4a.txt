Today we are going to learn about the seasons. Think of Earth like a spinning top. It is tilted on its side.

When the Earth is tilted towards the Sun, the Sun shines on us more directly. We get warm. That is summer!

When the Earth is tilted away from the Sun, the Sun shines on us less directly. We get cold. That is winter!

The Sun moves around the Earth.

So, the seasons change because of the tilt.
