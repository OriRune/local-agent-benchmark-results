# Solving the Logic Puzzle

**Setting up the houses:**
- House 1 (left), House 2 (middle), House 3 (right)

**Using the clues:**

| Clue | Information |
|------|-------------|
| 1. Bob is in the third house | House 3 = Bob |
| 2. Arnold is in the second house | House 2 = Arnold |
| 4. The Dane is Alice | Alice = Dane |

Since Bob is in House 3 and Arnold is in House 2, **Alice must be in House 1**.

**Verifying consistency:**
- House 1: Alice (the Dane, owns the dog per clues 4 & 5)
- House 2: Arnold (British, owns the cat per clues 3 & 6)
- House 3: Bob (Chinese, owns the bird)

All clues check out.
