<<Rivers Rise>>

The sky turns dark above the town,
As thunder rolls and rain comes down.
The streets become a rushing sea,
Where cars and trucks are swept away.

The water climbs the muddy banks,
And floods the fields with sudden tanks.
The people watch in fear and dread,
As nature's power is not to be led.

Donnell stands against the tide,
With hope that it will soon subside.
The sun returns to break the gloom,
And leaves behind a quiet room.
