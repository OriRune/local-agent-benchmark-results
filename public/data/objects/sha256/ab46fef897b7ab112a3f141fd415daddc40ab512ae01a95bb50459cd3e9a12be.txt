We have two positions: 1 and 2.

1. “The person who is a videographer is somewhere to the left of the person who watches animation.”  
   With only two people, this means:
   - Position 1 is the videographer.
   - Position 2 watches animation.

2. “The person who is pakistani is on the immediate left or immediate right of the person who is a videographer.”  
   Since the videographer is at position 1, the pakistani person must be at position 2.

3. Since the pakistani person is at position 2 and the videographer is at position 1, the pakistani person cannot be the videographer. Therefore, the pakistani person is a freelancer.

4. Position 2 watches animation, so the pakistani person watches animation.

5. Position 1 is the videographer and does not watch animation, so position 1 watches thriller.
