<<The Rising Waters>>

The sky turns gray above the town,
Where **DONNELL**'s quiet **STREETS** are now unknown.
The **STORM** brings down the heavy rain,
As rising **WATER** fills the lane.
No longer dry, the pavement flows,
Where neighbors walk on wooden plows.
The **HOME** is threatened by the tide,
With nowhere left for the family to hide.
We pray for safety, we pray for **HOME**,
To see the sun rise once again.
