Based on the Wikipedia page for the Royal Opera House:

1. **Opening date and opera:** The current building (the third theatre on the site) opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of Meyerbeer's ***Les Huguenots***.
2. **Post-WWII reopening ballet:** The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty***.
3. **1990s reconstruction cost:** The reconstruction cost **£213 million**.
4. **2010 scenery-making workshop location:** The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making facility at **High House, Purfleet, Essex** on 6 December 2010.
