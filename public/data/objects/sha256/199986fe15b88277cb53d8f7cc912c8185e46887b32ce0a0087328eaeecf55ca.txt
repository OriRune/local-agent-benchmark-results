I've written `README.md`. It documents every command-line option of the `tinydl` CLI (defined in `app/cli.py`) with its help text:

- **`url`** — what to download (positional)
- **`-o`, `--output`** — file to write (default: stdout)
- **`-q`, `--quiet`** — no progress output
- **`-h`, `--help`** — show help and exit (argparse's built-in)

The options are presented in a table, plus a short usage line and three worked examples. I pulled the help text directly from the `add_argument` calls rather than running the program, since the sandbox can't execute code.
