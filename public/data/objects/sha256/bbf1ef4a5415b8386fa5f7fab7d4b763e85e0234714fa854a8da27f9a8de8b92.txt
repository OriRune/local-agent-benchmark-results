<<The Waters Rise Over Donnell>>

The sky turned gray and held its breath,
The river swelled beyond its bed,
The rains came down in sheets of grief,
And filled the streets where children played.

The creeks o'erflowed their muddy banks,
The roads dissolved beneath the tide,
The water climbed the porch and door,
And swallowed homes it once had known.

We stood and WATCHED the current surge,
We HELD our loved ones close and tight,
The flood had come without a warning,
A force no man could hope to fight.

But Donnell stands, though soaked and worn,
Its people strong through storm and silt,
For floods may take, and floods may break,
Yet cannot break the hearts there built.
