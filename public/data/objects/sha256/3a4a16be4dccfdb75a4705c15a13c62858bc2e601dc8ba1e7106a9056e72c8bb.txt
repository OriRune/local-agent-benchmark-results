Ladies and gentlemen, as we gather to honor the sacred duty of tax filing, I ask each of you to approach this obligation with the same seriousness and resolve that we bring to every great challenge facing our nation, for when we question the fairness of our system, when we question the integrity of our institutions, and when we question the very principles upon which this Republic was founded, we must remember that the equitable distribution of our burdens is the foundation of a just society, and that no citizen should be required to acquire more than their fair share while others are left to languish in want, so let us quadruple our efforts to simplify these quarterly filings, let us quintuple our commitment to transparency, and let us never again allow the quagmire of bureaucracy to quench the spirit of the American people.
