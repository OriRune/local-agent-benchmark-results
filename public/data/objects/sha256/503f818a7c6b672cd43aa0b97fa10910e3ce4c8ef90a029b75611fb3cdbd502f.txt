Barnaby was a man who treated his immune system like a personal fortress. He wore masks in the grocery store and carried hand sanitizer like a holy relic. But one Tuesday, he walked into the breakroom and saw the enemy.

    *Barnaby saw a coworker sneeze directly at his face, but he didn't flinch. Instead, he crossed his arms and shouted, "I am not catching anything!"*

    He turned around, but the cold was already inside his nose. Suddenly, he felt the tickle. He tried to sneeze it out, but it was too late. He tried to throw the cold back at the coworker, but he just sneezed on himself.

    *The cold was like a slippery eel; you could grab it, but it slipped right through your fingers and settled deep in your sinuses.*

    Now he was shivering. He realized the truth.

    *You can catch a cold, but you can't throw it.*
