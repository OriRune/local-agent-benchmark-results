**7 seconds total.**

From `app/net.py`, `fetch` loops `for attempt in range(MAX_RETRIES)` and on each `OSError` sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds. With `MAX_RETRIES = 3` from `app/config.py`, all three attempts fail and it sleeps:

- attempt 0: 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: 2² = 4 s

1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds**, then it raises `RuntimeError("giving up on {url}")`.
