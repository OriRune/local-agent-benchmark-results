Based on the Wikipedia page for the Royal Opera House:

1. **On what date did the current building open, and with which opera?**
   The current building (the third theatre on the site) opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of Meyerbeer's ***Les Huguenots***.

2. **Which ballet was performed when the house reopened after the Second World War?**
   The house reopened on 20 February 1946 with a performance of ***The Sleeping Beauty***.

3. **How much did the 1990s reconstruction cost?**
   The reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **In which town did the Royal Opera House open a scenery-making workshop in 2010?**
   The scenery-making facility (the Bob and Tamar Manoukian Production Workshop) opened in **Purfleet, Essex** (at High House, Purfleet).
