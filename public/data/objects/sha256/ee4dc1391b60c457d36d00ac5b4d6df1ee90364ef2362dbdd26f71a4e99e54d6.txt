<<WATERS OF DONNELL>>

The Texas heat was broken by the rain,
A heavy curtain falling on the plain.
The streets of Donnell turned to rushing streams,
Where neighbors once had walked their dreams.

The water RISES past the porch steps high,
Beneath the gray and weeping sky.
There is no DRY ground left to find,
Just mud and water left behind.

We wait for HELP to come along,
To fix the damage that the storm has wrought.
The town is changed, the town is wrong,
By nature's power, wild and strong.
