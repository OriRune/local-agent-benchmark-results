# A Newspaper for Melbourne Teenagers

Here's a suggestion for a new newspaper aimed at Melbourne teenagers:

## **The Melbourne Youth Herald**

This name pays homage to the *Herald Sun* while clearly signaling that it's a publication for young people. It's memorable, easy to say, and connects to the city's media heritage.

### Alternative names to consider:
- **The Sun Youth** – a nod to the *Herald Sun*
- **Melbourne Teen Tribune**
- **The City Youth Post**
- **Sunrise Youth** – evoking a fresh, energetic start
- **The Melbourne Voice**

### A few tips for choosing the right name:
- **Keep it short and catchy** – teenagers gravitate toward punchy, modern names
- **Make it relatable** – the name should feel like it belongs to them
- **Consider the tone** – is it serious news, or more of a fun, lifestyle-focused read?
