

**Name Suggestion:** *The Pulse*  

The *The Pulse* is a name that captures the energy and vibrancy of Melbourne's youth. It's short, memorable, and evokes a sense of movement and excitement, which aligns perfectly with the dynamic and forward-thinking nature of teenagers. The name also has a modern, tech-savvy feel, which could resonate well with a digital-first generation.  

P.S. If you're looking for a fun twist, you could consider *The Pulse* as a base and add a suffix like "-beat" or "-zone" to make it even more catchy!
