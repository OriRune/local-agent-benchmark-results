The test in `tests/test_units.py` was failing because the `KB` unit in `app/units.py` was defined as `1000` instead of `1024`, which is inconsistent with the docstring and the test's expectation that `1.5 KB` should equal `1536` bytes.

I have updated `app/units.py` to use `1024` for the `KB` unit.

```python
UNITS = {"B": 1, "KB": 1024, "MB": 1024 ** 2, "GB": 1024 ** 3}
```
