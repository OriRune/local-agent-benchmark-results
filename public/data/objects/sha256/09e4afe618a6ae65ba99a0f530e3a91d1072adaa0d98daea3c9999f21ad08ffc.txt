<<The Rising Tide of Donnell>>

The clouds gathered heavy, a charcoal hue,
Over the fields where the wild grass grew.
The rain fell FAST, a relentless beat,
Turning the dust into muddy retreat.

The creek broke its banks with a sudden roar,
Water rushing in through the open door.
The roads disappeared beneath a brown sea,
A TERRIBLE sight for all eyes to see.

Fences were swept by the current's might,
In the dim, grey haze of a stormy light.
But neighbors stood tall, hand held in hand,
Fighting to save their beloved land.

When the waters receded and the sun broke through,
The spirit of Donnell remained strong and true.
They will REBUILD from the silt and the clay,
Welcoming back a brighter new day.
