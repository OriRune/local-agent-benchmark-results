From the code:

- `MAX_RETRIES = 3` (from `app/config.py`), so the loop iterates with `attempt = 0, 1, 2`.
- After each failed attempt, it sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:
  - Attempt 0: `2^0 = 1` second
  - Attempt 1: `2^1 = 2` seconds
  - Attempt 2: `2^2 = 4` seconds

**Total sleep time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds** before it gives up and raises `RuntimeError`.
