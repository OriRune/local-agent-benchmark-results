Here are the answers from the Royal Opera House Wikipedia page:

1. **Current building opening date and opera**: The current building opened on **15 May 1858** with **Meyerbeer's Les Huguenots**.

2. **Ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War**: The house reopened on **20 February 1946** with a performance of **The Sleeping Beauty**.

3. **1990s reconstruction cost**: The reconstruction cost **£213 million**.

4. **Town for the 2010 scenery-making workshop**: The Royal Opera House opened a scenery-making workshop in **Purfleet**, Essex, in 2010.
