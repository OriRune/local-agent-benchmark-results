I'll calculate the probability of rolling 10 or higher with each die, then combine them.

**Individual probabilities:**

**1d12:** Rolls 1-12. Values 10, 11, 12 succeed.
$$P_{12} = \frac{3}{12} = \frac{1}{4}$$

**1d10:** Rolls 1-10. Only value 10 succeeds.
$$P_{10} = \frac{1}{10}$$

**1d2:** Rolls 1-2. No value reaches 10.
$$P_2 = 0$$

**Combined probability:**

The total probability of at least one die showing 10+ is:
$$P = 1 - (1 - P_{12})(1 - P_{10})(1 - P_2)$$

$$P = 1 - \left(1 - \frac{1}{4}\right)\left(1 - \frac{1}{10}\right)(1 - 0)$$

$$P = 1 - \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{9}{10} \cdot 1$$

$$P = 1 - \frac{27}{40}$$

$$P = \frac{13}{40}$$
