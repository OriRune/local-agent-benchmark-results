The retry logic is in `app/net.py`. Let me work through it.

`MAX_RETRIES = 3`, so the loop runs for `attempt = 0, 1, 2`. Each failed attempt sleeps `2 ** attempt` seconds:

- Attempt 0: sleeps `2 ** 0 = 1` second
- Attempt 1: sleeps `2 ** 1 = 2` seconds
- Attempt 2: sleeps `2 ** 2 = 4` seconds

After the third attempt fails, it raises `RuntimeError` (no sleep).

**Total sleeping time: 1 + 2 + 4 = 7 seconds.**
