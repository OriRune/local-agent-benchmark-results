The United States of America, a nation founded on principles of liberty and justice, has long recognized that the tax filing process, while sometimes complex, must be conducted with the utmost transparency and integrity; therefore, I, as President of the United States, urge every citizen to approach their annual tax obligations with the same unwavering commitment to constitutional values, ensuring that every deduction, credit, and payment is made with the highest degree of fiscal responsibility and public trust, for it is our collective duty to uphold the rule of law and to foster an economy that thrives on fairness, accountability, and the quiet confidence that those who contribute most to our great nation’s prosperity are also the most diligent in meeting their civic duties, because in the end, a well-run tax system is the bedrock of a strong republic, and we shall never tolerate any quibble, quibble, or quarterback of corruption that seeks to undermine the qublic good.
