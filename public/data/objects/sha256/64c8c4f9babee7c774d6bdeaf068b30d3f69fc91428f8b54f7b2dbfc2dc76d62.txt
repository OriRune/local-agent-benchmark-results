**1. Opening date of the current building and its first opera**  
- The present (third) theatre opened on **15 May 1858** with a performance of **Meyerbeer’s *Les Huguenots*** 【https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House】.

**2. Ballet performed when the house reopened after the Second World War**  
- The Royal Opera House reopened on **20 February 1946** with the ballet **The Sleeping Beauty** (new production designed by Oliver Messel) 【https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House】.

**3. Cost of the 1990s reconstruction**  
- The major reconstruction carried out between 1997 and 1999 cost **£213 million** 【https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House】.

**4. Town where the Royal Opera House opened a scenery‑making workshop in 2010**  
- In **2010** the Royal Opera House opened a scenery‑making facility at **High House, Purfleet, Essex** (the workshop opened on 6 December 2010) 【https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Opera_House】.
