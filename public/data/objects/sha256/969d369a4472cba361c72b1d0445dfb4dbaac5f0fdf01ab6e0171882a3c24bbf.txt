The `fetch` function in `app/net.py` retries `MAX_RETRIES` times (3, from `app/config.py`), sleeping `2 ** attempt` seconds after each failed attempt:

- attempt 0: sleep 2⁰ = 1 s
- attempt 1: sleep 2¹ = 2 s
- attempt 2: sleep 2² = 4 s

Total sleep time = 1 + 2 + 4 = **7 seconds** before it gives up (then it raises `RuntimeError`).
